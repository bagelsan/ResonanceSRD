The Core Mechanic

  • Standard Roll (SR): Roll 2d10 (Main & Crit). Result is the Main die.
  • Critical Chain: If Main = Crit, add them together, add to total, and roll again! (Even double 1s).
  • Effect Value (EV): `SR + Level + Stat`. Used against Entities (Resistance).
  • Effort Roll (ER): `SR + Level + Stat`. Used against Barriers/Puzzles (Target Number).
  • Catastrophic Miss: Rolling a 1 with Disadvantage on the final die.

Conditions

  • Mounted/Riding: Rider dictates all actions; mount is silenced.
  • Prone: Disadvantage on all actions. Move action to stand.
  • Crouching: Advantage to concealment. Movement is halved.
  • Cover: Attacks against you suffer Disadvantage. Advantage to Hide.
  • High Ground: (+10 ft) Advantage on ranged effects.
  • Submerged: Disadvantage on actions; movement halved; grants cover.
  • Hidden: Cannot be targeted. Advantage on next attack.
  • Exhausted (0 SP): Disadvantage on all rolls.
  • Silenced (0 EP): Cannot use Innate Active Keywords.
  • Injured: Max HP, SP, and EP reduced by 5 until next Downtime.
  • Unconscious (0 HP): Cannot act. Next damage is Fatal.
  • Exposed: Attacks against you gain Advantage.

Keyword EP Costs

  • Base Cost: 1 EP per Keyword applied.
  • Reduction: Subtract your Power (P) stat from total base cost (Min 0).
  • Charge Tags: Expend to reduce remaining cost further.
  • Gear Passives: Free to use when utilizing that specific item.
