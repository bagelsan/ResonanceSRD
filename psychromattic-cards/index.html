    Psychromattica: The Complete Rulebook

    1.0 Game Objective

    The goal of Psychromattica is to reduce the opposing Player's Health Points (HP) from 10 to 0. This is achieved by placing 10 or more damage markers on their Player Mat.

    2.0 Core Mechanics & Definitions

    2.1 Game Zones

    • Personal Zones: A Player's Hand, their In-Play Units, their Personal Codex deck, and a designated Active Keyword Blocks Area on the Player Mat.

      • About the Player Mat

        The Player Mat contains the Player unit card, their active Codex Cards, equipped Item Cards, and any Tags. It also has designated trackers for Energy (EN) and Karma.
    • Communal Zones: The Main Deck and the Discard Pile are shared by all Players.

    2.2 Unit

    A "Unit" is any entity in the game that can be targeted, damaged, and destroyed. This includes a Player (represented by their Player card) and any Animation cards in play.

    2.3 Keyword Structure

    Keywords are designated by a two-part number (e.g., C.D where C is Color and D is Category).

    • The first digit (C) represents the keyword's Color Code (0-9).
    • The second digit (.D) represents its Category, defining its thematic role and function:
      • .0: Flaw (Typically detrimental or impose a cost/restriction)
      • .1: Gift (Often innate, beneficial passive or active abilities)
      • .2: Talent (Learned skills or proficiencies)
      • .3: Quirk (Unusual or specific minor abilities)
      • .4: Boost (Typically apply beneficial Boost Tags or positive effects)
      • .5: Status (Typically apply detrimental or conditional Status Tags)
      • .6: Form (Relate to the shape, area, or delivery method of an effect)
      • .7: Modifier (Alter how an effect or action works)
      • .8: Drive (Represent a style, approach, or potent offensive capability)
      • .9: Unique (Often powerful, signature, or reality-altering abilities)

    2.4 Keyword Blocks

    The game features 50 unique Keyword Blocks. Each Color (0-9) has five distinct Keyword Blocks, each represented by a physical Codex Card. A Keyword Block consists of a pair of keywords from the same Color, based on their Category:

    • Block Type 1: Keywords #.1 & #.2
    • Block Type 2: Keywords #.3 & #.4
    • Block Type 3: Keywords #.5 & #.6
    • Block Type 4: Keywords #.7 & #.8
    • Block Type 5: Keywords #.9 & #.0

    When a Player unlocks a Keyword Block, they gain access to both keywords on that Codex Card for Player Keyword Augmentation.

    2.5 The Guardian Precedence

    A Player cannot be targeted by an Attack or Critical Strike action if that Player controls one or more Animations. The Animations must be dealt with first.

    2.6 Action Economy

    • Each Animation may take one (1) action per turn.
    • Each Player can take two (2) actions per turn.

    An entity that has taken an action is considered "spent." A spent Animation is turned sideways. A Player places one Player Spent Token on their mat for each action taken. A Player may end their turn at any point except in the middle of resolving an action.

    2.7 System Refresh

    At the end of each Player's turn, that Player's hand size is set to exactly 5 by either drawing or discarding.

    2.8 The Perpetual Library

    If a Player must draw or reveal a card from the Main Deck and it is empty, the communal Discard Pile is immediately shuffled to become the new Main Deck. The action is then completed.

    2.9 Damage & Destruction

    Damage is tracked with damage markers. A unit is destroyed when the number of damage markers on it is equal to or greater than its Power.

    2.10 The Suppressed State

    To show a unit is Suppressed, flip its card face down. A Suppressed unit: * Cannot perform any actions. * Has its printed and equipped keywords inactivated. * Does not count for the Guardian Precedence. * Can be targeted but takes no damage. * Can still receive tags. * Does not ready during the Ready Phase.

    To remove the Suppressed state, spend 1 Karma to perform the Re-engage action or use a Liberate Tag.

    2.11 Unit Item Limits

    • Animations: May have a maximum of one (1) Item equipped.
    • Player: May have a maximum number of Items equipped equal to their current Focus stat.

    2.12 Command Limit

    A Player's Focus stat determines the maximum number of Animations they can command. A Player can control a number of Animations up to, but not exceeding, their current Focus stat.

    2.13 Keyword Activation Principle

    • Innate Keywords: Printed directly on an Animation's card. They are automatically active when the Animation performs an action and do not count towards any limit.
    • Augmented Keywords: Gained from an external source (e.g., equipped Item, Player's Codex). The number of Augmented keywords a unit can use for a single action is limited by its own Focus stat.

    2.14 Deployment State

    An Animation that enters play is considered 'spent' for that turn and cannot act unless an effect (e.g., Haste) specifically allows it.

    2.15 First Turn Restriction

    During the first turn of the game, the starting player cannot perform the Attack or Critical Strike actions.

    2.16 Defensive Sacrifice

    During the Defender Reaction step, a player may choose to destroy one Item equipped to the defending unit. If they do, that Item's Power value is added to the defending unit's RV for this combat. The destroyed Item is placed in the Discard Pile.

    2.17 Keyword Precedence: The Golden Rules

    When interpreting card effects, these rules take precedence over all others.

    1. "Can't" Supersedes "Can" If an effect states that something cannot happen, it overrides any effect that says it can. A prohibition always wins.

    2. Specific Overrides General A keyword that defines a complete, alternative resolution process (e.g., Snap, Reckless) takes precedence over keywords that only modify a part of the standard process (e.g., Ranged).

    3. Absolute Effects & Redirection Keywords with definitive language ("cannot be prevented," "instead of," "is unaffected by") create absolute exceptions that supersede standard rules.

    4. Cumulative Effects Unless they directly contradict, multiple non-exclusive effects that trigger from the same event are cumulative. All such effects are applied.

    5. The Actor Principle (Source of an Action) The entity that becomes "spent" to perform an action is the primary actor. This entity pays all costs, is targeted by retaliatory effects, and is referenced by keywords like "the attacker," even if another unit's stats were used.

    6. The Principle of Action Sovereignty The entity performing an action is the sole determinant of which and how many augmented keywords it can use, limited by its own Focus stat. A Player cannot lend their Focus or personal keyword access to an Animation's action.

    3.0 Game Components & Setup

    3.1 Components

    • Player Mat (for tracking HP, EN, Focus, Karma)
    • The Faction Pool (20x 10-card Faction Modules)
    • The Codex Pool (50x Codex Cards)
    • Tokens (Damage, Karma, Tags)
    • Player Spent Tokens (2 per player)

    3.2 The World Weaving Phase (Main Deck Creation)

    1. The Pool: Place all 20 Faction Module decks in the center of the table.
    2. Player Draft: Players roll a d10; the highest roller is first. In turn order, each player chooses one Faction Module until the limit is reached:
      • 2 Players: Each picks 5 modules.
      • 3 Players: Each picks 3 modules (9 total).
      • 4 Players: Each picks 2 modules (8 total).
    3. The Chaos Slot(s): Shuffle the remaining Faction Modules and draw randomly to complete the deck of 10.
    4. Final Assembly: Shuffle the 10 chosen Faction Modules together. This is the shared Main Deck.

    3.3 Player Setup

    1. Each Player takes a Player Mat. Set HP to 10, EN to 10, and Focus to 1.
    2. Each Player chooses a Primary Color and takes that color's Keyword Block Type 1 Codex Card (keywords #.1 & #.2). Place this in their Active Keyword Blocks Area.
    3. Each Player chooses two Secondary Colors. They then build a 10-card Personal Codex Deck by selecting from the remaining four Keyword Blocks of their Primary Color and all five Keyword Blocks from their two Secondary Colors (a pool of 14 cards).
    4. Shuffle the Main Deck and deal 5 cards to each Player.

    4.0 Turn Structure & Actions

    4.1 Turn Phases

    1. Ready Phase: Gain EN equal to your Focus (skip on the first turn of the game). Ready all spent units. Remove Player Spent Tokens. EN cannot exceed 10.
    2. Action Phase: Your Player may take two actions. Each of your readied Animations may take one action.
    3. End Phase: Perform the System Refresh (hand size to 5). Check for any Tag triggers.

    4.2 Action Menu

    When an entity takes an action, it becomes spent.

    4.2.1-4.2.5 Universal Actions

    • Attack: Initiates an Opposed Roll, fueled by discarding cards.
    • Critical Strike: A powerful attack fueled by discarding two cards of the same Power value. The acting unit's Focus is not added to the EV.
    • Support: Remove one damage marker from a friendly unit.
    • Purge: Remove one damage marker from this unit. Optionally, then remove one Status Tag from this unit.
    • Charge: Add 1 EN to your pool.

    4.2.6-4.2.7 Player-Only Actions

    • Deploy: Pay an Animation's EN cost (equal to its Power) to play it from your hand. You can only take this action if you control fewer Animations than your Focus stat.
    • Equip: Pay an Item's EN cost (its Power) to attach it to a valid unit. Can also be used to move an Item between units.

      • Keywords from Equipped Items

        A unit gains access to the keywords on its equipped Item as Augmented Keywords. The number of these keywords it can use is limited by its own Focus stat. Flaw keywords are always active.

    4.3 Opposed Roll Resolution

    The following steps resolve in order for an Attack or Critical Strike:

    1. Attacker Action & Fuel: The acting unit becomes spent. The Player discards card(s) from hand.
    2. Calculate Initial Effect Value (EV): EV = Acting Unit's Focus + Power of Discarded Card(s).
      • A Player attacker gains +1 EV for each of their equipped Items.
      • Critical Strike does not add the attacker's Focus to the EV.
    3. Declare Augmented Keyword Use (Attacker): The attacker's controller declares which Augmented Keywords are being used, up to the acting unit's Focus limit. Innate keywords are always active.
    4. Player Keyword Augmentation: A Player performing an Attack or Support may augment it with keywords from their Active Keyword Blocks or equipped Items, up to their Player Focus limit. This is separate from an Animation's keyword use.
    5. Defender Reaction: The defending player may choose one option:
      • Discard one card from hand to add its Power to the RV.
      • Perform a Defensive Sacrifice to add the destroyed Item's Power to the RV.
    6. Calculate Resistance Value (RV): RV = Defender's Focus + Power from defender's chosen option (if any).
    7. Resolution: If EV > RV, the defender receives damage equal to EV - RV. Then, apply all active keyword effects.

    Example A: A Player Attacks

    The Situation: * Your Player has Focus 2. * Your active Keyword Blocks include Brutal (1.8) and Splash (8.6).

    The Action: Your Player performs a Critical Strike.

    Resolution: 1. You discard two cards of the same power to fuel the Critical Strike. 2. You calculate the initial EV. 3. Player Keyword Augmentation: Because your Player is performing the action, you can augment it. Your Focus is 2, so you may choose up to two keywords. You choose Brutal and Splash.

    Result: The Critical Strike resolves with the effects of both Brutal and Splash applied.

    Example B: An Animation Attacks

    The Situation: * Your Player has Focus 2 and has the Splash (8.6) keyword active in their Codex. * Your Animation "Hunter" has Focus 1 and an Innate keyword of Martial (1.2). * Hunter is equipped with an Item that grants Piercing (4.6) and Ranged (7.7).

    The Action: Your Animation "Hunter" performs an Attack.

    Resolution: 1. Innate Keywords: Hunter's Martial keyword is automatically active. 2. Declare Augmented Keyword Use: You check Hunter's equipped Item. Hunter's own Focus is 1, so you may choose only one keyword from its Item. You choose Piercing. 3. Player Keyword Augmentation: This step is skipped. The rule only applies when the Player themself performs the action. You cannot use your Player's Focus to add Splash from your Codex to the Animation's attack.

    Result: Hunter's Attack resolves with Martial (innate) and Piercing (from its item) applied.

    4.4 Tag Application from Keywords

    • Boost Tags (from keywords ending in #.4):
      • On Attack or Critical Strike: Apply to the attacker.
      • On Support: Apply to either the target or the unit performing the action.
    • Status Tags (from keywords ending in #.5):
      • Typically applied to the target of the effect or action.

    5.0 The Karma System

    5.1 Gaining Karma (Gain 1)

    • When one of your Attack or Critical Strike actions successfully deals 1 or more damage.
    • When you perform a Critical Strike.
    • When you destroy an enemy Animation or the enemy Player.

    5.2 Spending Karma

    Spending Karma does not use a Player action but can only be done during your Action Phase.

    • Cost 1 Karma (System Tweak)
      • Overclock: Draw one card.
      • Re-engage: Ready one unit or remove its Suppressed state.
    • Cost 3 Karma (The Tactical Path)
      • Acquire Codex: Draw one card from your Personal Codex Deck and activate it.
    • The Strategic Path: Refine Focus
      • Effect: Permanently increase your Player's Focus stat by 1.
      • Cost: The Karma cost is equal to your current Focus stat + 1.
        • Focus 1 -> 2 costs 2 Karma.
        • Focus 2 -> 3 costs 3 Karma.
        • Focus 3 -> 4 costs 4 Karma, etc.

    Appendices

    Appendix A: Card Anatomy & Design

    Animation Card

    1. Power (PWR): A value of 1-5 in the top left corner. Represents EN Cost, HP, and discard value.
    2. Focus (FCS): A value of 1-5 in the center. Represents combat skill and capacity for Augmented Keywords.
    3. Keywords: A maximum of 3 keywords in the text box.

    Item Card

    1. Power (PWR) & EN Cost: A single value (1-3) in the top left corner that serves as both EN Cost to play and Power for discards or Defensive Sacrifice.
    2. Keywords: A maximum of 3 total keywords. The number of non-Flaw keywords cannot exceed the Item's EN Cost.

    Codex Card

    A reference card listing the two keywords from its block with their descriptions.

    Player Card

    A visual representation of the Player, performing no direct game function.

    Appendix D: Tag Definitions

    Tag Classifications

    • Boost Tags: Applied by keywords ending in #.4.
    • Status Tags: Applied by keywords ending in #.5.

    Defined Boost Tags

    Liberate (0.4), Augment (1.4), Haste (2.4), Ward (3.4), Bless (4.4), Charge (5.4), Cure (6.4), Regen (7.4), Enhance (8.4), Obscure (9.4).

    Defined Status Tags

    Mark (0.5), Impair (1.5), Slow (2.5), Daze (3.5), Curse (4.5), Bind (5.5), Weaken (6.5), Blight (7.5), Silence (8.5), Nullify (9.5).

    Individual Tag Definitions

    1. Liberate Tag: Remove this Tag from a Suppressed unit to ready it (not an action).
    2. Mark Tag: Attacks targeting this unit gain +1 EV.
    3. Augment Tag: Remove this Tag when the unit attacks to gain a keyword effect from one of its controller’s active Codex Cards for the attack.
    4. Impair Tag: This unit has -1 Focus (min 0).
    5. Haste Tag: Remove this Tag from a spent unit to ready it (not an action).
    6. Slow Tag: This unit does not ready during the Ready Phase. Its controller may spend 1 EN during the Ready Phase to remove this Tag.
    7. Ward Tag: The first time this unit would be dealt damage, prevent that damage and remove this Tag.
    8. Daze Tag: This unit cannot contribute Power from discarded cards when attacking or defending.
    9. Bless Tag: When this unit would be dealt damage, remove this Tag to prevent 1 of that damage.
    10. Curse Tag: Damage markers cannot be removed from this unit.
    11. Charge Tag: When an Animation with this Tag takes the Charge action, gain an additional 1 EN, then remove this Tag.
    12. Bind Tag: This unit cannot perform Attack or Critical Strike actions.
    13. Cure Tag: On gain, remove one Status Tag from this unit, then remove this Tag. If it has no Status Tags, this Tag remains and prevents the next one that would be applied.
    14. Weaken Tag: This unit's Attack or Critical Strike actions have -1 EV.
    15. Regen Tag: At the start of its controller's Ready Phase, this unit removes 1 damage marker from itself.
    16. Blight Tag: Whenever this unit performs any action, place 1 damage marker on it after the action resolves.
    17. Enhance Tag: In an Opposed Roll, this unit's controller may discard an additional card to add its Power to the EV or RV.
    18. Silence Tag: This unit's printed keywords are inactive.
    19. Obscure Tag: When an opponent attacks this unit, they must discard two cards and use the lower Power value for the EV.
    20. Nullify Tag: On gain, remove one Boost Tag from this unit, then remove this Tag. If it has no Boost Tags, this Tag remains and prevents the next one that would be applied.

    Appendix E: Turn & Action Flow

    I. Game Start & Setup

    1. Determine Starting Player (highest d10 roll).
    2. Perform World Weaving, Player Mat setup, Codex selection, and deal hands.

    II. Your Turn Structure

    A. Ready Phase 1. Gain EN: Gain EN equal to your Player's Focus (skip on game's first turn). 2. Ready Units: Turn all spent Animations upright. 3. Remove Tokens: Remove all Player Spent Tokens from your Mat.

    B. Action Phase * Player Actions: Take up to two (2) actions. * Animation Actions: Each readied Animation may take one (1) action. * Spend Karma: Use Karma abilities at any time during this phase (not an action). * End Turn: You may end your turn at any time (not mid-action).

    C. End Phase 1. System Refresh: Adjust hand size to exactly 5 (draw or discard). 2. Tag Triggers: Resolve any end-of-turn Tag effects.

    III. Opposed Roll Resolution (Attack / Critical Strike)

    1. Attacker Action & Fuel: Attacker becomes spent. Discard card(s).
    2. Calculate Initial EV.
    3. Declare Augmented Keywords (if an Animation is attacking).
    4. Player Keyword Augmentation (if the Player is attacking).
    5. Defender Reaction: Defender chooses to discard a card, perform a Defensive Sacrifice, or do nothing.
    6. Calculate RV.
    7. Resolution: Compare EV to RV, deal damage if EV > RV, and apply all keyword effects.

    Appendix F: Quick Start Guide

    Game Objective

    Be the first to reduce your opponent's HP from 10 to 0.

    Your Player Mat

    • HP: Starts at 10.
    • EN: Starts at 10. Resource for playing cards.
    • Focus: Starts at 1. Your skill, command limit, and item limit.
    • Karma: Starts at 0. Special resource for powerful effects.

    Key Concepts

    • Units: Your Player and your Animations.
    • Animations: Your creatures. Their Power (P) is their HP, EN cost, and discard value. Their Focus (F) limits their use of Item keywords.
    • Items: Equipment that grants keywords.
    • Guardian Precedence: You must defeat a Player's Animations before you can attack them directly.

    Your Turn Simplified

    1. Ready Phase: Gain EN equal to your Focus, ready all your units.
    2. Action Phase: Your Player takes 2 actions; each Animation takes 1.
    3. End Phase: Adjust your hand to 5 cards.

    Core Actions

    • Deploy (Player-Only): Pay an Animation's Power in EN to play it. It enters spent. You can't have more Animations than your Focus stat.
    • Attack (Universal):
      1. Declare attacker and target.
      2. Discard a card to fuel the attack.
      3. Calculate EV (Focus + Power of Card).
      4. Defender reacts (discards a card or sacrifices an item).
      5. Calculate RV (Focus + Power of Defender's Card/Item).
      6. If EV > RV, deal damage equal to the difference.

    Spending Karma

    • 1 Karma: Draw a card (Overclock) or ready a unit (Re-engage).
    • 3 Karma: Get a new Keyword Block from your Codex (Acquire Codex).
    • Current Focus + 1 Karma: Permanently increase your Focus (Refine Focus). This is a key long-term strategy!
