    Psycromattica: Matrix Dice

    1.0 OBJECTIVE

    The last player with Health Points (HP) remaining is the winner.

    2.0 GAME FORMATS

    • Duel: A two-player, head-to-head match.
    • Arena: A 3-4 player free-for-all.

    3.0 COMPONENTS

    • 1x Agent Board per player.
    • 10x ten-sided dice (d10s).
    • Keyword Tokens & Tag Tokens.

    4.0 SETUP

    1. Each player takes an Agent Board.
    2. Set starting values: HP = 10, Focus = 1, Karma = 0.
    3. Archetype Declaration: Each player chooses a Primary Color and two Secondary Colors.
    4. Determine the first player via initiative roll. Play proceeds clockwise.

    5.0 CORE CONCEPTS

    • 5.1 Affinity Matrix: A 3x3 grid for Keyword Tokens. Max capacity of 5 tokens per slot. If a slot is full, you must discard an existing token from that slot to place a new one.
    • 5.2 Focus: Max number of Keyword Tokens you can spend on a single Activation. Max Focus is 5.
    • 5.3 Karma: Primary resource for generating tokens. Max Karma is 10.
    • 5.4 Successful Activation: An activation targeting an opponent is "successful" if it is not Avoided and causes one of the following: target loses HP, a Status Tag is applied, a token is discarded/altered, or the target loses Karma. Self-targeted activations are always successful.
    • 5.5 Damage vs. Life Loss: "Damage" is HP loss from an activation's resolution. "Life Loss" is HP loss from costs or non-activation effects.

    6.0 THE CORE LOOP: TURN STRUCTURE

    1. Ready (Upkeep Phase): Resolve start-of-turn effects. Gain Karma equal to Focus. Perform one free Primary Color token generation. Spend Karma for additional token generations.
    2. Activate (Action Phase): Perform one primary action from the list in section 7.1.
    3. Respond (Reaction Phase): Targeted player(s) choose to Resist, Avoid, or Do Not React.
    4. Resolve & Reward (Resolution Phase): Effects are finalized. If successful against an opponent, gain 1 Karma (plus combo bonuses).

    7.0 ACTIONS & REACTIONS

    • 7.1 Activate Actions:
    • Basic Activation: Spend no tokens. Deals 1 damage. Can be enhanced with a Karma Surge (2 Karma for +1 damage).
    • Keyword Activation: Spend tokens up to your Focus value to add their effects.
    • Self-Targeted Activation: Targets yourself. Automatically successful, no damage, no Karma reward.
    • Focus Break: Spend 5 of your Affinity Color tokens to permanently increase your Focus by 1.
    • Resonance Lock-In: Spend 2-3 identical tokens to gain that Keyword's permanent Passive Effect.
    • 7.2 Respond Actions (Reactions):
    • Resist: Spend any Keyword Token to prevent 1 damage. Spend 2 Karma to prevent 1 damage from a Basic Activation.
    • Avoid: Spend tokens from your Matrix to exactly match the color of each token the attacker spent. Negates the entire activation. Your Focus does not limit this expenditure. Tokens "carried" by a .0 Flaw are not considered 'spent' and do not need to be matched.

    8.0 ADVANCED MECHANICS

    • 8.1 Combos: Special activations that can exceed your Focus limit (Chroma Burst, Resonance Cascade, Critical Activation).
    • 8.2 Tags (Boosts & Statuses): Boosts (self-targeted) are guaranteed. Statuses (opponent-targeted) are conditional on a successful activation. Multiple instances of the same tag stack and resolve independently.
    • 8.3 Entity Rules: An entity is a temporary body on the board. They are considered "players" and are legal targets. They are removed when their HP reaches 0. Unless an effect states otherwise, their unspent tokens are discarded on defeat.
    • Manifested Guardian: A passive shield. Opponents must target it instead of your Agent (unless an effect ignores it).
    • Split Entity: A second, independent body. As an explicit exception to the core rules, the Split keyword allows you to perform one Activation with your Agent and one with your Split Entity during your Activate phase.
    • Token Entity: A temporary avatar. You, the player, act as the entity, using its stats. Your Agent cannot be targeted while you are the entity.

    9.0 GLOBAL RULES & HIERARCHY

    • 9.1 Keyword Application: An activation cannot include more than one instance of the same named keyword. A .0 Flaw cannot "carry" another .0 Flaw.
    • 9.2 Effect Hierarchy: "Cannot" trumps "Can." Copied effects do not affect the original. "Unstoppable" effects bypass Respond actions but not passive immunities.
    • 9.3 Targeting Ethics: Any activation can target any legal player or entity.

    10.0 KEYWORD CATEGORY TRAITS

    • .0 Flaw: Suffer a drawback to use another token's effect for free.
    • .1 Gift: Utility effects; can be spent for 1 Karma in the Ready phase.
    • .2 Talent: Permanent upgrades; easier Resonance Lock-In cost (2 tokens).
    • .3 Quirk: Free "rider" effects; no Focus cost (limit 1 per activation).
    • .4 Boost: Applies a beneficial, guaranteed Boost Tag to yourself.
    • .5 Status: Applies a detrimental Status Tag to an opponent on a successful activation.
    • .6 Form: Alters how an activation targets opponents.
    • .7 Modifier: Bends the core rules of an action or the game state.
    • .8 Drive: Directly increases numerical efficiency and interacts with damage.
    • .9 Unique: Game-altering "ultimate" abilities.

    11.0 COMPLETE KEYWORD CODEX (v1.0)

    [0] NULL COLOR - UNIVERSAL TOOLS

    • [0.0] Restricted (.Flaw): Active: Choose a "carried" Keyword Token from one of your three Affinity Color slots; its Active Effect is applied, but the token is not spent. | Passive: Your max Karma is reduced to 5. Your free Keyword Token roll during the Ready phase is modified: you may choose to roll for any of your three Affinity Colors, instead of being restricted to only your Primary Color.
    • [0.1] Aura (.Gift): Active: This activation deals +1 damage for each other Keyword Token spent from your Primary Affinity Color. | Passive: Any activation you perform that includes at least one Keyword Token of your Primary Affinity Color deals +1 damage.
    • [0.2] Specialist (.Talent): Active: Deals +1 damage for each Keyword Token spent that shares a category with another token in this activation. | Passive: The first activation each turn that includes 2+ Keyword Tokens from the same category deals +1 damage.
    • [0.3] Alert (.Quirk): Active: The target must pay 1 Karma as a cost to perform a Resist or Avoid action. | Passive: The first Keyword Token you spend during your Respond phase each turn is returned to your Matrix.
    • [0.4] Liberate (.Boost): Active: Apply the Liberate Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Liberate tag instead. | Liberate Tag: Expend to prevent a Keyword Token from being discarded by an opponent's effect.
    • [0.5] Mark (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Mark Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Mark tag instead. | Mark Tag: The next time this player takes damage from any source, they take +1 damage. Remove this tag.
    • [0.6] Indirect (.Form): Active: The target cannot Resist or Avoid unless they first discard a Keyword Token that matches your Primary Color. | Passive: You take 1 less damage from activations with a .6 Form keyword.
    • [0.7] Adaptive (.Modifier): Active: During resolution, you may have this activation's damage become either: Target loses 2 Karma, or Target discards one Keyword Token. | Passive: Once per turn, you may spend a Keyword Token of any color as if it were one of your Affinity Colors.
    • [0.8] Gamble (.Drive): Active: Roll two d10s (Color, Category). This activation gains the Active Effect of the resulting keyword. | Passive: Once per turn, you may reroll any single d10 you have rolled. You must accept the new result.
    • [0.9] Edit (.Unique): Active: Swap a number of Keyword Tokens on your Matrix with an equal number on the target's Matrix, up to your Focus. This cannot be Resisted or Avoided. | Passive: Once per turn, when rolling the d10 for your Primary Color, you may choose the result instead.

    [1] SILVER COLOR - TACTICAL COMBAT

    • [1.0] Bane (.Flaw): Active: You suffer 1 Life Loss. Carry another token. | Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage to targets with higher HP than you.
    • [1.1] Traveller (.Gift): Active: Choose another token in this activation; it is considered one of your Affinity Colors for combo requirements. | Passive: Your first non-Affinity die roll each turn costs 1 Karma instead of 2.
    • [1.2] Martial (.Talent): Active: If the target's Resist reduces damage to 0, the activation is unsuccessful, but the target suffers 1 Life Loss anyway. | Passive: Your Karma Surges cost 1 Karma for +1 damage.
    • [1.3] Attuned (.Quirk): Active: If successful and includes a Primary Color token, gain 1 bonus Karma. | Passive: Once per turn, when an opponent activates a keyword matching one of your Affinity Colors, you may gain 1 Karma.
    • [1.4] Augment (.Boost): Active: Apply the Augment Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Augment tag instead. | Augment Tag: Expend when rolling a d10 for generation to roll two dice and choose one result.
    • [1.5] Impair (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Impair Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be an Impair tag instead. | Impair Tag: The next time this player spends Karma, the cost is increased by 1. Remove this tag.
    • [1.6] Reactive (.Form): Active: If this activation is Avoided or fully Resisted, you may return one of the other spent tokens to your Matrix. | Passive: When you successfully Avoid or fully Resist an activation, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [1.7] Phasing (.Modifier): Active: The target cannot perform a Resist action. They may still Avoid. | Passive: Opponents must spend one additional Keyword Token as part of the cost of any Resist action against you.
    • [1.8] Brutal (.Drive): Active: Deals +1 damage. The target cannot gain Karma or tokens from keyword effects during this activation's resolution. | Passive: When you deal 3+ damage with a single activation, the target loses 1 Karma.
    • [1.9] Translocate (.Unique): Active: Swap one of your other spent tokens with a token on the target's Matrix; the stolen token's effect is added to this activation. | Passive: At the end of your turn, you may swap one token on your Matrix with a token on an opponent's Matrix.

    [2] YELLOW COLOR - HIGH-TEMPO ACTION

    • [2.0] Anxious (.Flaw): Active: Discard a token from your Matrix. Roll a d10; this activation gains the effect of the keyword of the discarded token's Color and the rolled Category. | Passive: During your Ready phase, you may discard a token to reroll its category.
    • [2.1] Acrobatic (.Gift): Active: If this activation is Avoided, choose a token on your Matrix. The target must discard a token sharing its Color or Category, or the Avoid fails. | Passive: The first time your activation is Avoided each turn, you may reroll the category of one token on your Matrix.
    • [2.2] Sentry (.Talent): Active: If this activation is Avoided, the target suffers 1 Life Loss. This does not make the activation successful. | Passive: When you successfully Avoid or fully Resist an activation, the active player suffers 1 Life Loss.
    • [2.3] Eidetic (.Quirk): Active: If successful, generate a copy of a token on the target's Matrix. | Passive: Once per turn, when generating a token, you may pay 1 extra Karma to generate a second, identical token.
    • [2.4] Haste (.Boost): Active: Apply the Haste Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Haste tag instead. | Haste Tag: Expend at the start of your Ready phase to perform a second, additional Ready phase this turn.
    • [2.5] Slow (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Slow Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Slow tag instead. | Slow Tag: During your next Ready phase, your Focus is treated as 1 lower. Remove this tag at the end of that phase.
    • [2.6] Snap (.Form): Active: The target cannot perform any Respond actions. This activation's damage is 1 and can only include one .5 Status. | Passive: You are immune to the active effect of Snap. When targeted by an activation that includes Snap, you may perform Respond actions normally.
    • [2.7] Condense (.Modifier): Active: Deals bonus damage equal to (your Focus - tokens spent). | Passive: Focus Break costs 4 Affinity tokens instead of 5.
    • [2.8] Rapid (.Drive): Active: This activation's damage is dealt as a sequence of 1-damage instances. Only the first successful instance can apply tags. | Passive: Damage from your Basic Activations and Karma Surges is dealt as a sequence of 1-damage instances.
    • [2.9] Reckless (.Unique): Active: You may suffer any amount of Life Loss; this activation deals +1 damage for each HP lost this way. | Passive: You can perform a Karma Surge on any Keyword Activation.

    [3] GREEN COLOR - DEFENSE & CONTROL

    • [3.0] Hesitant (.Flaw): Active: This activation can only target yourself. Carry another token. | Passive: You cannot Avoid Basic Activations. You permanently reduce all damage from Keyword Activations by 1.
    • [3.1] Sturdy (.Gift): Active: For this activation's duration, the opponent cannot use effects that would cause you to discard tokens or lose Karma. | Passive: Your Keyword Tokens cannot be discarded by an opponent's keyword effects.
    • [3.2] Brawler (.Talent): Active: If successful, you may discard a token from your Matrix to discard a token from the target's Matrix. | Passive: If an opponent's effect would force you to discard a token, you may choose a token on their Matrix. They must discard that token or the activation fails.
    • [3.3] Stubborn (.Quirk): Active: You may ignore the effects of all Status Tags on your board until the end of the turn. | Passive: Once per turn, when one of your Status Tags would trigger, you may choose to have it not trigger. If you do, remove that tag.
    • [3.4] Ward (.Boost): Active: Apply the Ward Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Ward tag instead. | Ward Tag: Expend during your Respond phase to perform a special Resist that prevents all damage.
    • [3.5] Daze (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Daze Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Daze tag instead. | Daze Tag: You cannot spend more than one Keyword Token on any single activation. Remove this tag at the end of your next turn.
    • [3.6] Sculpt (.Form): Active: Choose another .6 Form on your Matrix. Its Active Effect is applied to this activation, but the chosen token is not spent. | Passive: Your Focus limit is increased by +1 for activations that include a .6 Form keyword.
    • [3.7] Defensive (.Modifier): Active: You are immune to any damage that would be dealt to you as a result of the target's keyword effects during this activation's resolution. | Passive: Your Resist actions are +1 more effective.
    • [3.8] Impact (.Drive): Active: If this activation is successful, you may choose one Keyword Token on the target's Matrix and discard it. | Passive: When you perform a successful activation, you may spend 1 Karma to discard one Keyword Token from the target's Matrix.
    • [3.9] Shift (.Unique): Active: Choose one of your active Passives. Add the Active Effect of that keyword to your current activation. | Passive: You may perform a Resonance Lock-In by spending one token of the correct color from three different categories, instead of three identical tokens.

    [4] BLACK COLOR - RESILIENCE & ATTRITION

    • [4.0] Feeble (.Flaw): Active: This activation's damage is reduced by 1. Carry another token. | Passive: When you take 3+ damage from a single activation, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [4.1] Survivor (.Gift): Active: If your HP is 5 or less, you gain 2 Karma. | Passive: If an effect would reduce your HP to 0, you may spend 1 Karma to set your HP to 1 instead.
    • [4.2] Stoic (.Talent): Active: If an opponent's Respond phase effect would cause you to discard a token, gain 1 Karma. | Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect forces you to discard a token, you gain 1 Karma. The token is still discarded.
    • [4.3] Ageless (.Quirk): Active: If this activation includes another keyword with a Karma cost, reduce that cost by 1. | Passive: The first time you would pay a Karma cost from a keyword's effect each turn, reduce that cost by 1.
    • [4.4] Bless (.Boost): Active: Apply the Bless Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Bless tag instead. | Bless Tag: Expend when you would gain Karma to gain 1 additional Karma.
    • [4.5] Curse (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Curse Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Curse tag instead. | Curse Tag: The next time this player would gain Karma, the gain is reduced by 1. Remove this tag.
    • [4.6] Piercing (.Form): Active: Cannot be Resisted and bypasses Ward. This activation's damage is 1. | Passive: Opponents must pay 1 Karma to be able to perform a Resist action against you.
    • [4.7] Flux (.Modifier): Active: You may spend Karma up to your Focus; deal +1 damage for each Karma spent. | Passive: When you perform a successful activation, you may spend 1 Karma to deal +1 damage.
    • [4.8] Siphon (.Drive): Active: If successful, gain Karma equal to damage dealt, up to your Focus. | Passive: When you deal damage with a Basic Activation, gain 1 Karma.
    • [4.9] Animate (.Unique): Active: Create a Token Entity. | Passive: When you create a Token Entity, its Focus is increased by 1.

    [5] ORANGE COLOR - TECHNOLOGY & RESOURCES

    • [5.0] Gremlins (.Flaw): Active: The target gains 1 Karma. Carry another token. | Passive: When an opponent performs a Focus Break or Resonance Lock-In, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [5.1] Sensor (.Gift): Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, this activation deals +1 damage. | Passive: When an opponent Resists or Avoids, you may spend 1 Karma to have the activation deal +1 damage.
    • [5.2] Improvisor (.Talent): Active: Choose another token in this activation; for combo requirements, it can be treated as any Color or Category. | Passive: Your Orange keywords are considered to be of any color for activation requirements.
    • [5.3] Crafty (.Quirk): Active: You may choose one non-Affinity token on your Matrix and reroll its category. | Passive: The first time you generate a non-Affinity token each turn, you may reroll it. Must keep the new result.
    • [5.4] Charge (.Boost): Active: Apply the Charge Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Charge tag instead. | Charge Tag: Expend when you would spend Karma to pay 1 Karma of that cost.
    • [5.5] Bind (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Bind Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Bind tag instead. | Bind Tag: The next time this player is targeted by an activation with fewer tokens than their Focus, they must Do Not React. Remove this tag.
    • [5.6] Imbue (.Form): Active: If successful, you may choose a Tag on your board and apply a copy of it to the target. | Passive: When you successfully apply a Tag, you may roll a d10 of a non-Affinity color and generate the token.
    • [5.7] Material (.Modifier): Active: You may discard tokens from your Matrix up to your Focus; gain 1 Karma for each. This deals no damage. | Passive: At the end of your turn, you may discard one token to gain 1 Karma.
    • [5.8] Leverage (.Drive): Active: If you have one or more Boost Tags, this activation deals +1 damage. | Passive: The first time you would expend a Boost Tag each turn, you gain its effect, but the tag is not removed.
    • [5.9] Manifest (.Unique): Active: Create a Manifested Guardian. | Passive: When you create an entity, its starting HP is increased by 1.

    [6] WHITE COLOR - HEALING & PURITY

    • [6.0] Vulnerable (.Flaw): Active: This activation is +1 easier for the target to Resist. Carry another token. | Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage. Activations targeting you deal +1 damage.
    • [6.1] Tolerant (.Gift): Active: Ignore effects of Status Tags on the opponent for this activation's duration. | Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's keyword effect of your Primary Color would affect you, it is negated.
    • [6.2] Deadeye (.Talent): Active: Ignores damage reduction from opponent's Passive abilities. | Passive: An opponent cannot spend more Keyword Tokens on a Resist action than your current Focus.
    • [6.3] Immunized (.Quirk): Active: You may remove any Status Tags from your board that match your Primary Color. | Passive: You are immune to all Status Tags that match your Primary Color.
    • [6.4] Cure (.Boost): Active: Apply the Cure Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Cure tag instead. | Cure Tag: Expend to either (1) remove a Status from yourself, or (2) prevent a Status from being applied.
    • [6.5] Weaken (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Weaken Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Weaken tag instead. | Weaken Tag: The next time a Status Tag would be applied to you, gain a second copy of it. Remove this tag.
    • [6.6] Sticky (.Form): Active: If successful, at the start of the target's next Ready phase, they suffer 1 Life Loss. | Passive: When a Status Tag you applied would be removed by its own effect, the opponent must pay 1 Karma or it is not removed.
    • [6.7] Restore (.Modifier): Active: Can only target yourself or an ally. Instead of dealing damage, the target gains HP equal to the damage value. | Passive: When you resolve a Self-Targeted Activation, you gain an additional 1 HP.
    • [6.8] Exploit (.Drive): Active: You may remove Status Tags from the target, up to your Focus; deal +1 damage for each tag removed. | Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage to targets with one or more Status Tags.
    • [6.9] Growth (.Unique): Active: Deals bonus damage equal to your current HP, up to a max bonus equal to your Focus. | Passive: If your HP is higher than 5, your activations deal +1 damage.

    [7] RED COLOR - AGGRESSION & ENERGY

    • [7.0] Limited (.Flaw): Active: For this activation, any effect that uses your Focus as a metric treats it as 1. Carry another token. | Passive: At the end of your turn, if you have 3 or fewer tokens, you may generate a Primary Color token for free.
    • [7.1] Resilient (.Gift): Active: Choose another token in this activation; if an opponent's effect would negate it, it resolves anyway. | Passive: The first time each turn your keyword effect is negated, gain 1 Karma.
    • [7.2] Font (.Talent): Active: For this activation, you may pay Karma costs with HP, or HP costs with Karma. | Passive: Once per turn during your Ready phase, you may suffer 1 Life Loss to roll an additional d10 for free.
    • [7.3] Insulated (.Quirk): Active: If an opponent's Respond phase effect would cause you to lose Karma, gain that much Karma instead. | Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect would cause you to lose Karma, gain 1 Karma instead.
    • [7.4] Regen (.Boost): Active: Apply the Regen Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Regen tag instead. | Regen Tag: At the end of your turn, expend this tag to either gain 1 HP or gain 1 Karma.
    • [7.5] Blight (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Blight Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Blight tag instead. | Blight Tag: The next time this player spends a Keyword Token, they suffer 1 Life Loss. Remove this tag. Only one Blight can trigger per action.
    • [7.6] Area (.Form): Active: Targets all other opponents. If an opponent controls an entity, create an additional instance targeting it. | Passive: When you deal damage to an entity, its controller suffers 1 Life Loss.
    • [7.7] Ranged (.Modifier): Active: This activation can target a player directly, ignoring Manifested Guardian. Token Entities can target Agents directly. | Passive: When you are a Token Entity, you may use your Agent's Focus instead of the entity's.
    • [7.8] Multiply (.Drive): Active: This activation gains additional instances, targeting different opponents, up to your Focus. | Passive: When you apply a Boost Tag to yourself, you may spend 1 Karma to apply a copy to another player.
    • [7.9] Split (.Unique): Active: Create a Split Entity. | Passive: When your entity is removed, you may reclaim any of its unspent tokens.

    [8] BLUE COLOR - INFLUENCE & CONTROL

    • [8.0] Awkward (.Flaw): Active: The target chooses the "carried" token from your Matrix. | Passive: During your Ready phase, you gain a free d10 roll, but an opponent chooses its Color.
    • [8.1] Charismatic (.Gift): Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, you gain 1 Karma. | Passive: When you successfully apply a Status Tag, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [8.2] Leader (.Talent): Active: When activating as your Agent or an entity, you may spend tokens from either your Matrix or the entity's micro-Matrix. | Passive: At the end of your turn, you may swap one token between your Matrix and an entity's.
    • [8.3] Insightful (.Quirk): Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, you may generate a copy of one token they spent. | Passive: The first time each turn an opponent Resists or Avoids, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [8.4] Enhance (.Boost): Active: Apply the Enhance Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Enhance tag instead. | Enhance Tag: Expend when declaring an activation to choose one spent token and increase its numerical effects by 1.
    • [8.5] Silence (.Status): Active: If successful, apply the Silence Status Tag. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Silence tag instead. | Silence Tag: Your next Activate action must be a Basic Activation. Remove this tag at the end of your next turn.
    • [8.6] Splash (.Form): Active: If successful against a primary target, create a secondary instance against all other opponents that deals 0 damage but applies all .5 Status keywords. | Passive: When a Status Tag would be removed from an opponent, you may pay 1 Karma to apply a copy to a different opponent.
    • [8.7] Lingering (.Modifier): Active: Choose a .5 Status in this activation. If successful, apply a second, identical copy of that tag. | Passive: At the end of your turn, you may choose a Status Tag on an opponent and apply a second, identical copy.
    • [8.8] Spread (.Drive): Active: If successful and the target has a Status Tag, you may apply a copy of that tag to a different opponent. | Passive: When a Status Tag is applied to an opponent, you may spend 1 Karma to apply a copy to a different opponent.
    • [8.9] Potent (.Unique): Active: This activation applies its .5 Status Tags even if unsuccessful. It can still be Avoided. | Passive: When declaring an activation, you may spend 1 Karma and a .5 Status token from your Matrix to add its effect. This does not use Focus.

    [9] PURPLE COLOR - TRICKERY & META-GAMING

    • [9.0] Mundane (.Flaw): Active: This activation cannot be a Combo Activation. Carry another token. | Passive: During your Ready phase, your Focus is treated as 1 lower for Karma gain. In exchange, the first time you spend a Keyword Token each turn, gain 1 Karma.
    • [9.1] Aware (.Gift): Active: The target must suffer 1 Life Loss as a cost to perform an Avoid action. | Passive: When an opponent successfully Avoids, you may generate a copy of one token they spent.
    • [9.2] Stealthy (.Talent): Active: The target must suffer 1 Life Loss as a cost to perform a Resist action. | Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect would discard your token, you may pay 1 Karma to return it to your Matrix.
    • [9.3] Trickster (.Quirk): Active: After the target Responds, you may choose one other token in this activation to not resolve its effect. If you do, return it to your Matrix. | Passive: Choose a Color. Your tokens of that color can be spent as if they were Purple for activation requirements.
    • [9.4] Obscure (.Boost): Active: Apply the Obscure Boost Tag. | Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Obscure tag instead. | Obscure Tag: Expend during your Respond phase; it is treated as a Keyword Token of any color and category for paying Resist or Avoid costs.
    • [9.5] Nullify (.Status): Active: If this activation is successful, you may choose one of the target's active Passive Effects and permanently remove it. | Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Nullify effect instead.
    • [9.6] Chain (.Form): Active: If successful against a primary target, you may choose additional opponents up to your Focus. They do not get the original effects, but each suffers 1 Life Loss. | Passive: When you deal damage to 2+ different opponents during your Activate phase, gain 1 bonus Karma.
    • [9.7] Natural (.Modifier): Active: The other Keyword Tokens in this activation cannot be affected by an opponent's keyword effects. | Passive: Your Passive Effects cannot be targeted, removed, or negated.
    • [9.8] Ambience (.Drive): Active: Choose a token on any Matrix. Reroll it: discard it, then roll a d10. Generate a new token of the same category but with the new color. | Passive: When a keyword effect lets you roll for a token outside your Ready phase, you may choose the Color of the roll.
    • [9.9] Channel (.Unique): Active: If successful, you may spend 3 additional tokens. If you do, choose one of the target's active Passives and permanently gain a copy of it. | Passive: When you perform a Resonance Lock-In or use Shift, you may generate a Primary Color token for free.
