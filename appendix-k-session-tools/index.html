    A Guide to Your Character Sheet

    Welcome to Psychromattica. Your character sheet is more than just a record of numbers; it is your tactical dashboard. It is the interface you will use to interact with the story, the world, and the dice.

    This guide will walk you through the six zones of your dashboard, helping you translate your character concept into a playable reality.

    Zone 1: The Profile (Top Left)

    "The Narrative Drive"

    In this system, who you are determines what you can do. Before you select a single stat, you must define your character’s internal engine.

    • The Trinity of Ideals: Define your character's core motivations.

      • Goal: What is the ultimate outcome they strive for? (e.g., Security, Discovery)
      • Method: How do they solve problems? (e.g., Ambition, Innovation)
      • Purpose: What is the deeper philosophy driving them? (e.g., Purity, Faith)
      • System Bonus: For each color associated with your chosen ideals, add +1 to the corresponding Stat in Zone 2.

    • The Duality of Conflict:

      • External Conflict: What force or philosophy in the world opposes you?
      • Internal Conflict: What personal doubt, hypocrisy, or fear holds you back?

    Zone 2: The Stat Grid (Middle Left)

    "The Core Engine"

    Your capabilities are mapped onto a 3x3 grid, representing the intersection of your nature (Body, Mind, Essence) and your action (Control, Execution, Foundation). * The Baseline: At Level 0 (The Seed), all stats start at 1. * The Awakening: At Level 1, all stats raise to a baseline of 2. * The Calibration: Add your +3 bonus points derived from your Trinity of Ideals in Zone 1.

    The Resistance Box:

    To the right of the grid is your Resistance. * Calculation: Add your three highest stats together. * Function: This is your passive defense. If an opponent's roll does not exceed this number, their effect fails or deals no damage.

    Zone 3: The Resource Clocks (Bottom Left)

    "The Fuel Tanks"

    These three tracks represent your vital reserves. You will mark boxes from left to right as you take damage or spend energy, and erase marks when you recover. * HP (Health Points): Your physical integrity. If this fills up (reaches 0), you are Downed. * SP (Stamina Points): Your physical exertion. Spend this to perform special Maneuvers (like Shove or Parry) and Reactions. * EP (Energy Points): Your inner power. Spend this to activate the active effects of your Keywords.

    Zone 4: The Tag Monitor (Bottom Left Corner)

    "Active Effects"

    Combat and exploration are dynamic. This grid tracks the temporary conditions affecting your character. * Boosts (Left Column): Beneficial effects you can choose to expend (e.g., Haste, Ward). * Status (Right Column): Detrimental effects that trigger automatically (e.g., Daze, Blight). * Intensity: Note that each Tag has 5 checkboxes. Effects in this system stack. Having three Weaken tags is significantly more dangerous than having one.

    Zone 5: Keywords (The Right Column)

    "The Capability Library"

    This column tracks your unique, rule-breaking abilities and your progression from novice to legend. * The Seed: Your starting ability at Level 0. * The Awakening (Level 1): The three additional abilities you gain when your journey truly begins. * Progression: As you level up, you will learn new Keywords, filling the slots down the column. * The "Passive" Checkbox: The small box next to each slot indicates if that Keyword is currently "slotted" as a Passive ability. Your Force stat determines how many of these you can have active at once.

    Zone 6: Flaw & Compensation (Bottom Right)

    "The Override"

    This optional section tracks a significant narrative trade-off. * Flaw: A permanent penalty or restriction your character has accepted. * Compensation: The unique, powerful ability gained in exchange for that flaw.

    Quick Reference: * Animations: Simplified stat blocks for any creatures, drones, or companions. They have their own HP, SP, and Resistance. * Items: Your inventory. Items have Levels (which determine their keyword capacity) and a Durability clock. * Factions: Your standing with the world's organizations. Use the checkboxes to track the specific resources (Civil Systems) a faction can offer you.

    SHEET 1

    THE ACTION MENU

    What can I do right now?

    THE CORE MECHANIC * Roll: 2d10 (Standard Roll). Doubles add & reroll (Chain). * Calculate: SR + Level + Stat = Effect Value (EV). * The Goal: Beat the Target Number (TN) or Resistance.

    1. EFFECT ACTIONS (I want to hurt or change it)

    • Activate (1 Action): The basic attack or ability use.
      • Cost: 1 EP per Keyword (Reduced by Power stat).
    • Called Shot (1 Action): Attack with Disadvantage to target a specific component.
      • Effect: Damage Item durability OR Auto-Critical vs Entity.
    • Effort Roll (1 Action): Overcome a Barrier or Puzzle.

    2. TACTICAL MANEUVERS (I want to control the fight)

    • Move (1 Action): Move up to your Movement stat in range bands.
    • Shove (1 Action, 1 SP): Force target to move or fall Prone.
    • Disarm (1 Action, 1 SP): Force target to drop an item.
    • Restrain (1 Action, 1 SP): Apply the Bind Tag.
    • Taunt (1 Action, 1 SP): Force target to Lock On to you.
    • Lock On (1 Action, 1 SP): Grant Advantage to yourself AND your target against each other.
    • Combined Strike (1 Action, 1 SP): Attack using keywords from two equipped items.

    3. FOCUS ACTIONS (I want to reset or prepare)

    ! These consume your ENTIRE turn !

    • Recharge (1 SP): Restore SP equal to Brawn OR EP equal to Wit.
    • Recover (1 SP): Remove Tags equal to Brawn/Wit/Influence.
    • Ready (1 SP): Add Wit to your Resistance until next turn.
    • Advantage (1 SP): Gain Advantage on your next roll.
    • Assess/Clue (1 SP): Ask the Curator facts about a target equal to Technique.

    4. REACTIONS (I want to stop them)

    Triggered on enemy turn. Costs SP. * Avoid / Dodge (1 SP): Roll vs Enemy EV. If higher, negate attack. * Resist / Block (1 SP): Add your Level to your Resistance against one hit. * Parry (3 SP): Roll vs Enemy EV. If higher, negate attack AND Seize Priority (take your turn immediately). * Clash (2 SP): Intercept attack with your own. Loser takes combined damage. * Guard (1 SP): Take the hit for an adjacent Ally.

    Conditions, Statuses, and Boosts

    1. CONDITIONS (Persistent States)

    • Hidden: You cannot be targeted. Attacks against unaware targets gain Advantage. Ends if you attack or are detected (Reveal action).
    • Cover: Attacks against you suffer Disadvantage.
    • Prone: You suffer Disadvantage on all actions. Standing up costs a Move Action.
    • Exhausted: You have 0 SP. You suffer Disadvantage on all rolls.
    • Silenced: You have 0 EP. You cannot use Innate Keywords (Active Effects).
    • Unconscious: You have 0 HP. You cannot act. Any further damage is fatal.
    • Exposed: Attacks against you gain Advantage.

    2. BOOST TAGS (Beneficial)

    Player chooses when to expend these.

    Tag Color Effect (Expend To...)
    Liberate Null Negate an Immobilized or movement-restricting effect.
    Augment Silver Add any Keyword to an action (must still pay EP cost).
    Haste Yellow Gain one additional action immediately.
    Ward Green Negate all effects of a single incoming action.
    Bless Black Gain Advantage on a roll (decide before rolling).
    Charge Orange Reduce the total EP Cost of an action by 1.
    Cure White Remove one Status Tag from your pool (Free Action).
    Regen Red Restore 1 point to a Value (HP/SP/EP) of your choice.
    Enhance Blue Add a bonus equal to Influence to a roll result (after rolling).
    Obscure Purple Become Hidden. (TN = 10 + Level + Stat).

    3. STATUS TAGS (Detrimental)

    Trigger automatically when conditions are met.

    Tag Color Trigger & Effect
    Mark Null Trigger: You are attacked. Effect: Attacker gains Advantage.
    Impair Silver Trigger: You spend SP. Effect: Cost increases by +1.
    Slow Yellow Trigger: Start of turn. Effect: Lose 1 Action.
    Daze Green Trigger: You roll dice. Effect: Result reduced by # of Daze tags.
    Curse Black Trigger: You roll dice. Effect: Roll with Disadvantage.
    Bind Orange Passive: You are Immobilized. Pay 1 SP + Action to remove.
    Weaken White Trigger: You take damage. Effect: Resistance reduced by # of Weaken tags.
    Blight Red Trigger: You take an action. Effect: Suffer 1 unavoidable damage.
    Silence Blue Trigger: You use Keywords. Effect: Keyword application fails.
    Nullify Purple Passive: Passive Keywords are disabled. Removing takes 1 Priority.

    The Grammar of Power

    THE 10 PSYCHROMAS (Thematic Roots)

    Use this to determine the "flavor" of a power or creature.

    Code Color Ideal Narrative Themes
    0 Null Discovery The Void, Potential, Adaptation, The Unknown.
    1 Silver Ambition Speed, Precision, Duality, Space/Time.
    2 Yellow Freedom Lightning, Air, Momentum, Unpredictability.
    3 Green Security Earth, Stone, Gravity, Protection.
    4 Black Legacy Death, Entropy, Fear, History, Bone.
    5 Orange Innovation Metal, Tech, Fire (Industrial), Construction.
    6 White Purity Light, Biology, Flora, Healing, Order.
    7 Red Glory Fire (Primal), Plasma, Rage, Energy.
    8 Blue Influence Water, Ice, Sound, Command, Social.
    9 Purple Faith Shadow, Illusion, Spirit, Psychic.

    THE 10 CATEGORIES (Mechanical Functions)

    Use this to determine how a keyword functions in play.

    • .0 Flaw: The Trade-off.
      • Mechanic: Provides a passive penalty. Always paired with a Compensation.
    • .1 Gift: The Innate Trait.
      • Mechanic: A passive utility or magical sense. Often "always on."
    • .2 Talent: The Learned Skill.
      • Mechanic: A martial or technical proficiency. Improves specific actions.
    • .3 Quirk: The Oddity.
      • Mechanic: A niche ability, often social or sensory.
    • .4 Boost: The Self-Buff.
      • Mechanic: Applies a positive Tag to the user.
    • .5 Status: The Debuff.
      • Mechanic: Applies a negative Tag to the target.
    • .6 Form: The Shape.
      • Mechanic: Alters targeting (e.g., AoE, Chain, Splash).
    • .7 Modifier: The Rule-Breaker.
      • Mechanic: Alters the physics of the action (e.g., Ranged, Piercing).
    • .8 Drive: The Style.
      • Mechanic: Increases output or efficiency (e.g., Brutal, Rapid).
    • .9 Unique: The Ultimate.
      • Mechanic: Powerful, game-altering effects. High EP cost.

    HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

    1. Setup: When a scene begins, fill in Zone 1. Determine if there is a Threat (is there a time limit?). If so, set the Clock size based on the Difficulty Table (Layer 4).

    2. Populate: Fill in Zone 3. * Entities: Enemies. The Clock is their HP. The TN is their Resistance. * Barriers: Physical obstacles (Doors, Walls). The Clock is their structure. The TN is the difficulty to break/bypass. * Puzzles: Mental challenges. Usually a Clock of 1 (Binary) or 3 (Complex), with a high TN.

    3. The Loop: * When a player deals damage or succeeds on an Effort Roll, mark off boxes in Zone 3. * When a specific line in Zone 3 is full (resolved), mark off 1 box on the Situation Clock (Zone 2). * When the Situation Clock is full, the encounter is won. Mark off 1 box on the Scene Clock (Zone 1).

    4. The Consequences: At the end of the session, look at what happened. Did the players defeat the Villain (Entity)? Did they save the Hostage (Barrier)? Update the Node Tracker (Zone 4). This is your "save data" for the next session.
