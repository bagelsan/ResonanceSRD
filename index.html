    Psychromattica System Reference Document

    Welcome to a new kind of roleplaying. You've seen games with deep rules and games with compelling stories, but rarely do they come together so seamlessly. Psychromattica is an engine for creating unforgettable, character-driven sagas. It's a game that knows who you are.

    Your journey starts not with a character sheet, but with a personality quiz. For players who want their character's inner world, including their goals, methods, and fears, to be the driving force of the game, this is where it all begins. Before you even learn a rule, the Psychromattic Assessment and 5-Psychroma Profile Method help you discover your character's core identity. Your character's fundamental motivation, like a burning desire for Glory or an unshakeable need for Security, directly translates into mechanical strengths, suggesting which abilities you'll excel at and what kind of hero you are destined to become.

    Once you know who you are, you can learn what you can do. You can learn the entire game's core concept in less than 60 seconds. Forget juggling different rules for combat, skills, and social encounters. In Psychromattica, every challenge is a Clock, which acts as a progress bar that you deplete. Whether you're breaking down a door, persuading a noble, or fighting a monster, the goal is the same: roll your dice, beat the target number, and deplete the Clock. This simple, intuitive system has explosive potential with Chaining Doubles that can lead to Super Critical results, letting you perform acts of legendary prowess that can change the course of a battle in a single, epic roll.

    With this foundation, your turn becomes a tactical puzzle with a dozen possible solutions. This is where the game's depth begins to unfold. Engage with the system by managing Health Points; your physical Stamina (SP) for powerful maneuvers; and your inner Energy (EP) for reality-bending abilities. The Action Catalog is your toolkit.

    • Will you Change the situation with a direct attack?
    • Control the battlefield by shoving an enemy off a ledge or disarming them?
    • Or will you Prepare for the future by taking a moment to aim or buff an ally? Every action point is a critical decision.

    You'll learn to bend the battlefield to your will by wielding powerful effects that persist, creating tactical opportunities and dangerous consequences. The system introduces Tags, which are game-changers. Inflict debilitating Status Tags on your foes, turning them into walking vulnerabilities. A Blight Tag will drain their life force every time they act, while a Weaken Tag will shred their defenses. Conversely, grant your allies powerful Boost Tags that they can cash in at the perfect moment. A Haste Tag provides a crucial extra action, while a Ward Tag can completely negate a devastating enemy attack. Mastering Tags means mastering the flow of battle.

    This mastery will be tested in cinematic, high-stakes scenarios where every second counts. The Race Situation introduces the Threat Clock, a relentless antagonist that ticks down every round and with every action you take. Suddenly, your goal isn't merely to defeat the enemies; it's to solve the puzzle, rescue the hostage, and get out before the whole place comes down around you. This turns a standard encounter into a heart-pounding thriller.

    Through these trials, you will become a legend. Evolve from a "Seed" of potential into an "Awakened" hero, with a progression system that is all about learning, not just increasing numbers. Your character's journey is your own. You start as a Level 0 Seed with a single, defining Keyword. After your first major trial, you experience The Awakening, a massive explosion of power that unlocks your true potential. From there, you level up by learning new Keywords, the fundamental building blocks of all powers. You can even have a breakthrough mid-battle with a dramatic Stress Roll, learning a new ability in your moment of greatest need. This is complemented by a rich system for Gear, allowing you to find, craft, and wield items with their own embedded keywords, creating a perfect synergy between who you are and what you carry.

    Ultimately, Psychromattica is a system designed to be a flexible toolkit, not a rigid prison, with advanced options for running the campaign of your dreams. Its final layer is a grant of permission: your story comes first. The Curator is empowered to use or set aside any rule to serve the narrative. But for those who want to go deeper, the appendices offer a treasure trove of advanced tools. The Dynamic Campaign Engine allows you to build a 'living world' with factions and villains that progress on their own timeline; your players aren't just following a plot, they are actively disrupting or accelerating the future. Or you can run the game as a tactical, high-lethality rogue-lite in The Archive, where players are agents diving into a corrupted AI, turning temporary power into permanent progression run after run.

    All of this is supported by a library of infinite possibilities. The Master Keyword List is the source code of your power: over 100 unique, modular abilities, each with three distinct uses, allowing for near-infinite character builds. The Codex provides a world of inspiration, a thematic bestiary filled with ready-to-use characters, creatures, and items. And to begin your journey, the New Player Guide \& One-Shot provide a complete, guided experience designed to take you from a curious newcomer to an 'Awakened' hero in a single, thrilling session. It's the perfect, zero-friction introduction to the game.

    Psychromattica is a promise: that the character you imagine can be the hero you play, that your choices will echo through a living world, and that your story will be truly, uniquely yours.
