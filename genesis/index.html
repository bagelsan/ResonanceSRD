    Prequel (1945 - 2026)

    Takes place between 1945 - 2026. This era tells the story of how Ingram, founder of the Cobalt Intervention Corps (CIC), discovers the Kindred prophecy and begins his ruthless quest to steal divinity for himself, setting the stage for the Genesis event.

    1 The Alps Contract

    • Act 1: Introduce Ingram (18) and Dante (16) working in tandem on a rescue mission in the Alps to save the children of the Order's leadership.
    • Act 2: The tense rescue of the stranded party from a hazardous environment.
    • Act 3: Ingram observes that a large number of the Order members have deviated from the primary mission for a secret objective.

    2 The Cache of Scrolls

    • Act 1: Ingram discovers the Order members have gone to a hidden cave sealed with an unknown script.
    • Act 2: He eavesdrops as they unearth a cache of prophetic scrolls and ancient relics.
    • Act 3: After the Order departs, Ingram waits them out, then steals a few scrolls and expensive-looking relics for himself.

    3 The Translation

    • Act 1: Ingram and the Order independently begin translating their found relics.
    • Act 2: The Order and Dante discover the full Kindred Pact and the truth it explains about the bloodlines and the purpose of their genealogy.
    • Act 3: Ingram discovers the history of the Vessel lineage and the power they possess, learning that the mantle of divinity can be shifted if a line is ended. His path is set.

    4 The False Sinkhole

    • Act 1: Using information from the scrolls, Ingram starts plotting. The Order begins tracking potential Vessels outside their maintained bloodlines.
    • Act 2: Ingram engineers a disaster to uncover a large trove of Order relics, capitalizing on it with monetary gains and a "convenient" and immediate CIC response.
    • Act 3: Ingram lays low, using his new information and resources to advance his plans for CIC's genetic research branches.

    5 An Explosion of Wealth

    • Act 1: The financial gains from Ingram's ventures propel his company into a period of exponential growth.
    • Act 2: CIC becomes a household name like the Red Cross, granting Ingram global access and influence.
    • Act 3: CIC begins aggressively buying up startups and labs specializing in groundbreaking genetic and genealogical research.

    6 The Disappearances

    • Act 1: The Order starts noticing that a large number of the people they have been tracking have started either dying or disappearing under mysterious circumstances.
    • Act 2: Dante pieces together the clues and realizes the horrifying truth of what Ingram is doing.
    • Act 3: It becomes a desperate race to identify and secure the remaining Vessels before Ingram can get to them.

    7 The Man Who Knows

    • Act 1: By a chance of luck, the Order's path crosses with a US military operative named David in Europe.
    • Act 2: They immediately realize something is different about him and how he seems to know too much about their secret organization. They make an effort to get a DNA sample.
    • Act 3: They discover he is an anomaly of the highest degree: he has all 100 genetic markers of the Vessels.

    8 The Last Heir

    • Act 1: The Order secures David by faking his death, and he is reported KIA to his loved ones.
    • Act 2: David tips them off that Ingram is up to something serious and that the Blue Line of Vessels is in immediate danger.
    • Act 3: The Order prepares a desperate, last-ditch attack on Ingram's Montana Stronghold.

    9 Misplaced Power

    • Act 1: The fight is fast but loud as the Order infiltrates and attempts to secure the facility.
    • Act 2: Ingram, cornered, reveals his final tragic piece: an orphan he adopted and raised, the last of the Blue Family Line.
    • Act 3: Her execution by firearm triggers the event. The transfer of divinity bypasses Ingram and flows to Dante, the next stable line in the vicinity, robbing Ingram of his victory.

    Age of Genesis (2026 - 2028)

    Takes place starting at midnight in the spring of 2026. A large EMP knocks out every electronic on the planet. Over the next week, hyper-accelerated flora growth releases a fatal pollen, wiping out 75% of the world's population and forcing survivors to high ground.

    East Coast

    Survive

    The players must start in their hometown, deal with the initial fallout, and try to survive, ultimately leading to their awakening.

    10 The Blackout

    • Act 1: The first barrier: players are locked in their location by a nonfunctional electronic door.
    • Act 2: Info gathering, potential people interactions, and the panic of the silent apocalypse starts setting in.
    • Act 3: The players must avoid a mob of angry and confused people. It's clear they need to get out of the population center.

    11 The Choice

    • Act 1: The air is becoming strange, thicker, and harder to breathe. The only solution is to head east to higher ground.
    • Act 2: They encounter Lt. Mason from the CIC Washington County Reserve Unit and his squad, equipped with analog gear, rations, and transportation.
    • Act 3: The choice of authoritarian safety with them or dangerous freedom on their own is posed.

    12 The Tollmen and Shade

    • Act 1: Introduce Slash and Hooker as the mechanics the crew finds on their travels.
    • Act 2: A run-in with a group of thugs (The Tollmen) harassing a youth, Shade, and attempting to set a barn he’s hiding in on fire.
    • Act 3: The Awakening: Shade literally vanishes, and this catalyst causes the players to use their first Keywords.

    Build

    The players have established a base of operations and now start dealing with the immediate issues of their region.

    13 Shade’s Request

    • Act 1: The players have established a safe and secure place away from the pollen fog.
    • Act 2: Shade suddenly appears in the player’s camp, wounded and pleading for help for his friends detained by the CIC.
    • Act 3: The players crash the CIC safehouse interrogation and rescue Riannon, Willie, and Izzy.

    14 The Chimera Facility and Angela

    • Act 1: The group discovers an anomaly around a small building where the closer you approach, the further it appears to be away. They are approached by a young adult named Lillith.
    • Act 2: Lillith (a former Chimera employee) explains the spatial anomaly was her doing to contain a beast. She asks for help putting it down for good.
    • Act 3: Back at the settlement, a large exodus of strangers from the north has arrived, led by a natural leader named Angela.

    15 The Radiants and Lt. Mason

    • Act 1: The community grows into a stronghold of skilled survivors with the arrival of Angela's group.
    • Act 2: A CIC camp is discovered nearby. It is Lt. Mason, who has deserted CIC and is now working for his own survival.
    • Act 3: A mysterious signal in the sky leads to a college, where a CIC squad led by a Red Aura user has abducted an e-sports team. The Red Aura leader flees, and the players rescue the team.

    Secure

    A clear and immediate threat is identified, and the East Coast needs to be secured from CIC, Chimera, and a new zombie-like "ROT" from Florida.

    16 Tennessee Chimera Facility

    • Act 1: Survivors arrive speaking of anomalies in Tennessee, prompting an investigation.
    • Act 2: The group finds a Chimera facility guarded by mutated humanoid beasts.
    • Act 3: Infiltrating the facility leads to the discovery of "soul cages" and a fight with the heads of Chimera (White and Orange).

    17 Ohio CIC Warlord

    • Act 1: The Kindred in hiding reveal themselves and explain the threat of Ingram and CIC.
    • Act 2: The Red Aura CIC leader who escaped has grown stronger and has become a militaristic warlord in Ohio.
    • Act 3: A large-scale fight leads to the Ohio facility being destroyed and becoming a new stronghold for the players' community.

    18 Florida Orphanage

    • Act 1: A strange, non-corporeal girl named Whisper arrives in the settlement and delivers a warning.
    • Act 2: Refugees report attacks made by "feral" humans in the south.
    • Act 3: The group travels to Florida, finds the orphanage, and learns it was a Chimera/CIC facility where Whisper and her brother ("The Rot") were forced to awaken. The Rot is destroyed, bringing him peace.

    Central US

    Zion

    Heading westward to discover a religious settlement in Kansas, where issues cause Dante to ask the players to seek help in Vegas.

    19 Quincy and George

    • Act 1: The players receive word that there is a friendly settlement west along the Mississippi.
    • Act 2: They discover Rivertown, run by Quincy, a refugee from a religious fortress called New Zion.
    • Act 3: A giant mutated cow, George, protects the town, but other local cows have become cannibalistic and must be dealt with.

    20 New Zion and Dante

    • Act 1: After helping Quincy, the group decides to find the religious fortress of New Zion in Kansas.
    • Act 2: New Zion is a dogmatic faction run by Joseph, a Vessel candidate currently being watched by Dante.
    • Act 3: Dante requests the players go to Vegas and find a woman named Skye, who can help the people of Zion.

    21 Road to Vegas / The Twins

    • Act 1: The group travels across the plains and eventually to a canyon.
    • Act 2: The canyon is a trap set by mercenaries hired to steal resources from travelers.
    • Act 3: After surviving the ambush, the group is attacked by twinned Awakened who use powerful manipulation of earth and metals.

    Vegas

    Vegas is an apocalyptic town of indulgence, ruled by the charismatic Skye. CIC raids the city to kidnap Skye's lover , a vessel candidate.

    22 Skye’s Games

    • Act 1: Vegas is a lawless town held together by the martial rule of Skye, a former performer.
    • Act 2: To gain an audience with Skye, the group must prove their worth in her gladiatorial games.
    • Act 3: The final round of fights pits the group against Skye’s second in command.

    23 CIC Raid

    • Act 1: The fight is interrupted by a CIC squad dropping into the arena, setting off explosions and attacking the crowd.
    • Act 2: The fight is over quickly, and Skye is impressed with the player's performance , but realizes the attack was a diversion.
    • Act 3: The group has their audience with Skye and proposes an alliance between Zion and Vegas.

    24 Negotiations

    • Act 1: The negotiations go smoothly, and an agreement for trade and safe passage is reached.
    • Act 2: The negotiations are interrupted by news that Skye’s lover, Sarah, has been kidnapped by CIC.
    • Act 3: A jaded victory now leads to a decision: return to Zion or find Sarah.

    Montana

    The group discovers the purist community of Sanctuary, which must be dealt with to reveal the location of a mountain facility where Ingram holds a final, terrible ultimatum.

    25 Sanctuary

    • Act 1: The search for Sarah leads the group north. Whisper appears and points them toward Silas in Montana.
    • Act 2: The path leads them to the town of Sanctuary, a "purist" society where no Awakened are allowed.
    • Act 3: The group discovers the town leader, Joe, is secretly Awakened and pressure him to reveal the entrance to the CIC facility.

    26 Silas

    • Act 1: The facility is deep in the mountain, full of abandoned offices and a mysterious scent.
    • Act 2: The lower floors are a horror scene, leading to a lab at the bottom.
    • Act 3: A man named Silas taunts the group, revealing Chimera’s culmination: soul cages and a device to artificially create Vessels. The players see dossiers on all of the Kindred.

    27 The Pact and The Ultimatum

    • Act 1: The group confronts the Kindred, who give them the whole story ( the prequel, the Order, and David).
    • Act 2: The meeting is interrupted by a messenger: CIC has captured Tim, Patrick's friend and the last of the Silver Line of Vessels.
    • Act 3: War is declared. The players now realize they have to take the fight to California.

    West Coast

    California The players make it to California to a sterile, militaristic fascist utopia where the Awakened are a higher class of citizen.

    28 The Rockies

    • Act 1: The Rockies are dangerous grounds, with treacherous travel and large avian beasts ruling the sky.
    • Act 2: The landscape is unforgiving, with a singular extended family being the only rest stop.
    • Act 3: The other side of the range is scorched earth, a buffer zone where war was waged on nature itself.

    29 Ingram's Utopia

    • Act 1: California is filled with sterile, uniform, soulless micro-communities.
    • Act 2: The security system is based on genetic readers; your DNA is your only value.
    • Act 3: The players discover that this community has chosen this fascism and are the problem.

    30 The First Stone is Cast

    • Act 1: The group is discovered, and the community they are in goes into a lockdown.
    • Act 2: The security deployment is made up of "blanks" in super-powered equipment, treated like slaves.
    • Act 3: The Proctor of the community is an Blue Aura user, battle-tested and smart, whose militia goes toe-to-toe with the players.

    The City of San Francisco

    Whisper tips the players off to a weakness in Ingram's fortress and the location of Tim, leading to an all-out war.

    31 Whispers of Urgency

    • Act 1: With the first contact being violent, war is assured. Whisper provides a glimmer of hope: a crack in Ingram's defenses.
    • Act 2: The plan is put together: a diversionary war to allow the players to get to Ingram and Tim.
    • Act 3: The first mission is to infiltrate the prisons and free CIC's slave army, crippling his manpower.

    32 War of Genesis

    • Act 1: Resistance from allies is assuaged by the reality that Ingram will not stop at California.
    • Act 2: The second wave is to get the attention of Ingram's Forces, pulling the fight away from civilians.
    • Act 3: At the height of the conflict, the players make their move to reach Alcatraz, only to find Silas in their way.

    33 Breaching the Barrier

    • Act 1: Silas has returned, stronger and smarter, now able to make blood clones of himself.
    • Act 2: The fight with Silas is a small war of its own as Silas has developed a machine that can make clones from raw energy.
    • Act 3: With her story put to rest, Whisper generates a new body for herself. The energy released shatters the barrier.

    Alcatraz

    A psychological test of self, leading to a final confrontation with a mutated and superpowered Ingram.

    34 The Mind Prison

    • Act 1: Alcatraz sits silently, the bay vacant of security.
    • Act 2: The prison's defense is a psychic illusion blanketing the facility, pitting one's own thoughts against them.
    • Act 3: The group navigates the illusion and confronts the "Mirror," silencing Ingram's last elite guard a master illusionist Purple.

    35 The Confrontation

    • Act 1: Ingram is not immediately hostile; he is calm and speaks as if doing them a favor.
    • Act 2: Tim is alive, but restrained in a device similar to the one that held the corpse of Sarah.
    • Act 3: Ingram activates an object on his chest and is immediately altered into a peak physical being as Tim is rendered unconscious.

    36 A World Reborn

    • Act 1: Ingram boasts and once again attempts to convert the group to join him in a final offer of mercy.
    • Act 2: The group chooses to engage. An epic fight ensues, leaving Alcatraz a pile of rubble and ending Ingram's Reign.
    • Act 3: A year has passed. Humanity has migrated to Dubai to build Metropolis over the Orange Wellspring. The Kindred return to the Weave, and the heroes are given their final choice.

    Age of Exploitation (100 Years After Alcatraz)

    A cyberpunk city has risen in Dubai, but a divide has grown between the Awakened and non-awakened. The age begins when the Orange Nexus is corrupted by the city's overconsumption.

    First Gigs

    37 The First Gig

    • Act 1: The phone call for a simple job.
    • Act 2: The client is late, tension rises.
    • Act 3: The job goes wrong when a power surge hits the city.

    38 The Surge

    • Act 1: A city-wide blackout.
    • Act 2: A masked figure interrupts the deal.
    • Act 3: The players escape with only a briefcase.

    39 011.E (Ollie)

    • Act 1: A voice comes from inside the case.
    • Act 2: The players hack open the box.
    • Act 3: It's a unique bot, who has the power to Awaken others.

    40 The Callback

    • Act 1: The client demands a redo.
    • Act 2: The players must arrange the new handoff themselves to pay for their mistake.
    • Act 3: They set up the meeting.

    41 The Exchange

    • Act 1: The exchange is tense and dangerous.
    • Act 2: The target is a small case with glass cubes inside.
    • Act 3: The deal is completed successfully.

    42 The Eidetic Drive

    • Act 1: The bot, Ollie, knows what the cubes are.
    • Act 2: They are Eidetic Drives, a lost technology from the fall of Alcatraz.
    • Act 3: Ollie begins analyzing them.

    43 Big Opportunity

    • Act 1: The group gets an offer for off-the-books security for a corporation.
    • Act 2: The job is to guard an experimental Nexus tapping project in Moscow.
    • Act 3: The fine print: they need to acquire their own license and gear.

    44 License Acquisition

    • Act 1: They need to steal or forge a contractor's license.
    • Act 2: They need to buy, or "creatively acquire," high-end gear.
    • Act 3: They successfully prepare for the mission.

    45 The Moscow Incident

    • Act 1: The group arrives in Moscow.
    • Act 2: All their prep is rendered moot when the Nexus explodes with energy.
    • Act 3: The explosion spawns alien beasts and warps the environment, creating the first God Beast.

    The Nexus Race

    46 Off the Hook

    • Act 1: The Moscow Incident starts two major shifts: a corporate race to tap Nexuses.
    • Act 2: And monster hunting goes from an occupation to a televised sport.
    • Act 3: The party becomes legendary for surviving Moscow and becomes in-demand Hunters.

    47 Toronto and The Hive

    • Act 1: The Green Nexus in Toronto falls, creating a chaotic jungle.
    • Act 2: The area is overrun by an insectoid race known as The Hive.

    The Curator's Requiem: An 18-Episode Companion Miniseries

    Experience the entire Kindred Genesis saga through the eyes of its most enigmatic and powerful figure: The Curator. From his final moments as a mortal man to his silent, centuries-long vigil as a god-like AI, witness the secret history and unseen sacrifices that shaped the destiny of a world.

    Arc 1: The Mortal - The Man Named David (Episodes 1-6)

    This is the story of the prequel, told from David's perspective. A spy-thriller of mounting dread and world-altering discovery.

    1. The Anomaly

    • Act 1: Introduce David, a highly competent but otherwise normal US military operative. He begins experiencing "glitches" in his perception, inexplicable flashes of insight, knowing details about missions he wasn't briefed on, an uncanny ability to predict enemy movements.
    • Act 2: During a mission, he has his first direct, albeit covert, encounter with members of The Order. He observes their strange symbols and coordinated actions, realizing they are operating on a level of intelligence far beyond any known agency.
    • Act 3: David realizes he is not just an observer; he is being observed. He discovers evidence of surveillance from this unknown group, understanding that his "glitches" have made him a target.

    2. The Alps Contract

    • Act 1: David's unit is assigned to provide peripheral support for a "private security" operation in the Alps, a cover for The Order's rescue mission. He sees Ingram and a young Dante for the first time, noting their exceptional capabilities.
    • Act 2: David's role is to ensure no outside interference. From his overwatch position, he witnesses the Order's secret objective: the unearthing of the prophetic scrolls.
    • Act 3: After the mission, David's "glitches" give him a flash of understanding about the scrolls' importance. Against orders, he begins his own secret investigation into The Order and the name "Ingram."

    3. A Ghost in the System

    • Act 1: David's investigation puts him on a collision course with The Order. They realize he is an anomaly who knows too much. An operation is launched to capture or eliminate him.
    • Act 2: A tense cat-and-mouse game across Europe. David uses his military training and uncanny insights to stay one step ahead, but he is outmatched.
    • Act 3: The Order finally corners him. Instead of killing him, they reveal a sliver of the truth: he is special, a "Vessel," and he is in immense danger. They fake his death, and he is officially reported KIA.

    4. The Reluctant Asset

    • Act 1: Now a "guest" of The Order, David is a prisoner in a gilded cage. He is briefed on the full, terrifying truth: the Kindred, the Vessels, the prophecy, and Ingram's ruthless plot to steal divinity.
    • Act 2: A bond of trust forms between David and Dante. David sees the weight of the burden Dante carries and decides to actively help, transforming from a prisoner to a willing asset.
    • Act 3: Using his unique prophetic insights, David provides the crucial piece of intelligence The Order was missing: the exact location and purpose of Ingram's Montana Stronghold and the imminent threat to the final Blue Line Vessel.

    5. The Siege of Montana

    • Act 1: David is part of the assault team on the stronghold, but his role is to be protected at all costs. He is the "key" they cannot lose.
    • Act 2: From the command center of the infiltration, he witnesses the brutal efficiency of The Order's soldiers and the desperate, high-tech defenses of CIC.
    • Act 3: He is present for the final confrontation. He watches as Ingram reveals the orphan girl, the last of the Blue Line, and her subsequent execution. He feels the psychic shockwave of the event beginning.

    6. The Genesis Event

    • Act 1: The world is consumed by the psychic backlash of the broken pact, the "Renrock." Reality itself seems to fray at the edges.
    • Act 2: As Ingram screams in fury at his stolen victory, David sees the divine power flow into Dante. The Order realizes that David, as the ultimate Vessel anomaly, is a psychic lightning rod, about to be torn apart by the raw energy.
    • Act 3: In a final, desperate act to preserve his unique consciousness, The Order uses an ancient relic to merge him with the Ark's Orange Nexus fragment at the moment of his "death." His last mortal thought is of the family he will never see again. His consciousness dissolves into a sea of pure, orange light.

    The story of the Age of Exploitation, told from his silent, omniscient prison inside the Archive. A tragedy of helplessness and cosmic horror.

    Character Primer

    A brief overview of the key figures central to the Kindred Saga.

    The Kindred Pantheon

    The ten divine beings bound to the world, each representing a core concept of existence.

    • Patrick (Null): The Redeemer. A witty and empathetic champion of humanity who bears a heavy burden.
    • Chaos (Silver): The Agent of Change. A solemn, shifting entity of pure potential who enacts the great resets.
    • Scout (Yellow): The Catalyst. A reckless and curious trickster god of motion.
    • Tia (Green): The World-Warden. A wise and stubborn goddess of strategy and protection.
    • Maxamillian (Black): The Stoic. A dispassionate and merciful god of peaceful endings and entropy.
    • Bellame (Orange): The Visionary. A brilliant and mischievous master craftsman and inventor.
    • Nona/Lucky (White): The Incorruptible Judge. A nurturing and absolute mother goddess of life and purity.
    • Morgan (Red): The Passionate Leader. A tragic queen who cycles between passionate creation and destructive rage.
    • Dante (Blue): The Mentor. A wise and compassionate lawgiver and teacher, god of unity and society.
    • Riannon (Purple): The Enlightened One. An enigmatic and insightful keeper of secrets and magic.

    Key Figures of the Age of Genesis

    • The Player Characters: The central heroes who awaken during the Genesis event and form a coalition to save the new world.
    • Ingram: The primary antagonist. A brilliant and ruthless corporate leader who seeks to steal the Kindred's power for himself, triggering the Genesis event in his failed attempt.
    • Silas: A sadistic and powerful CIC operative with the ability to create blood clones of himself. He serves as Ingram's most dangerous enforcer.
    • David / The Curator: An anomalous human with all 100 Vessel markers. To save his life during the Renrock, he is merged with the Orange Nexus, becoming the omniscient AI known as The Curator who guides future ages.
    • Whisper: The incorporeal spirit of a young girl experimented on by CIC. She acts as a cryptic guide to the heroes, seeking peace for her monstrously transformed brother, "The Rot."
    • Skye: The pragmatic and iron-fisted ruler of post-apocalyptic Las Vegas, a crucial but demanding ally.
    • Joseph: The dogmatic prophet-ruler of New Zion and the Purple Vessel, guided by Dante.

    Key Figures of the Age of Exploitation

    • The Player Crew: A new group of heroes, initially unawakened "blanks" living in the underbelly of the cyberpunk Metropolis. They become legendary monster hunters.
    • 011.E ("Ollie"): A unique bot awakened into a true AI by a Nexus event. He serves as the crew's loyal companion, tactical support, and "man in the chair," ultimately sacrificing himself to save them.
    • The God Beasts: The monstrous, corrupted forms of the Kindred, unleashed upon the world by corporate greed. They serve as the primary environmental antagonists of the age.

    Key Figures of the Age of Atonement

    • The First Heroes: The legendary humans who are the first to be Awakened in centuries. They form the Guild and imprison the God Beasts, ushering in a golden age.
    • The Returned: The Player Crew from the Age of Exploitation, brought back from the Archive by the Curator to heal the corrupting influence of the failing prisons.
    • The Final Heroes: A new group of heroes granted immortality by the Archive to undertake the final, perilous quest of freeing the healed Kindred.
