    Kindred Genesis Campaign Outline

    Prequel: Kindred Genesis (1945 - 2026)

    Overview

    This prequel, titled "Discovery," tells the tale of how Ingram started a private rescue company (Cobalt Intervention Corps, or CIC) that gets a job from the Order and discovers the prophecy, setting him on a dark path.

    Arc 1: Discovery

    Episode 1: The Alps Contract

    Introduce Ingram (18) and Dante (16) working in tandem on a rescue mission in the alps to save the children of Order leadership. The mission evolves from a simple rescue to the realization that a large number of Order members are missing.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, The Vessel Bloodlines
    • Supporting Nodes: Ingram, Dante

    Episode 2: The Cache of Scrolls

    Ingram discovers that the rescued Order members have gone off on a secret objective. He follows them to a cave sealed with unknown script on parchment of an unknown material. Ingram eavesdrops on the discovery of a cache of prophetic scrolls and ancient relics, then waits them out to steal a few scrolls and expensive-looking items.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, The Prophecy (Cache of Scrolls)
    • Supporting Nodes: Ingram, Dante

    Episode 3: The Translation

    Ingram and the Order independently begin translating their found relics. The Order and Dante discover the full Pact and the truth it explains about the bloodlines and the true purpose of the genealogy efforts they have maintained for centuries. Meanwhile, Ingram discovers the history of the Vessel lineage and the power those lines possess. He learns that the mantle of Vessel can be shifted if the line is ended, and his path is set.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, The Prophecy (The Pact), The Vessel Bloodlines
    • Supporting Nodes: Ingram, Dante

    Arc 2: Ingram's Rise

    Overview

    Using his stolen info, Ingram raids historical sites, looking for relics of the Order to steal and sell for money to fund his genealogy and genetic research branches of his now huge company of private disaster/humanitarian aid and security.

    Episode 4: The False Sinkhole

    Using information from the stolen relics, Ingram starts plotting how to gain enough power to enact his plan. At the same time, the Order starts tracking specific lineages to document potential Vessels. Ingram engineers a disaster to uncover a large trove of Order relics and written documents, capitalizing with monetary gains and a "convenient" and immediate response to the disaster before laying low with his new resources.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, Ingram / CIC, The Vessel Bloodlines
    • Event Node: The Engineered Sinkhole Disaster

    Episode 5: An Explosion of Wealth

    The financial gains from Ingram's ventures propel his company into exponential growth. The CIC becomes a household name like the Red Cross or FEMA, granting Ingram global access and influence. He then suddenly begins buying up startups and labs based on new, groundbreaking genetic and genealogical research.

    • Canon Nodes: Ingram / CIC
    • Theme Node: Genetic & Genealogical Research

    Episode 6: The Disappearances

    The Order starts noticing that a large number of the people they have been tracking have started either dying or disappearing under mysterious or tragic circumstances. Dante starts to put together the clues and realizes what is happening. It has now become a race to identify and secure the Vessels before Ingram can get to them.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, Ingram / CIC, The Vessel Bloodlines
    • Supporting Nodes: Dante

    Arc 3: The Catalyst

    Overview

    David and the Order try to cut off Ingram from his goal but ultimately fail, causing Ingram to initiate the Genesis, a planetary rebirth.

    Episode 7: The Man Who Knows

    By a chance of luck, David's and the Order’s paths cross in Europe whilst he is on military deployment. They realize immediately that something is different about him and how he seems to know too much about the Order. After making an effort to get a DNA sample, they discover that he is an anomaly of the highest degree: he has all 100 markers.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, The Vessel Bloodlines (Anomaly)
    • Supporting Nodes: David

    Episode 8: The Last Heir

    The Order secures David by faking his death, and he is reported KIA to his loved ones. David tips them off that Ingram is up to something serious and the Blue Line is in danger. The Order prepares an attack on Ingram's Montana Stronghold.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, The Vessel Bloodlines (Blue Line)
    • Supporting Nodes: David, Ingram

    Episode 9: Misplaced Power

    The infiltration of Ingram's facility is fast but loud. Inside, Ingram holds the last of the Blue Family Line, a former orphan he adopted and raised from childhood to be the final tragic piece of his plan. Her execution by firearm triggers the event, but the transfer of divinity is robbed from Ingram. Instead of going to him, it shifts from Eleanor's bloodline to Dante's, the next stable line in the vicinity.

    • Canon Nodes: The Order, Ingram / CIC, The Vessel Bloodlines (Blue & Dante's Lines), The Genesis Event
    • Location Node: Ingram's Montana Stronghold
    • Supporting Nodes: Dante, Eleanor (The Last Blue Heir)

    Age of Genesis (2026 Onward)

    Overview

    Takes place starting at midnight in the spring of 2026. A large EMP knocks out every electronic on the planet, causing a cataclysmic failure of humanity’s infrastructure. The fauna starts to grow at an increased rate, and a substance, like pollen, becomes noticeable in the air. This substance is fatal to many, forcing people to high ground. In one week, over 75% of the world population is wiped out.

    Arc 4: Survive (East Coast)

    Overview

    The players must start in their hometown and deal with the initial fallout of the event. They must choose between the security of a structured group or the freedom to forge their own way, ultimately leading to their Awakening.

    Episode 10: The Blackout

    In the first 24 hours, the players need to find a secure method of survival. They must gather resources amidst rising panic, and it becomes very clear that they need to get out of the population center.

    • Canon Node: The Genesis Event
    • Player Node: The Player Cell (Formation)

    Episode 11: The Choice

    The air is becoming strange, thicker, and harder to breathe, pushing people east to higher ground. The players encounter Lt. Mason from the CIC Washington County Reserve Unit and his squad, and are posed with the choice of safety with them or freedom on their own.

    • Player Node: The Player Cell
    • Supporting Nodes: Lt. Mason, CIC Washington County Reserve Unit
    • Environmental Node: The Pollen Fog

    Episode 12: The Tollmen and Shade

    The players meet Slash and Hooker, mechanics traveling either with Mason's crew or on their own. They run into a group of thugs shaking down a scared teen, Shade, who is trying to get to high ground. The resulting conflict triggers the players' Awakening. In the aftermath, Shade disappears.

    • Player Node: The Player Cell (Awakening)
    • Supporting Nodes: The Tollmen (Slash & Hooker), Shade, The Thugs

    Arc 5: Build

    Overview

    The players have established a base of operations and now start dealing with the immediate issues of their region, CIC being a threat to freedom and discovering the horrors of the apocalyptic world.

    Episode 13: Shade’s Request

    After establishing a safe place away from the pollen fog, Shade suddenly appears in the base, wounded and pleading for help for his friends. The party crashes a safehouse interrogation, saving Riannon, Willie, and Izzy. * Player Node: The Player Coalition * Supporting Nodes: Shade's Cell (Shade, Riannon, Willie, Izzy), The Vultures (Implied Antagonist)

    Episode 14: The Chimera Facility and Angela

    With the pollen mostly gone, the settlement expands its search for supplies and discovers an anomaly around a small building. It's a Chimera animal testing facility, abandoned but still containing a singular crazed beast. Lillith, a former employee, explains the anomaly was her doing to contain the beast. Back at the settlement, a large exodus of strangers from the north has arrived, led by a natural leader named Angela.

    • Canon Node: Chimera
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: The Chimera Testing Facility
    • Supporting Nodes: Lillith (Former Chimera Employee), Angela, The Northern Exodus

    Episode 15: The Radiants and Lt. Mason

    The players are introduced to the unique characters from Angela's group as the community fortifies itself. Lt. Mason's camp is discovered nearby; he has denounced his association with CIC and offers his skills. A mysterious signal leads to the rescue of an e-sports team ("The Radiants") who were abducted by a CIC squad, though their leader escapes.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Supporting Nodes: Angela's Group (The Radiants), Lt. Mason, The E-Sports Team, Escaped CIC Leader

    Arc 6: Secure

    Overview

    A clear and immediate threat is identified, and the east coast needs to be secured from CIC, Chimera, and a new "ROT" from Florida that creates zombies and feral humans. Chimera is discovered to be a subsidiary company of CIC owned by Ingram.

    Episode 16: Tennessee Chimera Facility

    As their local area grows, the settlement receives survivors who speak of anomalies in Tennessee. The group investigates a facility guarded by mutated humanoid beasts, discovering the "soul cages" and the heads of Chimera, leading to a major fight.

    • Canon Node: Chimera (Soul Cages)
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Tennessee Chimera Facility
    • Supporting Nodes: Chimera Leadership, Mutated Humanoid Beasts

    Episode 17: Ohio CIC Warlord

    The Kindred in hiding reveal themselves, explaining enough about Ingram and CIC to frame the threat. The large CIC facility in Ohio has grown under the command of the escaped CIC leader, who has become a militaristic warlord. A large-scale fight leads to the facility's destruction and its conversion into a new stronghold for the player's community.

    • Canon Nodes: Ingram / CIC, The Kindred
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Ohio CIC Facility
    • Supporting Nodes: The Ohio Warlord (Escaped CIC Leader)

    Episode 18: Florida Orphanage

    A strange, disembodied girl named Whisper arrives with a warning. Refugees from the south report attacks by "feral" humans whose victims rise again. The group travels to Florida and finds an orphanage facility, the source of the Rot. They learn it's a joint Chimera/CIC project where Whisper and her brother were forced to Awaken. The only way to stop her brother, The Rot, is to destroy him, bringing him peace.

    • Canon Nodes: Chimera, Ingram / CIC, The Rot
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Florida Orphanage Facility
    • Supporting Nodes: Whisper, The Rot (Brother)

    Central US Campaign

    Arc 7: Zion

    Overview

    Heading westward, the players discover a religious settlement in Kansas convinced their powers are a gift from God. Issues in the settlement cause Dante to ask the players to reach out to a new contact to help.

    Episode 19: Quincy and George

    Following a tip, the players discover Rivertown, run by Quincy, a refugee from Zion. The town's safety is ensured by George, a giant mutated cow who preys on other predators. However, George's herd is becoming cannibalistic and must be dealt with.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Rivertown
    • Supporting Nodes: Quincy, George (Mutated Cow)

    Episode 20: New Zion and Dante

    After helping Quincy, the group investigates the religious fortress of New Zion. It is a dogmatic faction run by Joseph, a Vessel candidate being watched by Dante. Dante requests the players travel to Vegas and find a woman named Skye, who can help the people of Zion.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: The Vessel Bloodlines
    • Location Node: New Zion
    • Supporting Nodes: Dante, Joseph (Vessel Candidate)

    Episode 21: Road to Vegas / The Twins

    Traveling across the plains, the group is ambushed in a canyon by a mercenary group hired to steal from travelers. After surviving the trap, they are attacked by twinned Awakened who manipulate earth and metal.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Supporting Nodes: The Mercenary Group, The Awakened Twins

    Arc 8: Vegas

    Overview

    Vegas is an apocalyptic town of indulgence ruled by the charismatic might of Skye. Her games are gladiatorial entertainment. A CIC raid kidnaps Skye’s lover, Sarah, for her genetic uniqueness.

    Episode 22: Skye’s Games

    To gain an audience with Skye, leader of lawless Vegas, the players must prove their worth in the arena at the Hoover Dam. Their first round of fights leads up to a match against Skye's second in command.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Vegas, The Hoover Dam Arena
    • Supporting Nodes: Skye, Skye's Second in Command

    Episode 23: CIC Raid

    The fight is interrupted by a CIC Squad dropping into the arena, setting off explosions and attacking the crowd. The attack seems purposeless, but Skye is impressed with the players' handling of the situation.

    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Vegas
    • Supporting Nodes: Skye, CIC Squad

    Episode 24: Negotiations

    The players have their audience with Skye and propose an alliance with Zion. A resolution is reached: Vegas will provide supplies and haven for those who wish to leave Zion. The negotiations are interrupted by news that Skye's lover, Sarah, has been kidnapped by CIC.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Location Node: Vegas, New Zion
    • Supporting Nodes: Skye, Sarah (Skye's Lover)

    Arc 9: Montana

    Overview

    Whisper tips off the players that Sarah is in Montana. The group must deal with the purist community of Sanctuary to find the CIC mountain facility. Ingram plays his first card, using an ally's life as leverage.

    Episode 25: Sanctuary

    The search for Sarah leads the group north to the town of Sanctuary, a purist society where no Awakened are allowed. They discover the town leader, Joe, is secretly Awakened and pressure him to reveal the entrance to the nearby CIC facility.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Location Node: Sanctuary
    • Supporting Nodes: Whisper, Sarah, Joe (Sanctuary Leader)

    Episode 26: Silas

    The facility is a 40-floor descent into horror, culminating in a lab at the bottom. The group is taunted by Silas as they discover Chimera's ultimate project: soul cages and a device to artificially create Vessels. They also find dossiers on all of the Kindred.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Nodes: Chimera (Soul Cages), The Kindred, The Order
    • Location Node: The Mountain Facility
    • Supporting Nodes: Silas

    Episode 27: The Pact and The Ultimatum

    The group confronts the Kindred, who give them the whole story (the Pact, the prequel, the Order, and David). The meeting is interrupted by a messenger: CIC has captured Tim, Patrick's friend and the last of the Silver Line of Vessels, who was hiding in the players' stronghold. War is declared.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Nodes: The Kindred, The Order, The Pact, Ingram / CIC, The Vessel Bloodlines (Silver Line)
    • Supporting Nodes: David, Tim (Patrick's friend)

    West Coast Campaign

    Arc 10: The State of California

    Overview

    Traversing the Rockies, the players make it to California: a sterile, organized, militaristic fascist utopia where the Awakened are a higher class.

    Episode 28: The Rockies

    The Rockies are dangerous, with large avian beasts ruling the skies. The only respite is a small, self-sufficient family who have adapted to survive the harsh ecosystem without being traditionally Awakened. The other side of the range is scorched earth, a buffer zone against the outside world.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Environmental Node: The Rocky Mountains Ecosystem
    • Supporting Nodes: The Mountain Family

    Episode 29: Ingram's Utopia

    The players find sterile, uniform micro-communities where one's DNA is their only value. They discover the citizens are not oppressed; they have chosen and embraced this fascist lifestyle.

    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: California Communities
    • Theme Node: Genetic-Based Fascism

    Episode 30: The First Stone is Cast

    The group is discovered, and the community goes into lockdown. They face a security deployment of "blanks" equipped with powerful technology, treated as slaves. The community's Proctor, a battle-tested Orange Awakened, goes toe-to-toe with the players.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Supporting Nodes: The Community Proctor (Orange Awakened), The Blanks

    Arc 11: The City of San Francisco

    Overview

    Whisper tips the players off to a weakness in Ingram's fortress and the location of Tim. This causes the Coalition to go to war to tie up the army, allowing the players to breach the final barrier to Alcatraz.

    Episode 31: Whispers of Urgency

    With the first contact being violent, war is assured. Whisper provides a glimmer of hope: a crack in Ingram's defenses. A plan is put together for a diversionary war to allow the players to get to Ingram and Tim. Resistance from allies is assuaged by the reality that Ingram will not stop at California.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Supporting Nodes: Whisper, Tim

    Episode 32: War of Genesis

    The Coalition's first wave is to get the attention of Ingram's forces, pulling the fight away from civilians. The second wave is a war of attrition. At the height of the conflict, the players make their move, only to find Silas standing in their way.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC
    • Event Node: The Coalition War
    • Supporting Nodes: Silas

    Episode 33: Breaching the Barrier

    Silas has returned, stronger and smarter, now able to make blood clones of himself. The fight is a small war of its own. With the last pieces of her story put to rest, Whisper is at peace and generates a new body for herself. The energy released from her self-genesis breaks the barrier, allowing the heroes into their final trial.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Supporting Nodes: Silas, Whisper (Gains new body)
    • Objective Node: Ingram's Barrier

    Arc 12: Alcatraz

    Overview

    A psychological test of self is forced on the players, who must reconcile who they are to overcome illusions and finally reach Ingram, now mutated and superpowered by Tim’s DNA and Chimera technology. The conclusion sees the Kindred return to the weave and the heroes given a choice.

    Episode 34: The Mind Prison

    Alcatraz sits silently, Ingram secure behind his barrier. The prison's defenses are psychological: a single person blankets the facility in an illusion designed to pit one's own thoughts, insecurities, and memories against them. After navigating this mental maze, the group confronts and silences this final elite guard, "The Mirror."

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Location Node: Alcatraz
    • Supporting Nodes: The Mirror (Illusionist)

    Episode 35: The Confrontation

    Ingram is not immediately hostile, speaking calmly as if their demise is a grand favor. Tim is alive but restrained in a device similar to the one Sarah's corpse was found in. Ingram activates an object on his chest and is transformed into a peak physical being, as Tim is rendered unconscious.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Node: Ingram / CIC, Chimera
    • Location Node: Alcatraz Lab
    • Supporting Nodes: Ingram (Mutated), Tim, Sarah (Corpse)

    Episode 36: A World Reborn

    Ingram makes his final offer of mercy. The group engages, and an epic fight ensues, leaving Alcatraz in rubble and ending Ingram's reign. A year passes. Humanity has migrated to Dubai, the only untouched place they know of, to build a metropolis over the Orange Wellspring. It is the start of a century of harmony and the return of the Kindred to the weave.

    • Player Node: The Player Coalition
    • Canon Nodes: Ingram's Reign (End), The Kindred (Return to Weave), The Archive, The Orange Wellspring
    • Location Node: Dubai / Metropolis
