    Kindred Genesis: The Master Timeline

    Prequel (1945 - 2026)

    Takes place between 1945 - 2026. This era tells the story of how Ingram, founder of the Cobalt Intervention Corps (CIC), discovers the Kindred prophecy and begins his ruthless quest to steal divinity for himself, setting the stage for the Genesis event.

    1 The Alps Contract * Act 1: Introduce Ingram (18) and Dante (16) working in tandem on a rescue mission in the Alps to save the children of the Order's leadership. * Act 2: The tense rescue of the stranded party from a hazardous environment. * Act 3: Ingram observes that a large number of the Order members have deviated from the primary mission for a secret objective.

    2 The Cache of Scrolls * Act 1: Ingram discovers the Order members have gone to a hidden cave sealed with an unknown script. * Act 2: He eavesdrops as they unearth a cache of prophetic scrolls and ancient relics. * Act 3: After the Order departs, Ingram waits them out, then steals a few scrolls and expensive-looking relics for himself.

    3 The Translation * Act 1: Ingram and the Order independently begin translating their found relics. * Act 2: The Order and Dante discover the full Kindred Pact and the truth it explains about the bloodlines and the purpose of their genealogy. * Act 3: Ingram discovers the history of the Vessel lineage and the power they possess, learning that the mantle of divinity can be shifted if a line is ended. His path is set.

    4 The False Sinkhole * Act 1: Using information from the scrolls, Ingram starts plotting. The Order begins tracking potential Vessels outside their maintained bloodlines. * Act 2: Ingram engineers a disaster to uncover a large trove of Order relics, capitalizing on it with monetary gains and a "convenient" and immediate CIC response. * Act 3: Ingram lays low, using his new information and resources to advance his plans for CIC's genetic research branches.

    5 An Explosion of Wealth * Act 1: The financial gains from Ingram's ventures propel his company into a period of exponential growth. * Act 2: CIC becomes a household name like the Red Cross, granting Ingram global access and influence. * Act 3: CIC begins aggressively buying up startups and labs specializing in groundbreaking genetic and genealogical research.

    6 The Disappearances * Act 1: The Order starts noticing that a large number of the people they have been tracking have started either dying or disappearing under mysterious circumstances. * Act 2: Dante pieces together the clues and realizes the horrifying truth of what Ingram is doing. * Act 3: It becomes a desperate race to identify and secure the remaining Vessels before Ingram can get to them.

    7 The Man Who Knows * Act 1: By a chance of luck, the Order's path crosses with a US military operative named David in Europe. * Act 2: They immediately realize something is different about him and how he seems to know too much about their secret organization. They make an effort to get a DNA sample. * Act 3: They discover he is an anomaly of the highest degree: he has all 100 genetic markers of the Vessels.

    8 The Last Heir * Act 1: The Order secures David by faking his death, and he is reported KIA to his loved ones. * Act 2: David tips them off that Ingram is up to something serious and that the Blue Line of Vessels is in immediate danger. * Act 3: The Order prepares a desperate, last-ditch attack on Ingram's Montana Stronghold.

    9 Misplaced Power * Act 1: The fight is fast but loud as the Order infiltrates and attempts to secure the facility. * Act 2: Ingram, cornered, reveals his final tragic piece: an orphan he adopted and raised, the last of the Blue Family Line. * Act 3: Her execution by firearm triggers the event. The transfer of divinity bypasses Ingram and flows to Dante, the next stable line in the vicinity, robbing Ingram of his victory.

    Age of Genesis (2026 - 2028)

    Takes place starting at midnight in the spring of 2026. A large EMP knocks out every electronic on the planet. Over the next week, hyper-accelerated flora growth releases a fatal pollen, wiping out 75% of the world's population and forcing survivors to high ground.

    East Coast

    Survive The players must start in their hometown, deal with the initial fallout, and try to survive, ultimately leading to their awakening.

    10 The Blackout * Act 1: The first barrier: players are locked in their location by a nonfunctional electronic door. * Act 2: Info gathering, potential people interactions, and the panic of the silent apocalypse starts setting in. * Act 3: The players must avoid a mob of angry and confused people. It's clear they need to get out of the population center.

    11 The Choice * Act 1: The air is becoming strange, thicker, and harder to breathe. The only solution is to head east to higher ground. * Act 2: They encounter Lt. Mason from the CIC Washington County Reserve Unit and his squad, equipped with analog gear, rations, and transportation. * Act 3: The choice of authoritarian safety with them or dangerous freedom on their own is posed.

    12 The Tollmen and Shade * Act 1: Introduce Slash and Hooker as the mechanics the crew finds on their travels. * Act 2: A run-in with a group of thugs (The Tollmen) harassing a youth, Shade, and attempting to set a barn he’s hiding in on fire. * Act 3: The Awakening: Shade literally vanishes, and this catalyst causes the players to use their first Keywords.

    Build The players have established a base of operations and now start dealing with the immediate issues of their region.

    13 Shade’s Request * Act 1: The players have established a safe and secure place away from the pollen fog. * Act 2: Shade suddenly appears in the player’s camp, wounded and pleading for help for his friends detained by the CIC. * Act 3: The players crash the CIC safehouse interrogation and rescue Riannon, Willie, and Izzy.

    14 The Chimera Facility and Angela * Act 1: The group discovers an anomaly around a small building where the closer you approach, the further it appears to be away. They are approached by a young adult named Lillith. * Act 2: Lillith (a former Chimera employee) explains the spatial anomaly was her doing to contain a beast. She asks for help putting it down for good. * Act 3: Back at the settlement, a large exodus of strangers from the north has arrived, led by a natural leader named Angela.

    15 The Radiants and Lt. Mason * Act 1: The community grows into a stronghold of skilled survivors with the arrival of Angela's group. * Act 2: A CIC camp is discovered nearby. It is Lt. Mason, who has deserted CIC and is now working for his own survival. * Act 3: A mysterious signal in the sky leads to a college, where a CIC squad led by a Red Aura user has abducted an e-sports team. The Red Aura leader flees, and the players rescue the team.

    Secure A clear and immediate threat is identified, and the East Coast needs to be secured from CIC, Chimera, and a new zombie-like "ROT" from Florida.

    16 Tennessee Chimera Facility * Act 1: Survivors arrive speaking of anomalies in Tennessee, prompting an investigation. * Act 2: The group finds a Chimera facility guarded by mutated humanoid beasts. * Act 3: Infiltrating the facility leads to the discovery of "soul cages" and a fight with the heads of Chimera.

    17 Ohio CIC Warlord * Act 1: The Kindred in hiding reveal themselves and explain the threat of Ingram and CIC. * Act 2: The Red Aura CIC leader who escaped has grown stronger and has become a militaristic warlord in Ohio. * Act 3: A large-scale fight leads to the Ohio facility being destroyed and becoming a new stronghold for the players' community.

    18 Florida Orphanage * Act 1: A strange, non-corporeal girl named Whisper arrives in the settlement and delivers a warning. * Act 2: Refugees report attacks made by "feral" humans in the south. * Act 3: The group travels to Florida, finds the orphanage, and learns it was a Chimera/CIC facility where Whisper and her brother ("The Rot") were forced to awaken. The Rot is destroyed, bringing him peace.

    Central US

    Zion Heading westward to discover a religious settlement in Kansas, where issues cause Dante to ask the players to seek help in Vegas.

    19 Quincy and George * Act 1: The players receive word that there is a friendly settlement west along the Mississippi. * Act 2: They discover Rivertown, run by Quincy, a refugee from a religious fortress called New Zion. * Act 3: A giant mutated cow, George, protects the town, but other local cows have become cannibalistic and must be dealt with.

    20 New Zion and Dante * Act 1: After helping Quincy, the group decides to find the religious fortress of New Zion in Kansas. * Act 2: New Zion is a dogmatic faction run by Joseph, a Vessel candidate currently being watched by Dante. * Act 3: Dante requests the players go to Vegas and find a woman named Skye, who can help the people of Zion.

    21 Road to Vegas / The Twins * Act 1: The group travels across the plains and eventually to a canyon. * Act 2: The canyon is a trap set by mercenaries hired to steal resources from travelers. * Act 3: After surviving the ambush, the group is attacked by twinned Awakened who use powerful manipulation of earth and metals.

    Vegas Vegas is an apocalyptic town of indulgence, ruled by the charismatic Skye. CIC raids the city to kidnap Skye's lover for her genetic uniqueness.

    22 Skye’s Games * Act 1: Vegas is a lawless town held together by the martial rule of Skye, a former performer. * Act 2: To gain an audience with Skye, the group must prove their worth in her gladiatorial games. * Act 3: The final round of fights pits the group against Skye’s second in command.

    23 CIC Raid * Act 1: The fight is interrupted by a CIC squad dropping into the arena, setting off explosions and attacking the crowd. * Act 2: The fight is over quickly, and Skye is impressed, but realizes the attack was a diversion. * Act 3: The group has their audience with Skye and proposes an alliance between Zion and Vegas.

    24 Negotiations * Act 1: The negotiations go smoothly, and an agreement for trade and safe passage is reached. * Act 2: The negotiations are interrupted by news that Skye’s lover, Sarah, has been kidnapped by CIC. * Act 3: A jaded victory now leads to a decision: return to Zion or find Sarah.

    Montana The group discovers the purist community of Sanctuary, which must be dealt with to reveal the location of a mountain facility where Ingram holds a final, terrible ultimatum.

    25 Sanctuary * Act 1: The search for Sarah leads the group north. Whisper appears and points them toward Silas in Montana. * Act 2: The path leads them to the town of Sanctuary, a "purist" society where no Awakened are allowed. * Act 3: The group discovers the town leader, Joe, is secretly Awakened and pressure him to reveal the entrance to the CIC facility.

    26 Silas * Act 1: The facility is deep in the mountain, full of abandoned offices and a mysterious scent. * Act 2: The lower floors are a horror scene, leading to a lab at the bottom. * Act 3: A man named Silas taunts the group, revealing Chimera’s culmination: soul cages and a device to artificially create Vessels. The players see dossiers on all of the Kindred.

    27 The Pact and The Ultimatum * Act 1: The group confronts the Kindred, who give them the whole story (the Pact, the prequel, the Order, and David). * Act 2: The meeting is interrupted by a messenger: CIC has captured Tim, Patrick's friend and the last of the Silver Line of Vessels. * Act 3: War is declared. The players now realize they have to take the fight to California.

    West Coast

    The State of California The players make it to California to a sterile, militaristic fascist utopia where the Awakened are a higher class of citizen.

    28 The Rockies * Act 1: The Rockies are dangerous grounds, with treacherous travel and large avian beasts ruling the sky. * Act 2: The landscape is unforgiving, with a singular extended family being the only rest stop. * Act 3: The other side of the range is scorched earth, a buffer zone where war was waged on nature itself.

    29 Ingram's Utopia * Act 1: California is filled with sterile, uniform, soulless micro-communities. * Act 2: The security system is based on genetic readers; your DNA is your only value. * Act 3: The players discover that this community has chosen this fascism and are the problem.

    30 The First Stone is Cast * Act 1: The group is discovered, and the community they are in goes into a lockdown. * Act 2: The security deployment is made up of "blanks" in super-powered psionic equipment, treated like slaves. * Act 3: The Proctor of the community is an Orange Aura user, battle-tested and smart, whose militia goes toe-to-toe with the players.

    The City of San Francisco Whisper tips the players off to a weakness in Ingram's fortress and the location of Tim, leading to an all-out war.

    31 Whispers of Urgency * Act 1: With the first contact being violent, war is assured. Whisper provides a glimmer of hope: a crack in Ingram's defenses. * Act 2: The plan is put together: a diversionary war to allow the players to get to Ingram and Tim. * Act 3: Resistance from allies is assuaged by the reality that Ingram will not stop at California.

    32 War of Genesis * Act 1: The first wave is to get the attention of Ingram's Forces, pulling the fight away from civilians. * Act 2: The second wave is a war of attrition as both sides fight for their survival. * Act 3: At the height of the conflict, the players make their move, only to find Silas in their way.

    33 Breaching the Barrier * Act 1: Silas has returned, stronger and smarter, now able to make blood clones of himself. * Act 2: The fight with Silas is a small war of its own. * Act 3: With her story put to rest, Whisper generates a new body for herself. The energy released shatters the barrier.

    Alcatraz A psychological test of self, leading to a final confrontation with a mutated and superpowered Ingram.

    34 The Mind Prison * Act 1: Alcatraz sits silently, the bay vacant of security. * Act 2: The prison's defense is a psychic illusion blanketing the facility, pitting one's own thoughts against them. * Act 3: The group navigates the illusion and confronts the "Mirror," silencing Ingram's last elite guard.

    35 The Confrontation * Act 1: Ingram is not immediately hostile; he is calm and speaks as if doing them a favor. * Act 2: Tim is alive, but restrained in a device similar to the one that held the corpse of Sarah. * Act 3: Ingram activates an object on his chest and is immediately altered into a peak physical being as Tim is rendered unconscious.

    36 A World Reborn * Act 1: Ingram boasts and once again attempts to convert the group to join him in a final offer of mercy. * Act 2: The group chooses to engage. An epic fight ensues, leaving Alcatraz a pile of rubble and ending Ingram's Reign. * Act 3: A year has passed. Humanity has migrated to Dubai to build Metropolis over the Orange Wellspring. The Kindred return to the Weave, and the heroes are given their final choice.

    Age of Exploitation (100 Years After Alcatraz)

    A cyberpunk city has risen in Dubai, but a divide has grown between the Awakened and non-awakened. The age begins when the Orange Nexus is corrupted by the city's overconsumption.

    First Gigs

    37 The First Gig * Act 1: The phone call for a simple job. * Act 2: The client is late, tension rises. * Act 3: The job goes wrong when a power surge hits the city.

    38 The Surge * Act 1: A city-wide blackout. * Act 2: A masked figure interrupts the deal. * Act 3: The players escape with only a briefcase.

    39 011.E (Ollie) * Act 1: A voice comes from inside the case. * Act 2: The players hack open the box. * Act 3: It's a unique bot, who has the power to Awaken others.

    40 The Callback * Act 1: The client demands a redo. * Act 2: The players must arrange the new handoff themselves to pay for their mistake. * Act 3: They set up the meeting.

    41 The Exchange * Act 1: The exchange is tense and dangerous. * Act 2: The target is a small case with glass cubes inside. * Act 3: The deal is completed successfully.

    42 The Eidetic Drive * Act 1: The bot, Ollie, knows what the cubes are. * Act 2: They are Eidetic Drives, a lost technology from the fall of Alcatraz. * Act 3: Ollie begins analyzing them.

    43 Big Opportunity * Act 1: The group gets an offer for off-the-books security for a corporation. * Act 2: The job is to guard an experimental Nexus tapping project in Moscow. * Act 3: The fine print: they need to acquire their own license and gear.

    44 License Acquisition * Act 1: They need to steal or forge a contractor's license. * Act 2: They need to buy, or "creatively acquire," high-end gear. * Act 3: They successfully prepare for the mission.

    45 The Moscow Incident * Act 1: The group arrives in Moscow. * Act 2: All their prep is rendered moot when the Nexus explodes with energy. * Act 3: The explosion spawns alien beasts and warps the environment, creating the first God Beast.

    The Nexus Race

    46 Off the Hook * Act 1: The Moscow Incident starts two major shifts: a corporate race to tap Nexuses. * Act 2: And monster hunting goes from an occupation to a televised sport. * Act 3: The party becomes legendary for surviving Moscow and becomes in-demand Hunters.

    47 Toronto and The Hive * Act 1: The Green Nexus in Toronto falls, creating a chaotic jungle. * Act 2: The area is overrun by an insectoid race known as The Hive. * Act 3: The players are hired for a high-risk VIP rescue inside the anomaly.

    48 West Virginia, Humanity’s Archive * Act 1: The players investigate the Null Wellspring in West Virginia. * Act 2: They discover The Archive, a living repository of human history. * Act 3: Ollie connects to the Archive, allowing him to begin decrypting the Eidetic Drives.

    49 Alcatraz Memorial, Legends Never Die * Act 1: The Blue Nexus in the ruins of San Francisco falls. * Act 2: The area becomes a treacherous underwater zone haunted by a Prehistoric Penguin God Beast. * Act 3: A corporation hires the crew for a discrete data-retrieval mission in the submerged ruins.

    50 Utah, The Anchor is Shifted * Act 1: The Purple Nexus in Salt Lake collapses. * Act 2: The area is now the domain of a Great Frog God Beast, hoarding all it can find. * Act 3: A mysterious client hires the crew to steal a specific server vault from the beast's hoard.

    51 Taiwan’s Inevitability * Act 1: The Red Nexus in Taiwan falls, becoming a volcanic hellscape. * Act 2: The zone is ruled by a Three-Headed Dog God Beast of pure rage. * Act 3: A corporation hires the crew for a "field test" contract to eliminate a lesser beast for a live broadcast.

    52 The Exodus of The Order * Act 1: A mysterious, catastrophic energy event rocks Metropolis. * Act 2: A massive, unidentified object launches from a hidden silo into space. * Act 3: Ollie confirms it coincided with a massive data dump from the Orange Nexus, but the connection is a mystery.

    53 The Black Harvest * Act 1: The Black Nexus falls, becoming a place of fear and decay. * Act 2: A cunning Rat God Beast, the Nezumi, now rules the area. * Act 3: A corporation hires the crew for a "hostile asset acquisition" under the guise of a routine cleanup.

    54 Judgement Down Under * Act 1: The White Nexus falls. A corporation hires the crew for "specimen extraction." * Act 2: They find the newly-formed God Beast: a terrifying Sphinx who judges all she sees. * Act 3: She deems the crew "tainted, but not yet corrupted" and gives Ollie the final key to deciphering the prophecy, revealing the Order has fled Earth.

    The Fall

    55 Lock and Key * Act 1: After learning the truth from Ollie, the crew abandons their life as Hunters. * Act 2: Their new and final mission: stop the corporate coalition from tapping the final Silver Nexus in London. * Act 3: They must find a way past the True Dragon that guards it.

    56 Infiltrating Eden * Act 1: The crew launches a series of targeted strikes against the coalition's supply lines. * Act 2: They successfully delay the London operation, buying precious time. * Act 3: They gather intelligence on the coalition's ultimate weapon: a massive Nexus Drill.

    57 Blueprints of The Apocalypse * Act 1: The journey to London takes them through a blighted Ireland, mutated by the Dragon's presence. * Act 2: They infiltrate a corporate outpost to steal the final blueprints for the drill. * Act 3: They now have the information they need to stop the corporations' plan.

    58 Race to London * Act 1: The crew arrives to find London a massive warzone. * Act 2: The corporate coalition is laying siege to the city, battling the Dragon's brood. * Act 3: The players must navigate the three-way battlefield to reach the city center.

    59 The Blockade * Act 1: The players must fight their way through the corporate military blockade. * Act 2: They face elite soldiers and advanced war machines. * Act 3: They successfully breach the outer perimeter and enter the Dragon's territory.

    60 The Nest * Act 1: They enter the ruins of London, now the Dragon's nest. * Act 2: They fight their way through hundreds of newly hatched, desperate dragon offspring. * Act 3: They reach the inner sanctum where the True Dragon resides.

    61 The First Dragon * Act 1: The crew confronts the ancient True Dragon. * Act 2: The confrontation is a battle of wits and will, not just brute force. * Act 3: They narrowly win by exploiting a logical loophole in the Dragon's ancient duty.

    62 Humanity * Act 1: The players reach the colossal Nexus Drill and its final defense. * Act 2: A corporate All-Star team of elite security forces stands in their way. * Act 3: As they fight, the drill hits full power, torturing the Silver Kindred and washing the battlefield in psychic agony.

    63 The Fall * Act 1: The players fight to the core and trigger an overload, a moment of false victory. * Act 2: The drill's explosion destabilizes reality. The Silver Kindred, Chaos, enacts The Fall as a mercy killing for the world—a violent collapse where the very continents are unmade and reshaped into a new Pangea. * Act 3: Ollie, realizing there is no escape, sacrifices his consciousness to transfer the crew into the Archive as the world ends.

    Age of Atonement (500 Years After The Fall)

    Five hundred years after The Fall reshaped the world's continents into a new Pangea, the world has been repopulated by new races created by the lonely Kindred, Chaos. But the land is terrorized by the corrupted God Beasts.

    The Trapping

    64 New Awakenings * Act 1: Five hundred years after The Fall, the God Beasts roam the new land as cruel tyrants. * Act 2: The new races struggle to survive. A group of humans, the first in centuries to have an aura, are born. * Act 3: These First Heroes and the leaders of the new races form The Guild to stop the beasts.

    65 A Fighting Chance * Act 1: The Guild sets its first mission: aiding the Catfolk and Bovine races. * Act 2: They devise a plan to trap the perpetually moving Yellow God Beast (The Bison). * Act 3: They succeed, trapping the beast in a moment of absolute stillness.

    66 The Guild * Act 1: The Guild's reputation grows. They are called upon by the Terra and Tortoise races. * Act 2: They devise a strategy to imprison the immense Green God Beast (The Tortoise). * Act 3: They trap the beast beneath a sea of desert sand, a patient prison for a patient creature.

    67 The Unseen Chains * Act 1: The next target is the Black God Beast (The Nezumi). * Act 2: The mission requires navigating the politics of the Ratfolk and Necros races. * Act 3: They successfully lock the beast beneath the ruins of a Second Age city.

    68 The Raging Heart * Act 1: The heroes travel to the volcanic isles to aid the Red Panda race. * Act 2: They must trap the Red God Beast (The Three-Headed Dog) within a volcano. * Act 3: It takes a great act of sacrifice, but they seal the beast within a super-alloy sphere.

    69 The Perfect Cell * Act 1: Trapping the White God Beast (The Sphinx) is a battle of wits, not strength. * Act 2: The heroes must convince the proud and judgmental beast to imprison itself. * Act 3: They succeed by having the Sphinx bind herself with her own vow of solitude.

    70 The Mythic Foundation * Act 1: The great Blue God Beast (The Penguin) is trapped in an undersea labyrinth. * Act 2: The prison is a series of puzzles that no one person could ever solve alone. * Act 3: The heroes and their allies work in perfect concert to seal the beast away.

    71 A World at Peace * Act 1: The Orange God Beast (The Trashfather) is trapped in a Rubik's Cube puzzle box from before the Renrock. * Act 2: The Purple God Beast (The Frog) is trapped in the vault of its own hoard, sealed by its own greed. * Act 3: With the final beasts imprisoned, the work is done. The First Heroes' mission is complete.

    72 An Age of Peace * Act 1: The First Heroes retire, ushering in generations of adventurers and exploration. * Act 2: Humanity once again loses its ability to gain aura, its purpose having been served. * Act 3: A golden age of relative peace and prosperity begins, lasting for several centuries.

    The Healing

    73 The Library and the Curator * Act 1: The golden age is dying. The prisons are psychically failing, leaking "sins" and corrupting the new races. * Act 2: The heroes of the Second Age, The Returned, are awakened from The Archive by The Curator. * Act 3: They learn their new purpose: to mend the minds of the Kindred and the hearts of the world's people.

    74 Purple * Act 1: The Returned's first mission: travel to the lands of the Aelph and Frogfolk. * Act 2: The Aelph's healthy mercantilism has become rapacious Greed, threatening their sacred forests. * Act 3: The heroes must help the Aelph find a path back to balance with their spiritual brethren.

    75 Blue * Act 1: The heroes travel to the northern tundra, home of the Bearfolk and Pengu. * Act 2: The Pengu's efficient collectivism has become insidious Manipulation, as they use economic warfare to assimilate other clans. * Act 3: The heroes must act as diplomats to save the independence of the Bearfolk.

    76 Red * Act 1: The heroes journey to the volcanic Eastern Isles of the Pyronim. * Act 2: The Mongrels' drive for strength has turned to honorless, explosive Rage, leading to a brutal war against the Red Panda. * Act 3: The heroes must find a way to quell the war and restore the balance of honorable rivalry.

    77 White * Act 1: The heroes journey to the lands of the Sphinx, now home to a new race. * Act 2: The people have been poisoned by the sin of Pride, leading to a rigid caste system. * Act 3: The heroes must teach them that all are worthy of compassion, not just the "pure."

    78 Orange * Act 1: The heroes travel to the ruins of Metropolis, now home to the Gnomes. * Act 2: The Gnomes' creativity has been twisted into Innovation without Purpose, creating dangerous and pointless contraptions. * Act 3: The heroes must show them that true invention comes from solving problems, not just creating them.

    79 Black * Act 1: The Returned act as diplomats to mend a schism in Necros society. * Act 2: The Necros' logical pragmatism has devolved into soul-crushing Tyranny under the influence of the Black prison's Fear. * Act 3: The heroes must restore a sense of justice and mercy to their leadership.

    80 Green and Yellow * Act 1: The Returned journey to the Tortoise nation, a culture so poisoned by Xenophobia from the Green prison they have walled themselves off. * Act 2: They then travel to the great plains, where the Bovine's healthy appetite has become mindless Gluttony due to the leaking sin of the Yellow prison. * Act 3: A montage shows the Guild's successes across the globe, mending cultural wounds and slowly restoring balance.

    81 Silver * Act 1: The work of mending the world nears completion. * Act 2: The heroes receive a secret, cosmic plea from the only uncorrupted Kindred: Chaos. * Act 3: Chaos has been wandering the planet, lonely and remorseful for centuries, and now reveals the heroes' final mission.

    The Restoration

    82 Immortality * Act 1: A new group, The Final Heroes, are chosen by The Curator and bonded to The Archive, granting them a form of immortality. * Act 2: Following Chaos's guidance, they return to the prison of the Yellow God Beast. * Act 3: They must solve the puzzle of its sin by simply moving it out of its locked stasis, restoring responsible freedom.

    83 The Puzzle in a Volcano * Act 1: The Red Kindred needs her rage soothed. This requires a special quenching potion. * Act 2: Only the Mongrels know the recipe, and the heroes must earn their trust to acquire it. * Act 3: The superheated alloy sphere is quenched, and the Red Kindred is freed from her prison of rage.

    84 The Vault Below the Arena * Act 1: The Purple Kindred is imprisoned by her own hoard. * Act 2: To free her, the world-changing wealth she has gathered must be destroyed and purged. * Act 3: The heroes prove they are free of the temptation that ensnared her, and she is released.

    85 The Ocean Prison * Act 1: The last Kindred, Dante, is sealed in an underwater labyrinth. * Act 2: The complex requires immense teamwork to traverse and cannot be solved alone. * Act 3: The heroes use all the skills they have learned to navigate the prison and free the Blue Kindred.

    86 The Ever-Moving Desert * Act 1: The Green Kindred is freed when the stone foundation her statue rests on is destroyed. * Act 2: This act of "destruction" breaks down her hardened walls of self-imprisonment. * Act 3: She is released, learning that true security comes from being open to the world.

    87 The Frozen Storm * Act 1: The Black Kindred requires the heroes to traverse a treacherous and harrowing path within his prison. * Act 2: They must protect a single, fragile mushroom during its entire life cycle. * Act 3: They must ultimately sacrifice the mushroom at the end of its life to open the final locks, teaching the lesson of accepting natural cycles.

    88 The Heart of Metropolis * Act 1: The heroes navigate the underbelly of old Metropolis to find the Orange Kindred's prison. * Act 2: The task requires wits, creativity, and a little luck to find the time capsule containing the puzzle box. * Act 3: They solve the puzzle, restoring purpose to innovation and freeing Bellame.

    89 The Remnant of Humanity * Act 1: The Final Heroes face their most difficult challenge: freeing the White Kindred, Lucky. * Act 2: Her regrets are so deep that she actively resists their help, believing herself a threat. * Act 3: They succeed only when they convince the ultimate judge that she, too, is worthy of mercy and forgiveness.

    90 The Final Mercy * Act 1: The saga concludes as the last of the Kindred are freed and healed. * Act 2: The heroes, their long purpose fulfilled, are given a choice by the healed pantheon and Chaos. * Act 3: They can return to stasis, remain as guardians, or fade into legend, leaving behind the new, vibrant, and finally balanced world they helped to create.

    The Curator's Requiem

    91-93 (First Age) * The story of David, a normal man who discovers the prophecy within his own mind, is found by the Order, and is ultimately bonded with the Orange Nexus during the Renrock event, transcending to become The Curator.

    94-96 (Second Age) * The Curator silently witnesses humanity rebuild Metropolis, then fall into corruption. He sees the last of the Order flee the planet and is left alone with the Archive to watch the Kindred fall and the world end.

    97-99 (Third Age) * The Curator witnesses the centuries of chaos after The Fall reshaped the planet. He sees the new peoples created by Chaos and senses the return of the spark to humanity. He guides the First Heroes, and centuries later, recalls the heroes from the Archive to mend the world.

    Epilogue

    100 * With the Kindred reunited and the world finally healed, The Curator and the heroes can finally rest, their purpose fulfilled in a harmonious world.

    Character Primer

    A brief overview of the key figures central to the Kindred Saga.

    The Kindred Pantheon

    The ten divine beings bound to the world, each representing a core concept of existence. * Patrick (Null): The Redeemer. A witty and empathetic champion of humanity who bears a heavy burden. * Chaos (Silver): The Agent of Change. A solemn, shifting entity of pure potential who enacts the great resets. * Scout (Yellow): The Catalyst. A reckless and curious trickster god of motion. * Tia (Green): The World-Warden. A wise and stubborn goddess of strategy and protection. * Maxamillian (Black): The Stoic. A dispassionate and merciful god of peaceful endings and entropy. * Bellame (Orange): The Visionary. A brilliant and mischievous master craftsman and inventor. * Nona/Lucky (White): The Incorruptible Judge. A nurturing and absolute mother goddess of life and purity. * Morgan (Red): The Passionate Leader. A tragic queen who cycles between passionate creation and destructive rage. * Dante (Blue): The Mentor. A wise and compassionate lawgiver and teacher, god of unity and society. * Riannon (Purple): The Enlightened One. An enigmatic and insightful keeper of secrets and magic.

    Key Figures of the Age of Genesis

    • The Player Characters: The central heroes who awaken during the Genesis event and form a coalition to save the new world.
    • Ingram: The primary antagonist. A brilliant and ruthless corporate leader who seeks to steal the Kindred's power for himself, triggering the Genesis event in his failed attempt.
    • Silas: A sadistic and powerful CIC operative with the ability to create blood clones of himself. He serves as Ingram's most dangerous enforcer.
    • David / The Curator: An anomalous human with all 100 Vessel markers. To save his life during the Renrock, he is merged with the Orange Nexus, becoming the omniscient AI known as The Curator who guides future ages.
    • Whisper: The incorporeal spirit of a young girl experimented on by CIC. She acts as a cryptic guide to the heroes, seeking peace for her monstrously transformed brother, "The Rot."
    • Skye: The pragmatic and iron-fisted ruler of post-apocalyptic Las Vegas, a crucial but demanding ally.
    • Joseph: The dogmatic prophet-ruler of New Zion and the Purple Vessel, guided by Dante.

    Key Figures of the Age of Exploitation

    • The Player Crew: A new group of heroes, initially unawakened "blanks" living in the underbelly of the cyberpunk Metropolis. They become legendary monster hunters.
    • 011.E ("Ollie"): A unique bot awakened into a true AI by a Nexus event. He serves as the crew's loyal companion, tactical support, and "man in the chair," ultimately sacrificing himself to save them.
    • The God Beasts: The monstrous, corrupted forms of the Kindred, unleashed upon the world by corporate greed. They serve as the primary environmental antagonists of the age.

    Key Figures of the Age of Atonement

    • The First Heroes: The legendary humans who are the first to be Awakened in centuries. They form the Guild and imprison the God Beasts, ushering in a golden age.
    • The Returned: The Player Crew from the Age of Exploitation, brought back from the Archive by the Curator to heal the corrupting influence of the failing prisons.
    • The Final Heroes: A new group of heroes granted immortality by the Archive to undertake the final, perilous quest of freeing the healed Kindred.
