    Episode Guide: 14 - The Chimera Facility and Angela

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Build
    • Timeline Position: Tick 3
    • Core Theme: Discovery and consequence. The players discover the world is not just broken but filled with unnatural horrors left over from the old world. Their growing reputation as a safe haven has the consequence of attracting a flood of new survivors, bringing both strength and new problems.
    • Player State: Players are Level 1 Awakened characters. This episode is a prime opportunity for them to use their new suite of keywords to solve a multi-faceted problem involving exploration, combat, and social negotiation.
    • Episode Goal: Investigate a strange anomaly, neutralize a dangerous pre-Genesis creature, and navigate the complex social challenge of integrating a large group of refugees led by a charismatic new leader.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    This is the technical shorthand for the entire episode's structure, generated using the tools from Appendix I.

    (3)[51]-4|1|3:[Y[L+0/C8/T10]],[Gen:C-L+1-79],[Y[L+0/C12/T10], Gen:C-L+1-81]

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[51]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Sensor (5.1), reflecting the investigation of a strange new phenomenon beyond normal perception.
    • -4|1|3: The three situations are an Exploration, a Conflict, and an Interaction.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Shimmering Air

    Narrative: With the immediate threat of the Pollen Fog gone, the need for supplies drives the players to explore further from their base. They discover a mystery that hints at the unnatural secrets the old world was hiding.

    Scene 1: The Supply Run (The Hook)

    • The Hook: The players' community is growing (either their independent settlement or their role within the CIC camp). A specific, complex supply is needed: medical equipment, advanced mechanical parts, or scientific tools. Scouts have reported a small, isolated research building a few miles away that might have what they need.
    • The Transition: As they approach the location, they notice something is wrong. The air around the building seems to shimmer, like a heat haze on a cold day. Sounds are muffled, and there's a low, almost sub-audible hum. It feels unnatural.
    • The Decision: This "anomaly" is clearly not normal. Do they proceed with caution, try to analyze it from a distance, or back away entirely?

    Scene 2: Piercing the Veil (The Anomaly)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C8/T10]] - A Level 0 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 8 and a Target Number of 10.
    • Running the Scene: The anomaly is a localized field of distorted energy. This is a Puzzle Clock representing "Understanding the Anomaly."
      • Mechanic: Players must make Effort Rolls to figure out how to bypass the field. Throwing a rock at it shows it bounce off with a dull thud. This is not a physical wall. Wit or Technique rolls could be used to analyze the field's properties. A creative use of a new Keyword (like Sensor or Aware) might grant Advantage or automatically deplete part of the clock.
      • Success: As the Clock depletes, they learn how the field works. It's a containment measure, designed to repel living things. They discover a "frequency" or "gap" they can exploit to pass through safely.
      • Entry: Once the clock is at 0, they can step through the shimmering veil into the silent, eerie grounds of the abandoned facility.

    Scene 3: The Abandoned Lab (The Foreboding)

    • The Hook: The players are inside the perimeter. The building is a small, windowless research lab with the "Chimera" corporate logo peeling off the door. The door is ajar.
    • The Transition: The interior is a mess. Papers are scattered, equipment is overturned, and there are signs of a panicked evacuation. A dried, dark substance stains the floors and walls. In the center of the main lab is a large, heavily reinforced containment pen, its door bent outward as if from a tremendous impact from the inside.
    • The Decision: The supplies they need are on the far side of the lab. Do they move quickly and quietly, or do they take time to search for clues about what happened here?

    Act 2: The Beast in the Cage

    Narrative: The players confront the source of the facility's ruin: a bio-engineered monster left to rot. This is a pure test of their new Level 1 combat abilities against a uniquely challenging foe.

    Scene 4, 5, & 6: The Crazed Beast (The Boss Fight)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+1-79] - A Level 1 Character with the Seed Keyword Split (7.9).
    • Running the Scene: As the players move to grab the supplies, a monstrous creature drops from the ceiling vents. It's a horrifying amalgamation of different animals, scarred and enraged.
      • The Chimera: A Level 1 Character. All stats are 2. Resistance: 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Split (7.9).
      • Curator Tactic: This is a memorable boss fight. The Chimera starts as a single, tough enemy. When it takes damage for the first time, use its Split keyword. It divides into two Level 0 Animations (Minions with HP 1, Resistance 3). If the players focus fire and defeat one of the minions, the main creature can use its action to re-absorb the "biomass" and heal, or split again. The puzzle is to control the number of creatures and defeat the main body.
      • Resolution: After a tough fight, the players defeat the beast. As it dies, the shimmering anomaly field outside flickers and dies with it.

    Act 3: The Floodgates Open

    Narrative: The players' victory is immediately followed by a new and more complex problem. They meet an unexpected survivor and return home to find their small, stable community has been turned upside down.

    Scene 7: The Scientist (The Revelation)

    • The Hook: As the anomaly collapses, a woman emerges from a hidden maintenance cellar. This is Lillith, a former Chimera employee. She's gaunt but alive.
    • The Transition: She explains everything. The creature was a bio-weapon prototype. She was the only survivor of its rampage and used the containment field to trap it, hoping it would starve. She thanks the players profusely for ending it. She is a brilliant scientist with a deep, guilty knowledge of Chimera's operations.
    • The Decision: The players have found their supplies and a potential new, highly skilled ally. Do they trust her and bring her back to their base?

    Scene 8 & 9: The Northern Exodus (The New Reality)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C12/T10], Gen:C-L+1-81] - A Level 0 Puzzle Clock of 12 (TN 10), and the new leader, Angela.
    • Running the Scene: The players return home to a shocking sight. Their small, organized base is now the center of a sprawling, makeshift refugee camp. Dozens, perhaps hundreds, of desperate survivors have arrived from the north, drawn by rumors of safety. The situation is chaotic.
      • The Challenge: This is a major Social Clock representing "Integrating the Exodus." It starts at 12. The players must immediately begin making decisions about food rationing, shelter, and maintaining order.
      • Angela: A Level 1 Character. All stats are 2. Resistance 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Charismatic (8.1). She is the de facto leader of the refugees. She is not trying to take over, but she is a natural leader and is already organizing her people. She approaches the players as equals, offering to help but also making it clear her people have needs that must be met.
      • The Goal: The players must work with (or against) Angela to deplete the clock. Success means the refugees are integrated, and the community is now much larger and more capable. Failure could lead to factionalism, resource hoarding, and internal conflict.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players have successfully dealt with a monstrous threat, gained a valuable new ally in Lillith, and navigated a massive social upheaval. Their base is no longer a small outpost; it is now a significant settlement with a complex social structure.
    • Node Creation:
      • The Player Coalition node has massively increased in scale and complexity.
      • The Lillith (Scientist) and Angela (Leader) supporting nodes are now part of the main cast.
      • The Chimera canon node has been formally introduced as a lingering threat.
    • What's Next: The expanded community has more talent but also greater needs. The pressure to secure more resources and establish a formal leadership structure will be immense. This sets the stage for Episode 15: The Radiants and Lt. Mason, where the players' new community may come into conflict or alliance with other organized groups, and they begin to realize they are not the only "special" people in this new world.
