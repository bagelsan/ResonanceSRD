    Episode Guide: 12 - The Tollmen and Shade

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Survive
    • Timeline Position: Tick 1 (concluded) / Tick 2 (begins)
    • Core Theme: Desperation and sacrifice. This episode explores the choices people make when their backs are against the wall, culminating in the moment of violent catharsis that triggers the players' transformation.
    • Player State: Players are Level 0 "Seeds." This is their final, most challenging adventure before their Awakening.
    • Episode Goal: The players must venture out for critical supplies, negotiate with morally grey survivors, and make a dangerous choice to intervene on behalf of a stranger, leading directly to the conflict that pushes them past their mortal limits.

    • (3)[58]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Leverage (5.8), representing the power plays, both social and physical, that define the encounters within.
    • -4|3|1: The three situations are an Exploration, an Interaction, and a Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Scavenger's Road

    Narrative: The reality of long-term survival sets in. The players are safe for now, but safety is fleeting without resources. This act is about the dawning realization that they must risk what they have to get what they need.

    Scene 1: The Empty Pantry (The Motivation)

    • The Hook: The episode opens in the players' base a few days after the events of Episode 11. The immediate crisis is over, but a new, slower one has begun. A critical resource is almost gone (fuel for a generator, clean water, specific food supplies).
    • The Transition: If they are with CIC, Lt. Mason gives them a direct order: "We need a water purifier, and our scouts tagged a possible source at a scrap yard down the valley." If they are alone, they find a map or hear a rumor about the same location.
    • The Decision: The players must leave their sanctuary and venture back into the dangerous world.

    Scene 2: The Wreckage Highway (The Journey)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C6/T9]] - A Level 0 Barrier with a Task Clock of 6 and a Target Number of 9.
    • Running the Scene: The journey to the scrap yard is a challenge. The roads are a graveyard of abandoned cars. This is a Journey Clock.
      • Mechanic: Players make Effort Rolls to navigate the complex and dangerous route. Technique to plot a course, Movement to climb over obstacles, Wit to spot potential ambushes.
      • Curator's Note: Describe the world. The strange, rapid plant growth is more noticeable now. The silence is punctuated by distant, unsettling sounds. Build the atmosphere of a world that is actively changing.

    Scene 3: The Tollmen's Gate (The Arrival)

    • The Hook: The players arrive at a large, fenced-in scrap yard. A crudely painted sign reads "THE TOLLMEN. GOT PARTS? GOT A PASS? GOT A PROBLEM? KEEP OUT."
    • The Transition: As they approach, two figures, a man and a woman covered in grease and armed with heavy wrenches, step out from behind a pile of crushed cars. They are the mechanics, Slash and Hooker. "State your business," the woman says, not unkindly, but not friendly either.
    • The Decision: This is a pure roleplaying scene. How do the players introduce themselves to these wary, but not necessarily hostile, survivors?

    Act 2: A Desperate Bargain

    Narrative: The players must negotiate for what they need, but the transaction is complicated by the arrival of local bullies. This act is a major moral test: do they protect the innocent, or mind their own business to ensure their own survival?

    Scene 4 & 5: The Deal and The Shakedown (The Social Test)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C8/T10], Gen:C-L+0-53, Gen:C-L+0-52] - A Level 0 Puzzle Clock of 8 (TN 10), and the two Tollmen.
    • Running the Scene: This is a two-part social encounter.
      • Part 1: The Deal: The Social Clock represents "Making a Deal." The Tollmen have what the players need, but they won't give it away. They require a steep trade or for the players to perform a dangerous favor. Players must use their social skills (Influence, Wit) to negotiate a fair price.
        • Slash (NPC): Level 0 Character. Seed Keyword: Crafty (5.3). He's the talker and the planner.
        • Hooker (NPC): Level 0 Character. Seed Keyword: Improvisor (5.2). She's the hands-on mechanic who can see the value in everything.
      • Part 2: The Shakedown: Just as a deal is being struck, a new group arrives: three brutish-looking thugs. They corner a skinny teenager who was also trying to trade. This is Shade. The lead thug shoves him. "You owe us, kid. Pay up." The Tollmen immediately turn away, busying themselves. "Not our business," Slash mutters.

    Scene 6: The Line in the Sand (The Climax of the Act)

    • The Hook: The thugs are becoming more aggressive with Shade, who is terrified. The Tollmen are pointedly ignoring the situation.
    • The Transition: The lead thug grabs Shade's bag and dumps its contents—a few cans of food and a worn-out comic book—onto the ground. "This is it? Pathetic." He raises a hand to strike the boy.
    • The Decision: This is the critical moment. The players have a clear window to walk away with their deal secured, leaving Shade to his fate. Or they can intervene, risking everything for a complete stranger.

    Act 3: The Spark of Defiance

    Narrative: The players' choice to intervene explodes into a desperate fight for survival. Pushed beyond their limits, they experience a traumatic, violent transformation, unlocking the power that has been dormant within them.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Junkyard Brawl (The Awakening)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+0-32, Gen:A-L-1-38, Gen:A-L-1-38] - One Level 0 Character (The Bully) and two Level -1 Minions (The Goons).
    • Running the Scene: The moment the players step in, the fight begins.
      • The Bully: A Level 0 Character. Resistance 3. HP 10. Seed Keyword: Brawler (3.2). He's tough and knows how to use his fists.
      • The Goons: Two Level -1 Animations, designated as Minions (HP 1). Resistance 2. Seed Keyword: Impact (3.8). They aren't skilled, but they hit hard and will try to shove players into dangerous piles of scrap metal.
      • The Awakening Trigger: This fight should be hard. The players are outmatched. When a player is at their lowest point—down to their last few HP, or watching an ally get knocked down—the Curator should trigger their Awakening. Describe it as a sudden, instinctual flash of power.
        • Example: "As the goon shoves you back towards a pile of rusted rebar, a surge of adrenaline hits you. But it's more than that. You feel a strange energy well up, and without thinking, you move. You don't just dodge, you flow around him with impossible grace (Acrobatic). The world slows down, and for the first time, you see the 'source code' of the fight."
      • The Resolution: The Awakening should turn the tide. The players, now using their new powers instinctively, defeat the thugs.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The thugs are beaten and flee. The Tollmen are shocked and a little scared of what they just saw. They quickly give the players what they need and urge them to leave. Shade, wide-eyed and terrified by the display of power, stammers his thanks and then slips away during the confusion.
    • The Awakening: The players are now officially Level 1. They feel fundamentally different. This is a moment for them to process what just happened to them.
    • What's Next: This episode provides the hook for the entire "Build" arc. The players now have their powers, and Shade's disappearance creates an immediate mystery and a future ally. This leads directly into Episode 13: Shade's Request, where the consequences of their heroism arrive on their doorstep, pulling them into a larger conflict and forcing them to use their newfound abilities to protect others.
