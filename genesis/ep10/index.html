    Episode Guide: 10 - The Blackout

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Survive
    • Timeline Position: Tick 1
    • Core Theme: The fragility of modern society and the birth of desperate survival.
    • Player State: Players begin this episode as Level 0 "Seeds," playing as themselves or characters based on themselves. They do not have access to their full Awakened powers yet, only their single Seed Keyword.
    • Episode Goal: The players must survive the first 24 hours of the Genesis event, secure a means of escape from their collapsing population center, and understand the absolute necessity of leaving.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    This is the technical shorthand for the entire episode's structure, generated using the tools from Appendix I.

    (3)[03]-2|3|1:[X[L+0/C5/T9]],[Y[L+0/C8/T8]],[Gen:C-L+0-32, Gen:A-L-1-18, Gen:A-L-1-18]

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[03]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Alert (0.3), representing the constant, low-level panic and heightened senses of a world gone quiet.
    • -2|3|1: The three situations are an Obstacle, an Interaction, and a Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Sudden Silence

    Narrative: Establish the players' ordinary lives and then shatter that normalcy with the Genesis Event. The immediate goal is survival and overcoming the first, most immediate obstacle: their own home.

    Scene 1: The Familiar Room (The Hook)

    • The Hook: The players are gathered in a location of their choosing on a normal evening. Without warning, every light and electronic device dies with a single, sharp click. The ambient hum of the city vanishes. All phones are inert bricks. The world is plunged into an unnerving, profound silence and darkness.
    • The Transition: The initial shock gives way to the immediate problem. The primary exit is a modern door with a magnetic lock, now completely seized without power. They are trapped.
    • The Decision: How do the players react to being confined? Their first choice leads directly into the episode's first mechanical challenge.

    Scene 2: The Locked Door (The Challenge)

    • Situation Type: Obstacle (Code 2)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C5/T9]] - A Level 0 Barrier with a Task Clock of 5 and a Target Number of 9.
    • Running the Scene: The jammed mag-lock is a physical Barrier. Players must make Effort Rolls (SR + Stat) to deplete its Clock.
      • Curator's Note: This is the tutorial. Prompt the players on how they are trying to open the door. "Are you putting your shoulder into it? That's a Brawn roll. Are you trying to find a tool to pry the mechanism? That sounds like Technique."
      • Success: The door groans open, leading into the uncertain world beyond.
      • Failure: Each failed roll should have a narrative consequence. A loud bang from a failed Brawn roll might attract unwanted attention; a slip with a tool could cause a minor injury (a narrative penalty, like needing to find a bandage).

    Scene 3: The Echoing Hall (The Consequence)

    • The Hook: The players step out into the hallway of their building or onto their street. The scene is one of quiet, anxious chaos. A few neighbors are milling about, whispering. The only light is from the moon.
    • The Transition: From somewhere nearby, a sharp scream or a sudden, panicked shout pierces the quiet. The reality that this is not a normal blackout begins to sink in.
    • The Decision: Do they investigate the sound, try to organize their immediate neighbors, or focus solely on their own escape? Their choice sets their immediate goal for the next act.

    Act 2: The Anxious Street

    Narrative: The players are forced out into the wider world, where the true scale of the event becomes apparent. The primary threat is not a monster, but the rising tide of human panic.

    Scene 4: The Crowd (The Social Clock)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C8/T8]] - A Level 0 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 8 and a Target Number of 8.
    • Running the Scene: The street is clogged with stalled cars and small, tense groups of people. Getting through requires navigating the social chaos. This is represented by a Social Clock for "Rising Panic."
      • Mechanic: Players make Effort Rolls using social or mental stats (Influence, Wit, Technique) to calm people, provide clear direction, or create distractions. Success depletes the Clock.
      • The Twist: Overtly aggressive actions (shouting, brandishing a weapon, using Brawn to shove through) are made against the same TN, but a "success" adds points to the Panic Clock, making the situation worse.
      • Curator's Note: Introduce a specific family or individual as the focus. A father trying to get medicine for his sick child, for example. This makes the social puzzle more personal and less abstract.

    Scene 5: The Glimmer of Hope (The Objective)

    • The Hook: In the distance, the players spot something crucial for their escape: a working emergency vehicle, a hardware store that might have supplies, or a defensible location like a brick schoolhouse.
    • The Transition: As they make their way toward it, they realize they are not the only ones who have noticed it. Another, more desperate-looking group is converging on the same location.
    • The Decision: Do they race the other group, try to intimidate them, or attempt to cooperate?

    Scene 6: The Confrontation (The Climax of the Act)

    • The Hook: The players arrive at their objective at the same time as the other group. There are limited resources (e.g., only enough gas in the vehicle for one group, only one secure room in the school).
    • The Transition: The leader of the other group steps forward. They are not overtly hostile, but they are desperate. "We need this," they say, gesturing to their own small, scared group. "We're not asking."
    • The Decision: This is a final social check or a prelude to conflict. Can the players negotiate a solution, or will this confrontation turn violent?

    Act 3: The Crossroads

    Narrative: The immediate survival loop of the first day culminates in a violent clash that solidifies the need to escape the city entirely. This is the final test of their "Seed" state.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Last Resource (The Fight)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+0-32, Gen:A-L-1-18, Gen:A-L-1-18] - One Level 0 Character (Brawler) and two Level 0 Minions (Brutal).
    • Running the Scene: Negotiation has failed. The desperate survivors are now antagonists.
      • The Leader (The Brawler): A Level 0 Character. All stats are 1. Resistance: 3. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Brawler (3.2) - They can use Maneuver actions without spending SP, making them surprisingly good at grappling and shoving. Give them a simple weapon like a tire iron.
      • The Followers (The Brutes): Two Level 0 Animations, designated as Minions. All stats are 1. Resistance: 3. Max HP: 1 (defeated on any successful hit). Seed Keyword: Brutal (1.8) - If they happen to roll a double, they can choose two bonus critical effects, making them unexpectedly dangerous.
      • The Goal: This fight should not be to the death. The goal is to drive the other group off or incapacitate them to secure the final resource needed for escape (the vehicle, the fortified room, etc.). Once the Leader is Downed (HP at 0), the others will flee.
      • Curator's Note: Emphasize the desperation of the opponents. They are not evil villains; they are just people who have been pushed to their limit by the same event the players are experiencing. This makes the conflict tragic, not heroic.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players have survived the first 24 hours, faced the harsh reality of the new world, and secured a means of escape. They should have a clear, immediate goal: get to higher ground, away from the chaos.
    • What's Next: This episode transitions directly into Episode 11: The Choice. As the players begin their journey out of the city, they will see the air growing thick with the strange pollen, and they will encounter Lt. Mason and his CIC squad on the road, presenting them with their first major ideological choice of the campaign. The players have survived, but their true journey is just beginning.
