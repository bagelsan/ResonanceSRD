    Episode Guide: 13 - Shade’s Request

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Build
    • Timeline Position: Tick 2
    • Core Theme: The first test of heroism. With their new powers, the players must choose to risk their hard-won security to save others, solidifying their role as protectors in the new world.
    • Player State: This is the first full episode where players are Level 1 Awakened characters. They have their Seed Keyword plus three new keywords, and all their stats are at a baseline of 2. This adventure is designed to let them explore their new abilities.
    • Episode Goal: The players must gather intelligence from a wounded Shade, locate a hostile safehouse, and rescue his three captured friends before their interrogators can extract critical information.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[82]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Leader (8.2), reflecting the players stepping up to a leadership/protector role and Shade's desperate attempt to lead his own cell.
    • -3|4|1: The three situations are an Interaction, an Exploration, and a Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: A Friend in Need

    Narrative: The fragile peace of the players' new base is shattered by a desperate plea. The act is about moving from a reactive survival mindset to a proactive, heroic one.

    Scene 1: The Ghost at the Gate (The Hook)

    • The Hook: The episode opens in the players' established base (either the CIC camp or their independent shelter). They are tending to routine tasks when a sentry sounds an alarm. A lone, bloody figure is stumbling towards them—it's Shade, the teen they encountered in Episode 12. He collapses as soon as he reaches them.
    • The Transition: He's wounded, delirious with pain and fear. He mutters about his friends, "The Vultures," and a "safehouse." The information is disjointed.
    • The Decision: This is the call to action. Do the players commit to helping this relative stranger, potentially drawing the attention of a hostile new faction?

    Scene 2: The Whispered Intel (The Social Puzzle)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C8/T9]] - A Level 0 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 8 and a Target Number of 9.
    • Running the Scene: The players need to calm Shade and get actionable intelligence. This is a Social Clock representing "Stabilizing Shade."
      • Mechanic: Players make Effort Rolls using relevant stats. Technique or Wit could be used for first aid, calming him down enough to speak clearly. Influence or Insightful keywords could be used to build trust and cut through his panic.
      • Success: Each success depletes the Clock. As it depletes, the Curator should reveal one piece of information:
        • (7-8) The names of his friends: Riannon, Willie, and Izzy.
        • (4-6) The name of the gang: The Vultures.
        • (1-3) The location: An old, fortified farmhouse a few miles away.
        • (0) The urgency: "They were interrogating Riannon... she knows where our other caches are."
      • Failure: A failed roll might cause Shade to pass out for a short time, or become more agitated, costing them precious time (narrative consequence).

    Scene 3: The Call to Arms (The Plan)

    • The Hook: The players have the information they need. Shade is being tended to, but his friends are in imminent danger.
    • The Transition: This is a planning scene. The players must use the information they gathered to form a simple infiltration and rescue plan.
    • The Decision: How do they approach the farmhouse? A stealthy night raid? A direct daytime assault? A clever ruse? Their plan will directly inform their approach in the next act.

    Act 2: The Vulture's Nest

    Narrative: The players put their plan into action. This act is about the tension of the approach and overcoming the Vultures' outer defenses, giving players a chance to use their new utility and movement powers.

    Scene 4: The Perimeter (The Approach)

    • The Hook: The players arrive within sight of the farmhouse. It's a two-story building with boarded-up windows and a hastily constructed scrap-metal fence.
    • The Transition: They spot movement. Sentries are patrolling the perimeter. A direct approach is impossible without being seen.
    • The Decision: How do they bypass the outer defenses?

    Scene 5: Breaching the Coop (The Exploration Challenge)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C8/T10], Gen:A-L-1-22, Gen:A-L-1-22] - A Level 0 Barrier Clock of 8 (TN 10), and two Level 0 Minion Sentries.
    • Running the Scene: This scene is a mix of a physical obstacle and a stealth challenge.
      • The Sentries: Two Level 0 Animations, designated as Minions (HP 1). Resistance 3. Seed Keyword: Sentry (2.2). Their job is to be alert. The TN to Hide from them is their SR + Level + Wit (a roll of d10+1). If the players are spotted, the Sentries will shout, potentially making the final fight harder (e.g., the enemies inside get Advantage on their first turn).
      • The Perimeter Fence: The X[L+0/C8/T10] is a Barrier Clock. Players must find a way over, under, or through it. This is a great place for creative use of new keywords (Traveler (Teleport/Fly), Acrobatic, Crafty, etc.). Direct, noisy solutions might alert the sentries.

    Scene 6: Inside the Walls (The Final Approach)

    • The Hook: The players have breached the perimeter. They are now at the farmhouse itself.
    • The Transition: They hear muffled voices from inside—a calm, menacing interrogator and the pained cries of one of Shade's friends. The interrogation is in progress.
    • The Decision: Which entry point do they use? The front door (loud), a window (stealthy but difficult), or the cellar doors (unpredictable)? The clock is ticking.

    Act 3: Crash the Party

    Narrative: The climax. The players burst in on the interrogation, facing the leader of the Vultures in a direct confrontation. This is a test of their new combat prowess.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Interrogation (The Final Conflict)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+0-68, Gen:A-L-1-32, Gen:A-L-1-32] - One Level 0 Character (The Interrogator) and two Level 0 Animations (The Thugs).
    • Running the Scene: The players kick in the door to a room where the Vulture leader is menacing Riannon, who is tied to a chair. Willie and Izzy are bound in a corner.
      • "Scab," The Interrogator: A Level 1 Character. All stats are 2. Resistance 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Exploit (6.8). He is ruthless and tactical.
        • Curator Tactic: Scab can use his Exploit keyword. If any of the captured friends have a Status Tag (e.g., from being hit), he can remove the tag to increase his EV against a player, representing him using their pain as a psychological weapon.
      • The Vulture Thugs: Two Level 0 Animations. All stats are 1. Resistance 3. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Brawler (3.2). They will try to tie up the players with Shove and Restrain maneuvers.
      • The Goal: The primary objective is to defeat the Vultures and free the prisoners. Defeating "Scab" will cause any remaining Thugs to surrender or flee.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players have successfully rescued Riannon, Willie, and Izzy. They have neutralized a local threat (or at least its leadership). They return to their base not just as survivors, but as heroes who have expanded their community.
    • Node Creation:
      • The Player Coalition node has grown, gaining three new skilled members.
      • A new supporting node, Shade's Cell, is now formally integrated with the players.
      • A new antagonist node, the Vulture Remnants, can be created for future plot hooks.
    • What's Next: This episode firmly establishes the "Build" arc. The players now have a larger group with more skills and more mouths to feed. Their next challenge will likely be logistical, leading into Episode 14: The Chimera Facility and Angela, where the need for more supplies sends them exploring deeper into the strange, new world, uncovering one of Ingram's secret projects.
