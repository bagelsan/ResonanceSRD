    Episode Guide: 16 - Tennessee Chimera Facility

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Secure
    • Timeline Position: Tick 5
    • Core Theme: Uncovering the truth. The players move from defending their home to actively hunting the monsters of the old world, discovering the truly horrific nature of Chimera's research.
    • Player State: Players are Level 1 (or possibly Level 2 by now). This is a classic "dungeon crawl" style adventure, testing their infiltration, exploration, and combat skills against a series of unnatural threats.
    • Episode Goal: The players must investigate rumors from traumatized survivors, infiltrate a fortified Chimera black site, fight their way past bio-engineered guards, and confront the facility's leadership to shut down their horrific experiments.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    This is the technical shorthand for the entire episode's structure, generated using the tools from Appendix I.

    (3)[47]-3|4|1:[Y[L+1/C10/T10]],[X[L+1/C10/T12], Gen:A-L+0-38, Gen:A-L+0-38],[Gen:C-L+1-49, Gen:C-L+1-48]

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[47]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Flux (4.7), representing the unnatural manipulation of life force and essence at the heart of the facility's "soul cage" experiments.
    • -3|4|1: The three situations are an Interaction, an Exploration, and a Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: Whispers of a Nightmare

    Narrative: The players' stronghold is now a beacon of hope, but that hope is shadowed by the terrifying stories brought by new arrivals. They are compelled to investigate a new, more sinister threat.

    Scene 1: The Survivor's Tale (The Hook)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+1/C10/T10]] - A Level 1 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 10 and a Target Number of 10.
    • Running the Scene: A new group of survivors arrives, deeply traumatized. This is a Social Clock for "Piecing Together the Intel."
      • Mechanic: The survivors are panicked and their stories are disjointed. Players must use Effort Rolls (Influence to calm them, Wit to ask targeted questions, Insightful keyword to understand their fear) to deplete the Clock.
      • The Story: As the Clock depletes, the players learn of a Chimera facility in Tennessee where "they put souls in jars." They speak of monstrous guards that look like twisted, hulking men and a place that felt wrong on a fundamental level. They provide a rough location.

    Scene 2 & 3: The Plan and The Journey

    • The Hook: With a clear, if horrifying, lead, the community leadership (including the players, Angela, and Mason) agrees this threat must be investigated. Lillith confirms the facility type sounds like a "Bio-Essence Containment Lab"—highly dangerous.
    • The Transition: The players gear up for a major infiltration mission. The journey to Tennessee is arduous but not the focus; this is about the destination.
    • The Decision: This is a planning scene. Based on the survivors' testimony, what is their infiltration strategy?

    Act 2: The Abomination's Gate

    Narrative: The players arrive at the facility and must bypass its living security system. This act is a tense stealth and combat challenge, a test of tactical coordination.

    Scene 4, 5, & 6: The Hunting Ground (The Infiltration)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [X[L+1/C10/T12], Gen:A-L+0-38, Gen:A-L+0-38] - A Level 1 Barrier Clock of 10 (TN 12) and two Level 0 Guards.
    • Running the Scene: The facility is a brutalist concrete structure surrounded by a perimeter fence and patrolled by horrific creatures.
      • The Guards (Mutated Humanoids): Two Level 0 Animations. Resistance 3, HP 10. Seed Keyword: Impact (3.8). These are not minions. They are hulking brutes that hit like trucks. Their job is to patrol. Players can try to sneak past them (opposed Technique vs. Wit rolls) or fight them.
      • The Defenses: The X[L+1/C10/T12] is a Barrier Clock representing the facility's physical and electronic security systems. The high TN means it requires teamwork. Players might use Technique to hack a keypad, Brawn to force a gate, or a creative Keyword to bypass a wall entirely. Alerting the guards will make this much harder.

    Act 3: The Soul Cages

    Narrative: Inside the lab, the players confront the architects of this horror. This is the climactic boss fight, revealing the dark purpose of Chimera's work and its direct link to Ingram's goals.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Head of the Snake (The Final Conflict)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+1-49, Gen:C-L+1-48] - The two Chimera Head Scientists.
    • Running the Scene: The final lab is filled with racks of glowing cylinders—the soul cages. Two scientists in advanced lab coats are overseeing the process. They are not surprised to see the players; they are arrogant. "New specimens have arrived," one says to the other.
      • Dr. Aris (The Controller): Level 1 Character. Resistance 6, HP 10. Seed Keyword: Animate (4.9). He will use his action to animate lab equipment or spare biomass into makeshift minions (Level -1, HP 1) to protect himself.
      • Dr. Bell (The Thief): Level 1 Character. Resistance 6, HP 10. Seed Keyword: Siphon (4.8). Her attacks drain the players' SP or EP, which she can use to power the lab's defenses or heal Dr. Aris.
      • The Soul Cages: These are an environmental factor. A stray shot that hits one could cause it to explode, releasing a chaotic psychic scream (giving everyone a Daze Tag) or a helpful spirit (granting a random player a Bless Tag).

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The scientists are defeated. The players have the choice to destroy the soul cages or, with Lillith's guidance, attempt to safely release the contained essences. They recover research data that explicitly links Chimera's work to Ingram's quest for artificial Vessel creation.
    • What's Next: The players have cut off a major source of Chimera's horrific research. This victory, combined with the intel they've gathered, points them to the next logical target: the military arm of the operation. The Kindred within their community now have the proof they need to step forward, leading directly into Episode 17: The Ohio CIC Warlord.

    Episode Guide: 17 - The Ohio CIC Warlord

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Secure
    • Timeline Position: Tick 6
    • Core Theme: Total War. The conflict escalates from covert operations to a full-scale assault. This is the players' first taste of leading an army and taking the fight directly to their primary antagonist.
    • Player State: Players are likely Level 2. This episode is a large-scale set piece designed to make them feel like leaders, culminating in a personal, high-stakes duel.
    • Episode Goal: The players must process the Kindred's revelations, lead their community's forces in an assault on the Ohio CIC fortress, and finally defeat the Warlord who escaped them in Episode 15.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    (3)[22]-3|*2|1:[Gen:C-L+2-91],[X[L+1/C20/T15]],[Gen:C-L+2-62.82, Gen:A-L+1-22, Gen:A-L+1-22]

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[22]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Sentry (2.2), reflecting the heavily fortified, militaristic nature of the Warlord's fortress.
    • -3|*2|1: The three situations are an Interaction, a large-scale Race/Obstacle, and a climactic Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Weight of Truth

    Narrative: With the horrors of Chimera exposed, the hidden Kindred in the community decide the time for secrets is over. The players are finally given the full, world-altering context for their fight.

    Scene 1, 2, & 3: The Council of War (The Revelation)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+2-91] - The Kindred Emissary.
    • Running the Scene: This is a major narrative act. One of the Kindred, a powerful and ancient-feeling individual, calls a meeting with the players and the settlement's other leaders (Angela, Mason).
      • The Lore Dump: The Kindred explains everything: Ingram, the Genesis event's true purpose, the Vessels, the Order, and David's sacrifice. They confirm that the CIC Warlord in Ohio is not just a rogue commander; he is a loyalist building an army for Ingram.
      • The Kindred Emissary: A Level 2+ Character. Use a keyword like Aware (9.1) or Insightful (8.3) to show their deep perception. This is not a combat encounter; they are here to provide truth and a call to action.
      • The Decision: The truth is laid bare. The Ohio facility isn't just a threat; it's the eastern command post for a war against humanity. The council unanimously agrees: it must be destroyed.

    Act 2: The Siege of the Ironclad

    Narrative: The players lead the Coalition's combined forces in a massive assault on the heavily fortified CIC base. This is a cinematic battle sequence.

    Scene 4, 5, & 6: The Gates of Hell (The Grand Assault)

    • Situation Type: Race/Obstacle (Code *2)
    • Manifest: [X[L+1/C20/T15]] - A Level 1 Barrier with a massive Task Clock of 20 and a high TN of 15.
    • Running the Scene: This is a Race Situation representing the entire siege. The Threat Clock (starting at 30) represents the Warlord preparing a devastating counter-offensive.
      • The Siege Clock: This massive Barrier Clock represents the fortress's defenses. It's too big to be taken down with simple rolls. The Curator should break it into three stages of 6-7 points each:
        1. Breach the Outer Wall: Players might lead a charge, use salvaged explosives, or find a weak point.
        2. Disable the Automated Turrets: Players must fight their way to a control tower under heavy fire.
        3. Secure the Command Center Entrance: A final push against entrenched soldiers (use Minion rules for flavor).
      • Leading the Charge: Players' rolls shouldn't just be them acting alone. A successful Influence or Leader roll could represent directing their troops effectively, depleting the clock. A Technique roll could be hacking defenses. Brawn could be leading a brute-force charge.

    Act 3: The Warlord's Last Stand

    Narrative: The players have broken through the defenses and cornered the Warlord in his command center for a final, personal confrontation.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Rematch (The Final Conflict)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+2-62.82, Gen:A-L+1-22, Gen:A-L+1-22] - The Warlord and his elite guard.
    • Running the Scene: The Warlord stands over a holographic map, flanked by his two best soldiers. "I underestimated you at the crash site," he says, drawing his weapon. "A mistake I will not make again."
      • The Warlord: Now a Level 2 Character. Resistance 7+, HP 15. Keywords: Deadeye (6.2) & Leader (8.2). He is a formidable duelist and a commander. He will use the Assist action to grant his guards Advantage and focus fire with his Deadeye ability.
      • Elite Guard: Two Level 1 Animations. Resistance 6, HP 10. Seed Keyword: Sentry (2.2). They are highly trained and will work to protect their commander.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The Warlord is defeated for good. The Ohio facility falls, and its resources, equipment, and strategic location are now under the Coalition's control. This is a massive victory, establishing the players' community as the dominant power on the East Coast.
    • What's Next: The celebration is cut short. As the players are securing the Ohio base, a ghostly figure appears before them—Whisper. She brings a dire warning of a different kind of threat, one not of metal but of flesh and decay, rising from the south. This shocking cliffhanger leads directly into Episode 18: The Florida Orphanage.

    Episode Guide: 18 - The Florida Orphanage

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Secure (Finale)
    • Timeline Position: Tick 7
    • Core Theme: Mercy and Monstrosity. This is a horror-themed adventure that forces players to confront a tragic enemy, blurring the lines between good and evil and ending the arc on a somber, emotional note.
    • Player State: Players are Level 2. This episode will test their resolve and their ability to solve a problem that can't be solved with violence alone.
    • Episode Goal: The players must heed a spirit's warning, travel into a blighted region, investigate a horrifying orphanage, and confront "The Rot"—a tragic monster that must be destroyed to be saved.

    2. Scene Blueprint (System Language)

    (3)[75]-3|4|1:[Y[L+1/C10/T12]],[X[L+1/C12/T12]],[Gen:C-L+3-88, X[L+0/C5/T10], X[L+0/C5/T10]]

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • (3)[75]: A 3-Situation episode with a core theme of Blight (7.5), perfectly capturing the decaying, life-draining horror of the Rot.
    • -3|4|1: The three situations are an Interaction, a horror-themed Exploration, and a final, tragic Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Ghost's Plea

    Narrative: A supernatural messenger brings a terrifying warning, pulling the players away from their war with CIC and into a personal, horror-filled mystery.

    Scene 1, 2, & 3: The Girl Made of Smoke (The Warning)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+1/C10/T12]] - A Level 1 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 10 and a TN of 12.
    • Running the Scene: The disembodied, spirit-like form of Whisper appears before the players in the newly captured Ohio base. This is a Social Clock for "Understanding the Vision."
      • Mechanic: Whisper cannot speak clearly. She communicates through flashes of imagery, feelings of cold dread, and single, whispered words ("Brother... hungry... the Rot... help him..."). Players must make Effort Rolls using Wit, Influence, or perceptive keywords to interpret her message. The high TN represents the difficulty of communicating with a spirit.
      • The Message: They eventually piece it together: a sickness is spreading from an old orphanage in Florida. Her "brother" is the cause, and he needs to be stopped, for his own sake. She gives them a clear vision of the location before fading.

    Act 2: The Blighted South

    Narrative: The journey to Florida is a descent into a horror landscape. The world here is not just overgrown; it is actively sick and predatory.

    Scene 4, 5, & 6: Land of the Feral (The Journey)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [X[L+1/C12/T12]] - A Level 1 Barrier with a Task Clock of 12 and a TN of 12.
    • Running the Scene: This is a horror-themed Journey Clock. The landscape is twisted and decayed, and "feral" humans (victims of the Rot) stalk the players.
      • The Hazard: The environment itself is hostile. The air is thick with spores. Failed rolls on the Journey Clock should have consequences: gaining a temporary Blight Tag, losing supplies to rot, or stumbling into an ambush.
      • The Feral: These are not standard enemies. They are fast, aggressive, and hunt in packs. The Curator should use them for jump scares and tense chase scenes rather than a pitched battle. A failed roll on the Journey Clock might trigger an encounter with a small pack of Level 0 Minions (HP 1) with the Blight (7.5) keyword.

    Act 3: The Brother

    Narrative: The players find the source of the decay: a decaying orphanage that has become a monstrous nest. They confront The Rot and learn the tragic truth.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Heart of the Rot (The Final Conflict)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+3-88, X[L+0/C5/T10], X[L+0/C5/T10]] - The Rot and his "Rot Nests."
    • Running the Scene: The orphanage is the heart of a massive, pulsating biomass. In the center is a monstrous, weeping creature of flesh and decay—The Rot.
      • The Rot: A powerful Level 3 Character. Resistance 8+, HP 20. Seed Keyword: Spread (8.8). He is a tragic boss. He doesn't speak, only cries. His attacks spread the Blight Tag to multiple targets.
      • The Puzzle: The Rot is covered in a thick, regenerating shell of biomass and is immune to all damage. Whisper's spirit appears and gestures frantically towards two pulsating "Rot Nests" (the Barriers) in the room. These nests are pumping energy into The Rot.
      • The Fight: The players must first destroy the two Rot Nests (Barrier Clocks of 5, TN 10). While they do this, The Rot will relentlessly attack them. Once both nests are destroyed, The Rot's shell cracks, he becomes vulnerable, and the players can finally damage him.
      • The Mercy: When defeated, The Rot's monstrous form dissolves, revealing the small, spectral form of a boy who whispers "Thank you" before fading. Whisper appears one last time, whole and at peace, and thanks the players for freeing her brother.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players have ended a major supernatural threat and brought peace to two tormented souls. They have proven they can handle not just the soldiers of the old world but the monsters of the new one. They have truly secured the East Coast.
    • What's Next: With the East secured, their reputation has grown. Word of their deeds travels. A message arrives from a new, unexpected source: a man named Quincy, leader of a settlement called Rivertown, located far to the west along the Mississippi. He has heard of their strength and needs their help, promising a new alliance and opening the door to the next major chapter of the campaign: the journey into the Central US. This concludes the first season and sets the stage for Episode 19: Quincy and George.
