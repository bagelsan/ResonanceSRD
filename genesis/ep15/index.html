    Episode Guide: 15 - The Radiants and Lt. Mason

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Build (Finale)
    • Timeline Position: Tick 4
    • Core Theme: Alliance and Opposition. As the players' community grows into a real stronghold, they must forge new alliances while facing the first organized opposition from a hostile remnant of the old world.
    • Player State: Players are Level 1 Awakened characters. This episode tests their ability to deal with complex social situations and their first true tactical challenge against a trained, hostile squad.
    • Episode Goal: The players must make contact with Lt. Mason's splinter group, investigate a mysterious signal, rescue a group of uniquely talented individuals from a CIC black site, and survive their first direct confrontation with Ingram's loyalist forces.

    Blueprint Breakdown:

    • -4|4|1: The three situations are an Exploration, another Exploration, and a final Conflict.
    • :[Manifests]: The specific challenges for each situation are detailed below.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: Ghosts of the Old World

    Narrative: The players' growing community solidifies, but this new stability attracts attention. A ghost from their past reappears, forcing a difficult decision about who to trust.

    Scene 1: The Stronghold (The New Normal)

    • The Hook: The episode opens with the players' community thriving. Under Angela's social leadership and with Lillith's scientific knowledge, the base is becoming a true stronghold. The players are now established leaders.
    • The Transition: A scouting party returns with unsettling news. They've discovered a small, professional military encampment a few miles away, flying no banners. The tracks and equipment, however, are unmistakably CIC.
    • The Decision: Is this a threat? A remnant? Or something else? The players must decide how to approach this potential powder keg.

    Scene 2: The Splinter Camp (The Tense Meeting)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C8/T10], Gen:C-L+1-82] - A Level 0 Puzzle with a Task Clock of 8 (TN 10), and Lt. Mason.
    • Running the Scene: The players approach the camp. This is a Social Clock representing "Establishing Trust."
      • Mechanic: A direct or aggressive approach will be met with armed defensiveness. Players must use stealth (Technique) or diplomacy (Influence) to make peaceful contact. Each success on an Effort Roll depletes the clock, getting them closer to a face-to-face meeting with the camp's leader.
      • The Reveal: The leader is Lt. Mason. He explains that after the Genesis event, CIC command went dark, then came back online with horrifying new orders: secure assets, liquidate non-essential personnel, and hunt "anomalous individuals." He and his squad refused, denounced CIC, and went rogue. He offers his skills and knowledge to the settlement in exchange for sanctuary.
      • Lt. Mason: A Level 1 Character. Resistance 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Leader (8.2). He is an asset, but his presence officially makes the community an enemy of whatever CIC has become.

    Scene 3: The Ghost Signal (The New Mystery)

    • The Hook: Just as the players are negotiating with Mason, one of their technicians (or Lillith) picks up a strange, repeating signal on a short-wave frequency. It's weak, coded, and clearly a distress call.
    • The Transition: Mason recognizes the encryption. It's an old CIC emergency beacon, one used for deep cover agents. Someone from the old world is in trouble.
    • The Decision: Do they act on this immediately, risking a potential CIC trap to save an unknown operative? Or do they focus on securing their new alliance with Mason first?

    Act 2: The Rescue

    Narrative: Following the signal, the players are drawn into a race against time. They must locate the source of the distress call and uncover what this new, more sinister CIC is hunting.

    Scene 4: The Hunt (The Search)

    • Situation Type: Exploration (Code 4)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C6/T9], Gen:A-L-1-03, Gen:A-L-1-03] - A Level 0 Barrier Clock of 6 (TN 9), and two Level -1 competing scavengers.
    • Running the Scene: This is a Journey Clock representing the search for the signal's source.
      • Mechanic: Players use Technique to triangulate the signal, Wit to navigate, and Movement to make good time.
      • The Complication: The players aren't the only ones who noticed the strange energy signature. Two Scavenger Drones or mutated creatures are also drawn to the site, creating a minor race or obstacle.
      • Scavengers: Two Level -1 Animations, Minions (HP 1). Resistance 2. Seed Keyword: Alert (0.3). They are not a major threat but can slow the players down.

    Scene 5 & 6: The Black Site (The Discovery)

    • The Hook: The signal leads them to a crashed, armored CIC transport vehicle hidden in a ravine. The distress beacon is active, but there are signs of a struggle.
    • The Transition: Inside, they find several young adults, all wearing matching, flashy esports team jerseys ("The Radiants"). They are terrified. One of them explains they were ambushed and abducted by a CIC "Hunter" squad. They are not agents; they were just a group of friends who all started manifesting strange abilities after the Genesis event.
    • The Climax: Before they can get everyone out, the CIC Hunter squad returns. "Asset reclamation in progress," a cold voice crackles over a loudspeaker. "Surrender the anomalies."

    Act 3: The Hunter Hunted

    Narrative: The players engage in their first real battle as heroes, fighting a trained CIC squad to protect innocent lives. This conflict establishes the primary antagonist for the next arc and solidifies the players' role as defenders.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: First Strike (The Final Conflict)

    • Situation Type: Conflict (Code 1)
    • Manifest: [Gen:C-L+1-62, Gen:A-L+0-22, Gen:A-L+0-22] - The CIC Squad Leader and two CIC Soldiers.
    • Running the Scene: The fight begins. The players must protect the Radiants while fighting off the CIC squad.
      • CIC Squad Leader: A Level 1 Character. Resistance 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Deadeye (6.2). He is a ruthless professional trained to identify and eliminate high-value targets. He will use Called Shots and focus fire on the most dangerous-looking player.
      • CIC Soldiers: Two Level 0 Animations. Resistance 3. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Sentry (2.2). They will provide cover fire and try to pin the players down.
      • Curator Tactic: The leader is the villain for the next arc. He should not die here. When he is reduced to low HP or his soldiers are defeated, he should use a pre-planned escape route (a grapple line, a flashbang, etc.), leaving with a parting threat: "Target profiles acquired. You are now designated hostile."

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players defeat the squad, but the leader escapes. They have rescued the Radiants, a group of newly Awakened individuals who now need protection and training. They return to their base with new allies, new responsibilities, and a powerful, named enemy.
    • Node Creation:
      • The Player Coalition now includes Mason's Splinter Group and The Radiants, making it a major regional power.
      • A new recurring antagonist node, the CIC Hunter Squad Leader, has been established.
      • The Ingram / CIC canon node is now confirmed as an active, hostile force.
    • What's Next: This episode concludes the "Build" arc and launches the "Secure" arc. The players' home is no longer a simple shelter; it's a target. The escaped CIC leader will report their location and strength. The players must now shift from building their community to actively defending it and taking the fight to CIC, starting with the investigation into the Tennessee Chimera Facility in Episode 16, a lead likely provided by their new, knowledgeable allies, Lillith and Mason.
