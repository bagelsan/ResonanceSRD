    Episode Guide: 11 - The Choice

    1. Episode Overview

    • Season: Age of Genesis
    • Arc: Survive
    • Timeline Position: Tick 1 (continued)
    • Core Theme: Security vs. Freedom. This episode presents the players with their first major ideological choice, forcing them to define what kind of group they will become in this new world.
    • Player State: The players are still Level 0 "Seeds." They have survived their first violent encounter and are now on the run, exhausted and facing a new, insidious environmental threat.
    • Episode Goal: The players must escape the increasingly toxic air of the city, confront a well-equipped and organized faction (CIC), and make a binding choice that will set the course for their immediate future.

    3. Act & Scene Breakdown

    Act 1: The Suffocating Air

    Narrative: The immediate high of survival gives way to a dawning horror. The world isn't just broken; it's becoming actively hostile to life. The goal is to establish the pressure of the Pollen Fog, forcing the players to keep moving.

    Scene 1: A Moment's Peace (The Aftermath)

    • The Hook: The episode opens right after the conflict in Episode 10. The players have their hard-won resource (a vehicle, a secure room). The world is quiet again, save for the distant sounds of chaos.
    • The Transition: They get their first look at the sky. A strange, yellowish-green haze is gathering in the low-lying areas, and the air has a weirdly sweet, metallic taste. It's unsettling.
    • The Decision: This is a narrative scene. The players must decide their immediate next step. Do they rest? Scavenge? Or begin their journey east to the Appalachian mountains immediately?

    Scene 2: The Open Road (The Growing Threat)

    • Situation Type: Race/Obstacle (Code *2)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C10/T10]] - A Level 0 Barrier with a Task Clock of 10 and a TN of 10. This is a Race Situation.
    • Running the Scene: The journey out of the city is the first major challenge. The Pollen Fog is the true antagonist.
      • The Threat Clock: The Curator activates a Threat Clock starting at 20. This represents the "Time Until Suffocation." It ticks down by 1 for every hour of travel.
      • The Journey Clock: The X[L+0/C10/T10] is a Journey Clock representing the difficult trek. Players make Effort Rolls using relevant stats (Technique for navigation, Wit for spotting clear paths, Brawn for clearing minor blockades) to deplete the clock. Each successful roll represents making good time and finding a safer route.
      • Consequences: For every 5 points the Threat Clock loses, the Curator should describe the worsening conditions: breathing becomes labored, visibility drops, they start coughing.

    Scene 3: Shelter from the Storm (A Glimmer of Hope)

    • The Hook: The fog is becoming dangerously thick. The players spot a potential safe haven just off the road—an overpass, a sturdy barn, an abandoned tunnel.
    • The Transition: As they approach, they notice signs of recent passage: fresh tire tracks, a recently extinguished fire. They are not the first people to have this idea.
    • The Decision: Do they enter cautiously, prepared for a confrontation, or announce their presence, hoping for allies?

    Act 2: Order in the Chaos

    Narrative: The players are confronted with a vision of the old world: organized, armed, and offering a solution. This act is a tense social encounter where the players must decide if this offer of safety is worth the price of their freedom.

    Scene 4: First Contact (The Encounter)

    • The Hook: Inside the shelter, they find a small, disciplined squad of soldiers in clean gear, tending to their equipment. These are the men of the CIC Washington County Reserve Unit.
    • The Transition: Their leader, a calm and professional man named Lt. Mason, approaches them. He's not hostile, but he is assessing them. "You're a long way from home," he says. "So are we."
    • The Decision: How do the players respond? Their opening words will set the tone for the most important negotiation of their lives.

    Scene 5: The Offer (The Social Clock)

    • Situation Type: Interaction (Code 3)
    • Manifest: [Y[L+0/C10/T9], Gen:C-L+1-82, Gen:A-L+0-31, Gen:A-L+0-31] - A Level 0 Puzzle Clock of 10 (TN 9), and the CIC squad.
    • Running the Scene: This is a multi-stage social challenge represented by a Social Clock for "Negotiation & Trust." The players must deplete the clock to earn Mason's trust and get a clear picture of his offer.
      • Lt. Mason: A Level 1 Character. All stats are 2. Resistance: 6. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Leader (8.2) - He can command his animations effectively and projects an aura of command.
      • CIC Soldiers: Two Level 0 Animations. All stats are 1. Resistance: 3. HP/SP/EP: 10. Seed Keyword: Sturdy (3.1) - They are disciplined and resilient.
      • The Negotiation: Players make Effort Rolls using Influence (to persuade), Wit (to ask smart questions), or Technique (to demonstrate useful skills). Success chips away at Mason's professional reserve. Failure might make him more suspicious or demanding.

    Scene 6: The Choice (The Climax of the Act)

    • The Hook: The negotiation concludes. Mason is satisfied (or wary) and lays out the choice.
    • The Transition: "We're establishing a secure base in the mountains. We have supplies, order, and a clear chain of command. We're offering you a place. You'll be safe, you'll be fed, but you'll follow our rules. The alternative is... that," he says, gesturing to the toxic fog. "Your skills would be an asset. Our way is the only way to rebuild. So, are you with us?"
    • The Decision: The players must give their answer: Security with CIC, or Freedom on their own.

    Act 3: The Path Chosen

    Narrative: The players immediately experience the consequences of their decision. They face one final obstacle on their way to the temporary safety of the foothills, a challenge defined by the path they have chosen.

    Scene 7, 8, & 9: The Final Hurdle (The Consequence)

    • Situation Type: Obstacle (Code 2)
    • Manifest: [X[L+0/C8/T12]] - A Level 0 Barrier with a Task Clock of 8 and a high Target Number of 12.

    • This scene is a branching path.

    • Path A: Security (If they join CIC):

      • The Challenge: The "final hurdle" is a test of compliance. Mason points to a washed-out bridge that's blocking their convoy. "Our engineering team is handling the main repair," he says. "Your job is to secure the far bank. No questions." The challenge is not physical but a test of their willingness to follow orders. The Clock represents "Earning Your Place." They must use their skills in a way that impresses the CIC soldiers and proves their worth.
      • The Result: They succeed and are formally accepted into the structured, secure CIC encampment. They have supplies and safety, but they are now part of a military machine.

    • Path B: Freedom (If they refuse CIC):

      • The Challenge: Mason nods grimly. "Your choice. Good luck." The players must find their own way. Their path is blocked by the same washed-out bridge, but with no convoy to help. The X[L+0/C8/T12] is now a brutal physical Barrier. They must use their wits and brawn to find a way across the treacherous ravine with a high risk of failure.
      • The Result: They succeed and find their own shelter in the foothills, away from everyone. They are free and independent, but they are also completely alone, with dwindling resources.

    4. Episode Results & Next Steps

    • Resolution: The players have made their foundational choice and reached the relative safety of the Appalachian foothills, ending the immediate threat of the Pollen Fog. They have either integrated into a larger, powerful faction or solidified their identity as independent survivors.
    • What's Next: This episode leads into Episode 12: The Tollmen and Shade. Regardless of their choice, they will need supplies and parts. Their first foray into the new world will lead them to a makeshift scrap-town, where they will encounter opportunistic thugs, a couple of clever mechanics, and a mysterious teenager named Shade, culminating in the conflict that finally triggers their Awakening.
