    Psychromattica: Matrix Dice

    1.0 OBJECTIVE

    The last player with Health Points (HP) remaining is the winner. The game is played until all other players have had their HP reduced from 10 to 0.

    2.0 GAME FORMATS

    • Duel: A two-player, head-to-head match.
    • Arena: A 3-4 player free-for-all.

    3.0 COMPONENTS

    • 1x Agent Board per player.
    • 10x ten-sided dice (d10s): One for each of the 10 system Colors (0-9).
    • Keyword Tokens: For digital play, the token pool is considered infinite.
    • Tag Tokens: For the 10 Boost and 10 Status tags.

    4.0 SETUP

    1. Each player takes an Agent Board.
    2. Set starting values: HP = 10, Focus = 1, Karma = 0.
    3. Archetype Declaration: Each player chooses a Primary Color and two Secondary Colors to form their 3-Color Archetype.
    4. Determine the first player. Play proceeds clockwise.

    5.0 CORE CONCEPTS

    • 5.1 Affinity Matrix: A 3x3 grid for your Keyword Tokens. Each slot has a maximum capacity of 5 tokens. If generating a new Keyword Token would cause a slot to exceed this limit, you must choose and discard one of the existing tokens from that slot to make room for the new one.
    • 5.2 Focus: Determines the complexity of your actions; it's the maximum number of Keyword Tokens you can spend on a single Keyword Activation. The maximum Focus is 5.
    • 5.3 Karma: The primary resource for generating tokens. The maximum Karma is 10.
    • 5.4 Successful Activation:
      • An activation targeting an opponent is "successful" if it is not Avoided and directly causes one or more of the following:
        • The target loses at least 1 HP from the activation's final calculated damage.
        • A Status Tag is successfully applied to the target.
        • A Keyword Token the target controls is discarded, swapped, altered, or removed.
        • The target loses Karma as a direct result of the activation's effect.
      • An activation targeting yourself is automatically successful.
      • Losing resources to pay the cost of a Respond action does not count as a successful outcome for the attacker.

    Damage vs. Life Loss

    • Damage: The reduction of HP that occurs from an activation's resolution. Effects that trigger "when you take damage" only apply to this. Damage can often be Resisted.
    • Life Loss: The reduction of HP from paying costs or from non-activation effects. This is not considered "damage" and cannot be Resisted.

    6.0 THE CORE LOOP: TURN STRUCTURE

    A player's turn is a cycle of four phases following a strict sequence of Declaration -> Response -> Resolution.

    1. Ready (Upkeep Phase): Resolve "start of turn" effects. Your Ready phase then proceeds in this strict order:
      • Gain Resources: Gain Karma equal to your current Focus.
      • Free Generation: Perform one free Keyword Token generation. You must choose your Primary Color for this; roll a d10 to determine the token's category (the result of the die).
      • Paid Generation: You may now spend Karma to generate additional Keyword Tokens by rolling a d10. The costs are:
        • 1 Karma: Choose one of your three Affinity Colors or the Null [0] Color.
        • 2 Karma: Choose any non-Affinity Color.
    2. Activate (Action Phase): Perform one primary action from the list in section 7.0.
    3. Respond (Reaction Phase): Targeted player(s) independently choose to Resist, Avoid, or Do Not React.
    4. Resolve & Reward (Resolution Phase): Effects are finalized. If your activation was successful against an opponent, you gain 1 Karma (plus any combo bonuses). If multiple effects trigger simultaneously, you (the active player) choose the order of resolution.

    7.0 ACTIONS & REACTIONS

    7.1 Activate Actions

    • Basic Activation: Spend no tokens. Deals 1 damage. This can be enhanced with a Karma Surge by spending 2 Karma for +1 damage.
    • Keyword Activation: Spend Keyword Tokens up to your Focus value to add their effects to the activation.
    • Self-Targeted Activation: An activation that targets yourself. It is automatically successful, deals no damage, and grants no Karma reward.
    • Focus Break: Spend 5 of your Affinity Color tokens to permanently increase your Focus by 1.
    • Resonance Lock-In: Spend 2-3 identical tokens to gain that Keyword's permanent Passive Effect. (Note: .2 Talent keywords cost 2 tokens; all others cost 3).

    7.2 Respond Actions (Reactions)

    • Resist: Spend any Keyword Token to prevent 1 damage. You may also spend 2 Karma to prevent 1 damage.
    • Avoid: To Avoid, you must spend Keyword Tokens from your Matrix to exactly match the color of each token the active player spent for their activation. If the cost is paid, the entire instance of the activation is negated.
      • Your Focus does not limit the number of tokens you can spend to pay an Avoid cost.
      • "Spent" tokens are those that count toward the attacker's Focus limit. Tokens included via a .0 Flaw or .3 Quirk effect are not considered 'spent' and do not need to be matched.

    8.0 ADVANCED MECHANICS

    8.1 Combos & Special Activations

    These are specific Keyword Activations that can be performed even if their token cost exceeds your Focus.

    Chroma Burst

    • Requirement: Spend 3 or more tokens of the same color.

    • Effect: All of their effects resolve simultaneously.

    • Bonus: +1 Karma

      If this activation is successful, you gain 1 bonus Karma in addition to the standard reward.

    Resonance Cascade

    • Requirement: Spend exactly 3 tokens (one from each of your three Affinity Colors).

    • Effect: Their effects resolve as normal. This is primarily a play for resources.

    • Bonus: +2 Karma

      If this activation is successful, you gain a large bonus of 2 Karma.

    Critical Activation

    • Requirement: Spend 5 tokens of the same color.

    • Effect Replacement

      This activation does not use the keywords' normal Active Effects. Instead, its entire effect becomes the following:

      "Deal damage to the target's HP equal to your current Focus. The target also loses Karma equal to your current Focus."

    • Bonus: +3 Karma

      If this activation is successful, you gain a massive bonus of 3 Karma.

    8.2 Tags (Boosts & Statuses)

    • Boosts (self-targeted via .4 keywords) are always successfully applied.
    • Statuses (opponent-targeted via .5 keywords) are conditional on a successful activation.
    • Multiple instances of the same tag on a player stack and resolve independently.

    8.3 Entity Rules

    An entity is a temporary body on the board created by a keyword's effect.

    • They are considered "players" and are legal targets for any activation.
    • They are removed from play when their HP is reduced to 0.
    • Default Rule on Defeat: Unless an effect states otherwise (e.g., the [7.9] Split passive), when an entity is removed from play, any unspent Keyword Tokens on its micro-Matrix are discarded.
    Manifested Guardian (The Shield)
    • Created By: [5.9] Manifest
    • Creation Rules: When created, set the Guardian's starting HP equal to your current Focus.
    • Function: While the Guardian is active, opponents must target it instead of your Agent, unless an effect specifically allows it to be ignored (e.g., [7.7] Ranged).
    Split Entity (The Partner)
    • Created By: [7.9] Split
    • Creation Rules: When created, you sacrifice an amount of your HP and temporarily reduce your Focus. These amounts become the entity's starting HP and Focus. The other tokens spent on the activation form its starting micro-Matrix.
    • Function: During your Activate phase, you may perform one action with your Agent and one with your Split Entity, in the order of your choice.
    Token Entity (The Summon)
    • Created By: [4.9] Animate
    • Creation Rules: When created, set the entity's starting HP and Focus equal to your current Focus. The other tokens spent form its starting micro-Matrix.
    • Function: You temporarily set your Agent board aside and act as the Token Entity, using its HP, Focus, and micro-Matrix. Your Agent cannot be targeted or affected while you are the entity.

    9.0 GLOBAL RULES & HIERARCHY

    • 9.1 Keyword Application:
      • An activation cannot include more than one instance of the same named keyword.
      • A .0 Flaw cannot "carry" another .0 Flaw.

    • 9.2 Effect Hierarchy:

      The Golden Rule: 'Cannot' Trumps 'Can'

      If one game effect states that something can happen, and another effect states that it cannot, the effect that prohibits the action always takes precedence.

      • Copying is Not Stealing: Effects that "copy" a token or passive do not affect the original owner.
      • Unstoppable vs. Immunity: Effects that "cannot be Resisted or Avoided" refer only to Respond actions and do not bypass permanent passive immunities.

    10.0 KEYWORD CATEGORY TRAITS

    • .0 Flaw: Suffer a drawback to use another token's effect without spending it.
    • .1 Gift: Utility effects. Can be spent during the Ready phase for 1 Karma instead of their normal effect.
    • .2 Talent: Permanent upgrades. A Resonance Lock-In for a Talent costs 2 identical tokens (instead of the usual 3).
    • .3 Quirk: Free "rider" effects that can be added to an activation without costing Focus (limit 1 Quirk per activation).
    • .4 Boost: Applies a beneficial, guaranteed Boost Tag to yourself.
    • .5 Status: Applies a detrimental Status Tag to an opponent, but only on a successful activation.
    • .6 Form: Alters how an activation targets opponents.
    • .7 Modifier: Bends the core rules of an action or the game state.
    • .8 Drive: Directly increases numerical efficiency and interacts with damage.
    • .9 Unique: Game-altering "ultimate" abilities.

    11.0 COMPLETE KEYWORD CODEX (v10.4)

    • [0.0] Restricted (.Flaw):
      • Active: Choose a "carried" Keyword Token from one of your three Affinity Color slots; its Active Effect is applied, but the token is not spent.
      • Passive: Your max Karma is reduced to 5. Your free Keyword Token roll during the Ready phase is modified: you may choose to roll for any of your three Affinity Colors, instead of being restricted to only your Primary Color.
    • [0.1] Aura (.Gift):
      • Active: This activation deals +1 damage for each other Keyword Token spent from your Primary Affinity Color.
      • Passive: Any activation you perform that includes at least one Keyword Token of your Primary Affinity Color deals +1 damage.
    • [0.2] Specialist (.Talent):
      • Active: Deals +1 damage for each Keyword Token spent that shares a category with another token in this activation.
      • Passive: The first activation each turn that includes 2+ Keyword Tokens from the same category deals +1 damage.
    • [0.3] Alert (.Quirk):
      • Active: The target must pay 1 Karma as a cost to perform a Resist or Avoid action.
      • Passive: The first Keyword Token you spend during your Respond phase each turn is returned to your Matrix.
    • [0.4] Liberate (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Liberate Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Liberate tag instead.
      • Liberate Tag: Expend to prevent a Keyword Token from being discarded by an opponent's effect.
    • [0.5] Mark (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Mark Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Mark tag instead.
      • Mark Tag: The next time this player takes damage from any source, they take +1 damage. Remove this tag.
    • [0.6] Indirect (.Form):
      • Active: The target cannot Resist or Avoid unless they first discard a Keyword Token that matches your Primary Color.
      • Passive: You take 1 less damage from activations with a .6 Form keyword.
    • [0.7] Adaptive (.Modifier):
      • Active: During resolution, you may have this activation's damage become either: Target loses 2 Karma, or Target discards one Keyword Token.
      • Passive: Once per turn, you may spend a Keyword Token of any color as if it were one of your Affinity Colors.
    • [0.8] Gamble (.Drive):
      • Active: Roll two d10s (Color, Category). This activation gains the Active Effect of the resulting keyword.
      • Passive: Once per turn, you may reroll any single d10 you have rolled. You must accept the new result.
    • [0.9] Edit (.Unique):
      • Active: Swap a number of Keyword Tokens on your Matrix with an equal number on the target's Matrix, up to your Focus. This cannot be Resisted or Avoided.
      • Passive: Once per turn, when rolling the d10 for your Primary Color, you may choose the result instead.
    • [1.0] Bane (.Flaw):
      • Active: You suffer 1 Life Loss. Carry another token.
      • Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage to targets with higher HP than you.
    • [1.1] Traveller (.Gift):
      • Active: Choose another token in this activation; it is considered one of your Affinity Colors for combo requirements.
      • Passive: Your first non-Affinity die roll each turn costs 1 Karma instead of 2.
    • [1.2] Martial (.Talent):
      • Active: If the target's Resist reduces damage to 0, the activation is unsuccessful, but the target suffers 1 Life Loss anyway.
      • Passive: Your Karma Surges cost 1 Karma for +1 damage.
    • [1.3] Attuned (.Quirk):
      • Active: If successful and includes a Primary Color token, gain 1 bonus Karma.
      • Passive: Once per turn, when an opponent activates a keyword matching one of your Affinity Colors, you may gain 1 Karma.
    • [1.4] Augment (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Augment Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Augment tag instead.
      • Augment Tag: Expend when rolling a d10 for generation to roll two dice and choose one result.
    • [1.5] Impair (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Impair Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be an Impair tag instead.
      • Impair Tag: The next time this player spends Karma, the cost is increased by 1. Remove this tag.
    • [1.6] Reactive (.Form):
      • Active: If this activation is Avoided or fully Resisted, you may return one of the other spent tokens to your Matrix.
      • Passive: When you successfully Avoid or fully Resist an activation, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [1.7] Phasing (.Modifier):
      • Active: The target cannot perform a Resist action. They may still Avoid.
      • Passive: Opponents must spend one additional Keyword Token as part of the cost of any Resist action against you.
    • [1.8] Brutal (.Drive):
      • Active: Deals +1 damage. The target cannot gain Karma or tokens from keyword effects during this activation's resolution.
      • Passive: When you deal 3+ damage with a single activation, the target loses 1 Karma.
    • [1.9] Translocate (.Unique):
      • Active: Swap one of your other spent tokens with a token on the target's Matrix; the stolen token's effect is added to this activation.
      • Passive: At the end of your turn, you may swap one token on your Matrix with a token on an opponent's Matrix.
    • [2.0] Anxious (.Flaw):
      • Active: Discard a token from your Matrix. Roll a d10; this activation gains the effect of the keyword of the discarded token's Color and the rolled Category.
      • Passive: During your Ready phase, you may discard a token to reroll its category.
    • [2.1] Acrobatic (.Gift):
      • Active: If this activation is Avoided, choose a token on your Matrix. The target must discard a token sharing its Color or Category, or the Avoid fails.
      • Passive: The first time your activation is Avoided each turn, you may reroll the category of one token on your Matrix.
    • [2.2] Sentry (.Talent):
      • Active: If this activation is Avoided, the target suffers 1 Life Loss. This does not make the activation successful.
      • Passive: When you successfully Avoid or fully Resist an activation, the active player suffers 1 Life Loss.
    • [2.3] Eidetic (.Quirk):
      • Active: If successful, generate a copy of a token on the target's Matrix.
      • Passive: Once per turn, when generating a token, you may pay 1 extra Karma to generate a second, identical token.
    • [2.4] Haste (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Haste Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Haste tag instead.
      • Haste Tag: Expend at the start of your Ready phase to perform a second, additional Ready phase this turn.
    • [2.5] Slow (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Slow Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Slow tag instead.
      • Slow Tag: During your next Ready phase, your Focus is treated as 1 lower. Remove this tag at the end of that phase.
    • [2.6] Snap (.Form):
      • Active: The target cannot perform any Respond actions. This activation's damage is 1 and can only include one .5 Status.
      • Passive: You are immune to the active effect of Snap. When targeted by an activation that includes Snap, you may perform Respond actions normally.
    • [2.7] Condense (.Modifier):
      • Active: Deals bonus damage equal to (your Focus - tokens spent).
      • Passive: Focus Break costs 4 Affinity tokens instead of 5.
    • [2.8] Rapid (.Drive):
      • Active: This activation's damage is dealt as a sequence of 1-damage instances. Only the first successful instance can apply tags. (Note: Each instance of 1 damage in the sequence can be Resisted independently.)
      • Passive: Damage from your Basic Activations and Karma Surges is dealt as a sequence of 1-damage instances.
    • [2.9] Reckless (.Unique):
      • Active: You may suffer any amount of Life Loss; this activation deals +1 damage for each HP lost this way.
      • Passive: You can perform a Karma Surge on any Keyword Activation.
    • [3.0] Hesitant (.Flaw):
      • Active: This activation can only target yourself. Carry another token.
      • Passive: You cannot Avoid Basic Activations. You permanently reduce all damage from Keyword Activations by 1.
    • [3.1] Sturdy (.Gift):
      • Active: For this activation's duration, the opponent cannot use effects that would cause you to discard tokens or lose Karma.
      • Passive: Your Keyword Tokens cannot be discarded by an opponent's keyword effects.
    • [3.2] Brawler (.Talent):
      • Active: If successful, you may discard a token from your Matrix to discard a token from the target's Matrix.
      • Passive: If an opponent's effect would force you to discard a token, you may choose a token on their Matrix. They must discard that token or the activation fails.
    • [3.3] Stubborn (.Quirk):
      • Active: You may ignore the effects of all Status Tags on your board until the end of the turn.
      • Passive: Once per turn, when one of your Status Tags would trigger, you may choose to have it not trigger. If you do, remove that tag.
    • [3.4] Ward (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Ward Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Ward tag instead.
      • Ward Tag: Expend during your Respond phase to perform a special Resist that prevents all damage.
    • [3.5] Daze (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Daze Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Daze tag instead.
      • Daze Tag: You cannot spend more than one Keyword Token on any single activation. Remove this tag at the end of your next turn.
    • [3.6] Sculpt (.Form):
      • Active: Choose another .6 Form on your Matrix. Its Active Effect is applied to this activation, but the chosen token is not spent.
      • Passive: Your Focus limit is increased by +1 for activations that include a .6 Form keyword.
    • [3.7] Defensive (.Modifier):
      • Active: You are immune to any damage that would be dealt to you as a result of the target's keyword effects during this activation's resolution.
      • Passive: Your Resist actions are +1 more effective.
    • [3.8] Impact (.Drive):
      • Active: If this activation is successful, you may choose one Keyword Token on the target's Matrix and discard it.
      • Passive: When you perform a successful activation, you may spend 1 Karma to discard one Keyword Token from the target's Matrix.
    • [3.9] Shift (.Unique):
      • Active: Choose one of your active Passives. Add the Active Effect of that keyword to your current activation.
      • Passive: You may perform a Resonance Lock-In by spending one token of the correct color from three different categories, instead of three identical tokens.
    • [4.0] Feeble (.Flaw):
      • Active: This activation's damage is reduced by 1. Carry another token.
      • Passive: When you take 3+ damage from a single activation, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [4.1] Survivor (.Gift):
      • Active: If your HP is 5 or less, you gain 2 Karma.
      • Passive: If an effect would reduce your HP to 0, you may spend 1 Karma to set your HP to 1 instead.
    • [4.2] Stoic (.Talent):
      • Active: If an opponent's Respond phase effect would cause you to discard a token, gain 1 Karma.
      • Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect forces you to discard a token, you gain 1 Karma. The token is still discarded.
    • [4.3] Ageless (.Quirk):
      • Active: If this activation includes another keyword with a Karma cost, reduce that cost by 1.
      • Passive: The first time you would pay a Karma cost from a keyword's effect each turn, reduce that cost by 1.
    • [4.4] Bless (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Bless Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Bless tag instead.
      • Bless Tag: Expend when you would gain Karma to gain 1 additional Karma.
    • [4.5] Curse (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Curse Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Curse tag instead.
      • Curse Tag: The next time this player would gain Karma, the gain is reduced by 1. Remove this tag.
    • [4.6] Piercing (.Form):

      • Passive: Opponents must pay 1 Karma to be able to perform a Resist action against you.
    • [4.7] Flux (.Modifier):
      • Active: You may spend Karma up to your Focus; deal +1 damage for each Karma spent.
      • Passive: When you perform a successful activation, you may spend 1 Karma to deal +1 damage.
    • [4.8] Siphon (.Drive):
      • Active: If successful, gain Karma equal to damage dealt, up to your Focus.
      • Passive: When you deal damage with a Basic Activation, gain 1 Karma.
    • [4.9] Animate (.Unique):
      • Active: Create a Token Entity.
      • Passive: When you create a Token Entity, its Focus is increased by 1.
    • [5.0] Gremlins (.Flaw):
      • Active: The target gains 1 Karma. Carry another token.
      • Passive: When an opponent performs a Focus Break or Resonance Lock-In, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [5.1] Sensor (.Gift):
      • Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, this activation deals +1 damage.
      • Passive: When an opponent Resists or Avoids, you may spend 1 Karma to have the activation deal +1 damage.
    • [5.2] Improvisor (.Talent):

      • Passive: Your Orange keywords are considered to be of any color for activation requirements.
    • [5.3] Crafty (.Quirk):
      • Active: You may choose one non-Affinity token on your Matrix and reroll its category.
      • Passive: The first time you generate a non-Affinity token each turn, you may reroll it. Must keep the new result.
    • [5.4] Charge (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Charge Boost Tag.

      • Active: If successful, apply the Bind Status Tag.

    • [5.6] Imbue (.Form):
      • Active: If successful, you may choose a Tag on your board and apply a copy of it to the target.
      • Passive: When you successfully apply a Tag, you may roll a d10 of a non-Affinity color and generate the token.
    • [5.7] Material (.Modifier):

      • Passive: At the end of your turn, you may discard one token to gain 1 Karma.
    • [5.8] Leverage (.Drive):
      • Active: If you have one or more Boost Tags, this activation deals +1 damage.
      • Passive: The first time you would expend a Boost Tag each turn, you gain its effect, but the tag is not removed.
    • [5.9] Manifest (.Unique):
      • Active: Create a Manifested Guardian.
      • Passive: When you create an entity, its starting HP is increased by 1.
    • [6.0] Vulnerable (.Flaw):
      • Active: This activation is +1 easier for the target to Resist. Carry another token.
      • Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage. Activations targeting you deal +1 damage.
    • [6.1] Tolerant (.Gift):
      • Active: Ignore effects of Status Tags on the opponent for this activation's duration.
      • Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's keyword effect of your Primary Color would affect you, it is negated.
    • [6.2] Deadeye (.Talent):
      • Active: Ignores damage reduction from opponent's Passive abilities.
      • Passive: An opponent cannot spend more Keyword Tokens on a Resist action than your current Focus.
    • [6.3] Immunized (.Quirk):
      • Active: You may remove any Status Tags from your board that match your Primary Color.
      • Passive: You are immune to all Status Tags that match your Primary Color.
    • [6.4] Cure (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Cure Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Cure tag instead.

    • [6.5] Weaken (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Weaken Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Weaken tag instead.
      • Weaken Tag: The next time a Status Tag would be applied to you, gain a second copy of it. Remove this tag.
    • [6.6] Sticky (.Form):
      • Active: If successful, at the start of the target's next Ready phase, they suffer 1 Life Loss.
      • Passive: When a Status Tag you applied would be removed by its own effect, the opponent must pay 1 Karma or it is not removed.
    • [6.7] Restore (.Modifier):
      • Active: Can only target yourself or an ally. Instead of dealing damage, the target gains HP equal to the damage value.
      • Passive: When you resolve a Self-Targeted Activation, you gain an additional 1 HP.
    • [6.8] Exploit (.Drive):
      • Active: You may remove Status Tags from the target, up to your Focus; deal +1 damage for each tag removed.
      • Passive: Your activations deal +1 damage to targets with one or more Status Tags.
    • [6.9] Growth (.Unique):
      • Active: Deals bonus damage equal to your current HP, up to a max bonus equal to your Focus.
      • Passive: If your HP is higher than 5, your activations deal +1 damage.
    • [7.0] Limited (.Flaw):
      • Active: For this activation, any effect that uses your Focus as a metric treats it as 1. Carry another token.
      • Passive: At the end of your turn, if you have 3 or fewer tokens, you may generate a Primary Color token for free.
    • [7.1] Resilient (.Gift):
      • Active: Choose another token in this activation; if an opponent's effect would negate it, it resolves anyway.
      • Passive: The first time each turn your keyword effect is negated, gain 1 Karma.
    • [7.2] Font (.Talent):
      • Active: For this activation, you may pay Karma costs with HP, or HP costs with Karma.
      • Passive: Once per turn during your Ready phase, you may suffer 1 Life Loss to roll an additional Primary Color d10 for free.
    • [7.3] Insulated (.Quirk):
      • Active: If an opponent's Respond phase effect would cause you to lose Karma, gain that much Karma instead.
      • Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect would cause you to lose Karma, gain 1 Karma instead.
    • [7.4] Regen (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Regen Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be a Regen tag instead.
      • Regen Tag: At the end of your turn, expend this tag to either gain 1 HP or gain 1 Karma.
    • [7.5] Blight (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Blight Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Blight tag instead.
      • Blight Tag: The next time this player spends a Keyword Token, they suffer 1 Life Loss. Remove this tag. Only one Blight can trigger per action.
    • [7.6] Area (.Form):
      • Active: Targets all other opponents. If an opponent controls an entity, create an additional instance targeting it.
      • Passive: When you deal damage to an entity, its controller suffers 1 Life Loss.
    • [7.7] Ranged (.Modifier):
      • Active: This activation can target a player directly, ignoring Manifested Guardian. Token Entities can target Agents directly.
      • Passive: When you are a Token Entity, you may use your Agent's Focus instead of the entity's.
    • [7.8] Multiply (.Drive):
      • Active: This activation gains additional instances, targeting different opponents, up to your Focus.
      • Passive: When you apply a Boost Tag to yourself, you may spend 1 Karma to apply a copy to another player.
    • [7.9] Split (.Unique):
      • Active: Create a Split Entity.
      • Passive: When your entity is removed, you may reclaim any of its unspent tokens.
    • [8.0] Awkward (.Flaw):
      • Active: The target chooses the "carried" token from your Matrix.
      • Passive: During your Ready phase, you gain a free d10 roll, but an opponent chooses its Color.
    • [8.1] Charismatic (.Gift):
      • Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, you gain 1 Karma.
      • Passive: When you successfully apply a Status Tag, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [8.2] Leader (.Talent):
      • Active: When activating as your Agent or an entity, you may spend tokens from either your Matrix or the entity's micro-Matrix.
      • Passive: At the end of your turn, you may swap one token between your Matrix and an entity's.
    • [8.3] Insightful (.Quirk):
      • Active: If the target Resists or Avoids, you may generate a copy of one token they spent.
      • Passive: The first time each turn an opponent Resists or Avoids, you gain 1 Karma.
    • [8.4] Enhance (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Enhance Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Enhance tag instead.
      • Enhance Tag: Expend when declaring an activation to choose one spent token and increase its numerical effects by 1.
    • [8.5] Silence (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, apply the Silence Status Tag.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Silence tag instead.
      • Silence Tag: Your next Activate action must be a Basic Activation. Remove this tag at the end of your next turn.
    • [8.6] Splash (.Form):
      • Active: If successful against a primary target, create a secondary instance against all other opponents that deals 0 damage but applies all .5 Status keywords.
      • Passive: When a Status Tag would be removed from an opponent, you may pay 1 Karma to apply a copy to a different opponent.
    • [8.7] Lingering (.Modifier):
      • Active: Choose a .5 Status in this activation. If successful, apply a second, identical copy of that tag.
      • Passive: At the end of your turn, you may choose a Status Tag on an opponent and apply a second, identical copy.
    • [8.8] Spread (.Drive):
      • Active: If successful and the target has a Status Tag, you may apply a copy of that tag to a different opponent.
      • Passive: When a Status Tag is applied to an opponent, you may spend 1 Karma to apply a copy to a different opponent.
    • [8.9] Potent (.Unique):
      • Active: This activation applies its .5 Status Tags even if unsuccessful. It can still be Avoided.
      • Passive: When declaring an activation, you may spend 1 Karma and a .5 Status token from your Matrix to add its effect. This does not use Focus.
    • [9.0] Mundane (.Flaw):
      • Active: This activation cannot be a Combo Activation. Carry another token.
      • Passive: During your Ready phase, your Focus is treated as 1 lower for Karma gain. In exchange, the first time you spend a Keyword Token each turn, gain 1 Karma.
    • [9.1] Aware (.Gift):
      • Active: The target must suffer 1 Life Loss as a cost to perform an Avoid action.
      • Passive: When an opponent successfully Avoids, you may generate a copy of one token they spent.
    • [9.2] Stealthy (.Talent):
      • Active: The target must suffer 1 Life Loss as a cost to perform a Resist action.
      • Passive: The first time each turn an opponent's effect would discard your token, you may pay 1 Karma to return it to your Matrix.
    • [9.3] Trickster (.Quirk):
      • Active: After the target Responds, you may choose one other token in this activation to not resolve its effect. If you do, return it to your Matrix.
      • Passive: Choose a Color. Your tokens of that color can be spent as if they were Purple for activation requirements.
    • [9.4] Obscure (.Boost):
      • Active: Apply the Obscure Boost Tag.
      • Passive: Any Boost you apply can be an Obscure tag instead.
      • Obscure Tag: Expend during your Respond phase; it is treated as a Keyword Token of any color and category for paying Resist or Avoid costs.
    • [9.5] Nullify (.Status):
      • Active: If successful, you may choose one of the target's active Passive Effects. That Passive is removed, and the Keyword Tokens the target spent to perform that Resonance Lock-In are returned to their Matrix.
      • Passive: Any Status you apply can be a Nullify effect instead.
    • [9.6] Chain (.Form):
      • Active: If successful against a primary target, you may choose additional opponents up to your Focus. They do not get the original effects, but each suffers 1 Life Loss.
      • Passive: When you deal damage to 2+ different opponents during your Activate phase, gain 1 bonus Karma.
    • [9.7] Natural (.Modifier):
      • Active: The other Keyword Tokens in this activation cannot be affected by an opponent's keyword effects.
      • Passive: Your Passive Effects cannot be targeted, removed, or negated.
    • [9.8] Ambience (.Drive):
      • Active: Choose a token on any Matrix. Reroll it: discard it, then roll a d10. Generate a new token of the same category but with the new color.
      • Passive: When a keyword effect lets you roll for a token outside your Ready phase, you may choose the Color of the roll.
    • [9.9] Channel (.Unique):
      • Active: If successful, you may spend 3 additional tokens. If you do, choose one of the target's active Passives. Generate copies of the tokens the target spent to perform that Resonance Lock-In and place them on your Matrix. The target's Passive is unaffected.
      • Passive: When you perform a Resonance Lock-In or use Shift, you may generate a Primary Color token for free.
